Building a gym or fitness studio website, in detail

A gym website has one main job: get a nearby person to come in for a trial. Everything else supports that. For more detail, see websites for gyms, fitness studios and yoga studios.

Must-have features

  • Timetable. Classes and batches by day, editable by your staff, and readable on a phone without zooming.
  • Membership plans. Laid out side by side with what each includes. You decide whether prices are shown.
  • Trial pass form. Name, phone, preferred time and goal, sent to WhatsApp and email.
  • Trainer profiles. Name, certifications and specialities.
  • Facility photos. Equipment, changing rooms, parking. These answer the "is it worth visiting?" question.

Pages list

  1. Home: trial offer, facilities, timetable preview, location.
  2. Classes or programmes, each with a short description.
  3. Timetable.
  4. Membership plans.
  5. Trainers.
  6. Contact with map, timings and the trial form.

Integrations

  • WhatsApp for trial sign-ups and quick questions.
  • UPI and card payments for plans or class packs through Razorpay, if you want online payments.
  • Gym software booking widgets or member login links, embedded where available.
  • Google Maps and your Google Business Profile, so "gym near me" searches match the site.

Content checklist

  • Photos of the space and classes (with members' consent).
  • Timetable, plans and what each includes.
  • Trainer names, photos and certifications.
  • Address, timings, phone and WhatsApp number.

FAQ

Common questions

Can members pay fees online?

Yes. Memberships or class packs can be paid through Razorpay, which supports UPI, cards and netbanking. If you prefer to collect at the desk, the site just takes enquiries.

Can I update the timetable myself?

Yes. The timetable is managed from the dashboard, so a new batch or a holiday change takes a minute.

Can the site connect to my gym management software?

If your software offers a booking widget or member login link, I can embed or link it. The website does not replace attendance or billing software.

Should I put transformation photos on the site?

Only with written consent from the member, and with honest captions. I avoid promising results, and I do not add any testimonial or before-and-after that you have not supplied and approved.

I run a yoga or dance studio, not a gym. Does this fit?

Yes. The same structure works: batches and timings, a trial class form, teacher profiles and fees.

How long does it take?

Usually 1 to 2 weeks once I have photos, plans, timings and trainer details.
