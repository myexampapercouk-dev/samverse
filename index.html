About me

From the post-COVID digital rush to live websites for real businesses

When COVID pushed businesses online in 2020, I started building WordPress websites to help them get there. I've been doing it full time ever since, for doctors, industrial companies, creative agencies, food brands and community portals.

I care about the parts clients notice: a site that looks good on every phone, loads quickly, turns up on Google, and is simple for you to update later. I work directly with business owners, and I also take white-label work for agencies that need a WordPress developer they can depend on.

FAQ

Questions clients often ask

How long does it take to build a WordPress website?

A business website of 5–8 pages usually takes 1–2 weeks once I have your content. WooCommerce stores and larger sites take 2–4 weeks, depending on the number of products and features.

How much does a WordPress website cost?

It depends on the number of pages, design needs and features such as WooCommerce or bookings. Share your requirements on WhatsApp or through the form and I'll send you a clear, fixed quote within 24 hours.

Will my website work well on mobile phones?

Yes. Every site I build is fully responsive and tested on phones, tablets and desktops, because most visitors now come from mobile.

Do you make websites SEO-friendly?

Yes. I set up clean page structure, meta titles and descriptions, schema markup, XML sitemaps, Google Search Console and speed optimization so your site is ready to rank on Google.

Can I update the website myself after it's live?

Yes. WordPress with Elementor lets you edit text, images and pages without code, and I'll show you how. I also offer ongoing maintenance if you'd rather leave it to me.

Do you work with agencies and clients outside India?

Yes. I take white-label WordPress work for agencies and work with clients worldwide over WhatsApp, email and video calls.
