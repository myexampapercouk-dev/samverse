WordPress website development, step by step

The three stages are summarised above. This is the detail: what happens at each step of a WordPress website project with me, the choices that decide cost and timeline, and what you own at the end.

  1. Free review and a fixed quote. We talk through your goals, the pages you need, the features and two or three sites you like. You get a written scope, a fixed price and a timeline, usually within 24 hours.
  2. A page plan. Before any design, I map out the pages and what each one has to answer. Every main service gets its own page, because each one is a separate search; how to write service pages that rank and convert explains why.
  3. Design of the key pages. The homepage and other key pages are designed first and shared for your feedback, so the look is agreed before the full build.
  4. Build on staging. The site is built on a private staging link, with colours, fonts and spacing set as global styles so it stays consistent and easy to edit. Forms, WhatsApp, maps and any integrations go in at this stage.
  5. Content and SEO basics. Text and images go in, with titles, descriptions, headings and schema markup on every page, and a sitemap for the site.
  6. Testing. Every page is checked on real phones and desktops, forms are tested to make sure the emails arrive, and speed is tested on mobile.
  7. Launch and walkthrough. The site goes live, Search Console and Google Analytics are connected, a backup is taken, and I show you how to edit it on a video call.

How long it takes

Most business websites take a few weeks once content is ready. A small site of five to eight pages sits at the shorter end; a B2B site with a catalogue, or a directory, at the longer end. What decides it:

  • How ready your content and photos are, the most common cause of delay.
  • The number of unique page templates, more than the total number of pages.
  • Custom features such as an assessment tool, directory listings, bookings or a second language.
  • How quickly feedback comes back, and whether one person signs it off.

How long a WordPress website takes gives typical ranges by type of site.

FAQ

Common questions

What kinds of WordPress websites do you build?

Business and corporate websites, personal brand sites, B2B catalogues, directories and portals, blogs and magazines, and WooCommerce online stores.

Do you use templates or custom designs?

Either. I can adapt a quality theme for speed and budget, or build a fully custom design from Figma or your references.

Will I be able to update the website myself?

Yes. I build with Elementor or the block editor and show you how to edit pages, so everyday changes don't need a developer.

What do you need from me to start?

Your logo, the pages you need, content and photos (or a plan for them), and 2–3 websites you like. Then I send a fixed quote and timeline.

Are you a WordPress development company?

No. I'm a freelance WordPress developer based in Pune, building WordPress websites since 2020. You deal directly with the person building your site, and the price carries no agency overheads. If your project needs a large team working in parallel, an agency may be a better fit.

Can you build a multilingual WordPress website?

Yes. WordPress handles sites in English, Hindi and regional languages, with each language on its own pages so Google can show the right version. Our Temples, for example, includes Telugu content. Translations should be written or checked by a fluent speaker.

Will my new website be ready for Google at launch?

It launches with the foundations in place: titles and descriptions, a clean heading structure, schema markup, a sitemap and Search Console. Nobody can honestly guarantee rankings, but these give your pages the best start.

Can you rebuild my existing website on WordPress?

Yes. Whether it runs on an old WordPress theme, Wix or plain HTML, I rebuild it on a staging site, keep the content worth keeping, and set up redirects for any URLs that change, to protect the Google visibility you already have.
