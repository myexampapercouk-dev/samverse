Freelance WordPress developer for the UK: what I do

To be clear upfront: Samverse is not based in the UK. I work remotely from Pune, India, for UK clients. Everything below is work I do day to day.

WordPress development

New WordPress websites built on a lightweight theme or custom blocks, editable by you without a page-builder mess. Redesigns of existing sites, keeping your URLs and rankings intact.

WooCommerce development

WooCommerce stores with UK VAT, Stripe, PayPal and Royal Mail or courier shipping, plus fixes and upgrades on stores that already exist.

Shopify development

Shopify store setup, theme customisation and migrations from WooCommerce to Shopify for UK sellers who want less maintenance.

WordPress speed optimisation

Core Web Vitals work: image and script clean-up, caching, removing heavy plugins, and checking the checkout and cart pages, not only the homepage.

WordPress technical SEO

Redirects, sitemaps, schema, internal links and indexing fixes in Google Search Console, so the site is easy for Google to crawl.

WordPress troubleshooting and maintenance

Broken layouts after updates, plugin conflicts, white screens, malware clean-up and monthly care plans with updates and backups.

WordPress hosting advice

Help choosing and moving to UK or EU hosting that suits your traffic, with the account kept in your name.

Why hire a freelance WordPress developer instead of an agency?

You talk directly to the person building your site, decisions are faster, and you are not paying for account managers. The trade-off is capacity: one developer can take on fewer projects at once, so timelines are agreed before work starts.

UK store setup checklist

  • VAT: standard, reduced and zero rates, prices shown including VAT, and your VAT number on invoices.
  • Payments: Stripe or Shopify Payments for cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay; PayPal or Klarna if you want them.
  • Shipping: Royal Mail and courier rates by weight or zone, Click & Drop or a shipping app for labels.
  • GDPR and PECR: a cookie banner that blocks analytics until consent, a privacy policy, and opt-in boxes for marketing emails.

FAQ

Common questions

What time are you available for UK calls?

India is 4.5 hours ahead of the UK during British Summer Time and 5.5 hours ahead in winter. Calls are easiest between about 8am and 1pm UK time, which is my afternoon and evening. Messages sent later in your day are usually answered by your next morning.

Can you set up VAT on my WooCommerce or Shopify store?

Yes. I configure the standard, reduced and zero VAT rates you need, choose whether prices show including VAT, and set invoices to show your VAT number. Your accountant should confirm which rates apply to your products; I make the store follow them.

Do you build for UK restaurants and hospitality?

Yes. The Southern Flavours site in Solihull includes table reservations with instant confirmation, a weekend buffet section, menus and a WooCommerce shop for online ordering.

Do you work white-label for UK agencies?

Yes. I build from your Figma or Adobe XD designs, handle WordPress fixes and speed work, and stay in the background so your client relationship stays with you.

How do I pay a developer in India from the UK?

You are invoiced in GBP and can pay by international bank transfer or a common online payment method agreed at the start. Every invoice is itemised against the written scope, and larger projects are split into milestones.

Will I own the website, domain and store?

Yes. The domain, hosting, Shopify store, payment accounts and apps are registered in your business name and you pay those providers directly. I work inside your accounts as a collaborator or staff user, so you can remove my access at any time.

How much will my website cost?

It depends on the number of pages, features and whether you need a store. Send a short note through the contact page or WhatsApp with what you need, and you get a written scope and fixed quote, usually within 24 hours.
