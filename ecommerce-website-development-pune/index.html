WooCommerce or Shopify for a Pune business?

WooCommerce runs on WordPress on your own hosting. It suits stores that want low running costs, full control, lots of content (recipes, guides, B2B product details) and custom rules such as wholesale prices for shops in Market Yard. It needs updates and backups, which a maintenance plan covers.

Shopify is hosted, so security, hosting and updates are handled for you. It suits brands that run Instagram and Meta ads, want to change banners and launch collections themselves, and accept a monthly plan plus app fees. Many clothing, skincare and gifting brands start here.

Neither is better in general. The choice depends on your catalogue, budget and who will run the store day to day, and it is made before the quote. The longer comparison is in WooCommerce vs Shopify in India.

Payment gateways: Razorpay, PhonePe, UPI and COD

Indian buyers expect UPI first, then cards, net banking and wallets. Gateways such as Razorpay, PhonePe, PayU and Cashfree connect to both WooCommerce and Shopify, and the account is opened in your business name so payouts go straight to your bank. Each gateway is tested in test mode, including refunds and failed payments, then checked with one real low-value order before launch.

Cash on delivery is still common, so COD can be limited by order value or pin code, with an optional fee and a WhatsApp confirmation step to reduce refused orders. PayPal or a card gateway that accepts foreign cards can be added for NRI and export customers.

Domain and hosting add roughly ₹3,000 to ₹16,000 a year for WooCommerce, and gateways charge a fee per transaction.

Common questions

Are you an ecommerce website development company in Pune?

Samverse is a freelance ecommerce website developer based in Pune rather than a company or agency. You deal directly with the person who builds the store, on WhatsApp, calls or a meeting in Pune when it helps, with a fixed quote before work starts.

How much does ecommerce website development cost in Pune?

A WooCommerce store usually costs ₹30,000 to ₹90,000 or more, depending on products, design and features. Shopify setups are quoted on the same basis, plus the Shopify plan and app fees you pay directly. You get a fixed quote after a free review.

Should I choose WooCommerce or Shopify?

WooCommerce suits lower running costs, full control and content-heavy or custom stores. Shopify suits brands that want a hosted store and are fine with monthly plan and app costs. The recommendation is based on your store, and both are built here.

Which payment gateways can you set up?

Razorpay, PhonePe, PayU, Cashfree and others, plus UPI, cards, net banking, wallets and cash on delivery. PayPal or a card gateway for foreign cards can be added for international buyers. The gateway account is always in your business name.

Can you set up GST invoices and shipping?

Yes. GST tax classes, CGST/SGST or IGST by the buyer's state and invoices with your GSTIN and HSN codes are set up, along with shipping rates by weight or pin code and a shipping aggregator if you use one. Your CA should confirm the rates.

How long does an online store take?

Usually 2 to 6 weeks, depending on the number of products, how much is customised and how quickly product photos and details are ready. The timeline is written into the quote.

Can I manage products and orders myself?

Yes. You get a walkthrough on adding products, changing prices and stock, and processing orders, and both WooCommerce and Shopify have mobile apps for orders on the go.

Do you also build business websites without a store?

Will I own the store?

Yes. The domain, hosting or Shopify account and payment gateway are registered in your business name, and you get full admin access. My access can be removed whenever you choose.
