FAQs

Questions before you start

Is the sample homepage layout really free?

Yes. You get a sample layout of your homepage with the quote, with no obligation to go ahead.

WordPress or Shopify: which should I choose?

WordPress suits service businesses, clinics and content-led sites. Shopify suits stores that mainly sell products online. I will recommend one based on your needs.

How long does a website take?

A business website of 5–8 pages usually takes 1–2 weeks once content is ready. Online stores take 2–4 weeks depending on products and features.

How is the price decided?

By the number of pages, design needs and features such as online payments or bookings. You get one fixed quote before any work starts.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. You get access to edit text, images and products, and help is available if you need changes later.
