FAQs

Questions before you start

How quickly can you fix my site?

Many problems such as errors and broken layouts are fixed the same day. Hacked sites and larger speed work can take a little longer; you get a time estimate with the quote.

What do you need from me?

Your website address, a description or screenshot of the problem, and admin or hosting access once you approve the quote.

Will I lose any content?

A full backup is taken before any fix, so your content and settings can be restored if needed.

Can you stop my site being hacked again?

After cleanup I update WordPress, themes and plugins, remove unused ones, reset passwords and add security hardening to lower the risk.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance?

Yes. After the fix, monthly maintenance with updates, backups and monitoring is available if you want it.
