The live store stays untouched

How to choose a Shopify development company or developer

Whether you are shortlisting the best Shopify development company for your brand or a freelancer, the same checks apply:

  1. Open live stores they built. Check them on your phone: speed, product pages and checkout.
  2. Ask who does the work. Find out whether the person you talk to builds the store or hands it on.
  3. Get a written scope and fixed quote. It should list pages, apps, payments and shipping, and what counts as extra.
  4. Ask how theme edits are made. Work should happen on a duplicate theme, never on the live store.
  5. Check Indian payment and tax experience. Razorpay or another gateway, cash on delivery and GST invoices should be familiar.
  6. Ask which apps they will install, and why. Every app adds monthly cost and scripts; fewer is usually better.
  7. Confirm ownership. The Shopify account, domain and payment accounts must be in your name, with collaborator access only.
  8. Agree support after launch. Know who fixes things when an app update breaks a section.

Choosing a theme, and when to customise instead

Current Shopify themes are built on Online Store 2.0, where pages are made of sections and blocks you can move in the theme editor, and apps can add blocks without anyone touching code. The free themes made by Shopify are a sensible start for a small catalogue: light, maintained and easy for staff to edit. Paid themes from the Theme Store add layouts and features, but every feature ships code, so choose on what you will actually use rather than on the demo.

If you already have a theme, customising it is usually cheaper and safer than switching. Products, collections and URLs stay put, and your team already knows the editor. A new theme makes sense when the current one is an older vintage theme without sections on every page, when it no longer receives updates, or when the code has been patched so often that each change breaks something else. Bugs such as a variant picker showing the wrong price, a cart drawer that will not update or a mobile layout that jumps are fixed on the duplicate theme in the same way.

Apps and speed: keep the list short

Apps are how Shopify stores add reviews, filters, invoices, WhatsApp buttons and much more. Many are billed monthly and many load their own scripts on every page, so each one costs money and speed. Before adding an app, I check whether the theme or Shopify itself already does the job, and whether a small custom section would do it with less code.

  • Audit what is installed. Each app is listed with what it does, what it costs and which pages it loads on. Apps nobody remembers installing turn up regularly.
  • Remove leftovers. Uninstalling an app switches off its app blocks and embeds, but older apps sometimes wrote code straight into theme files, and that code stays behind until someone removes it.
  • Images sized for their slot. The Shopify CDN can resize images and serve modern formats to browsers that support them, but the theme still has to request sensible sizes, and the main image at the top of a page should not be lazy loaded.
  • Fewer fonts and widgets. One or two font families, a chat or WhatsApp button that loads only when tapped, and a single strong image where a slider adds nothing.

The admin includes a web performance report built from real visits at the time of writing, and Google PageSpeed Insights shows which scripts take the most time on a phone. How third-party scripts slow a website explains the measuring and trimming in more detail.

Payments, cash on delivery and GST in India

Payment providers

At the time of writing, Shopify Payments is not offered in India, so Indian stores connect a third-party provider such as Razorpay, PayU, Cashfree or PhonePe for UPI, cards, net banking and wallets. The account is opened in your business name, the provider runs its own KYC checks, and payouts go to your bank. Depending on your country and plan, Shopify may also charge its own transaction fee on orders paid through a third-party provider, so check the current pricing for India before comparing plans. Choosing a payment gateway in India lists what to compare between providers.

Cash on delivery

COD is a manual payment method in Shopify: the order comes in unpaid and is marked as paid once the courier remits the cash. Limiting COD by order value or pin code, adding a COD fee or confirming orders on WhatsApp usually needs an app, or a checkout app from a payment or logistics company. Those change how checkout behaves, so they are tested as carefully as the payment provider.

GST and invoices

Shopify can calculate GST, but its default Indian rates are only a starting point and need checking against your products. At the time of writing, Shopify does not produce a GST-compliant invoice by itself, so an invoice app adds your GSTIN, HSN codes and numbering. Your CA should confirm the rates, how in-state tax is shown and the invoice format before launch.

Shopify SEO basics

  • Collections as landing pages. A collection with a short, useful introduction, a clear title and a sensible handle can rank for category searches such as cotton kurtas for women. An empty product grid rarely does.
  • Product pages written for buyers. Your own descriptions rather than supplier text, real photos with alt text, and the details people ask about before ordering: size, material, delivery time and returns. See writing product descriptions that sell.
  • Duplicate product URLs. A product opened from a collection can load at /collections/name/products/item as well as /products/item. The canonical tag points to the short address, but some themes link to the long one from collection grids, so I make internal links use the short URL everywhere.
  • Titles, descriptions and handles are edited in the search engine listing area of each product, collection and page. When a handle changes, a redirect from the old URL goes in the same day.
  • Structured data. Most themes output product data for search engines. I check it with the Google testing tools and fill gaps such as a missing price, availability or brand.

Moving to or from Shopify

Migrations run in both directions. From WooCommerce, WordPress or Wix to Shopify, products, collections and customers are imported first, then historical orders where the old platform can export them, through CSV files or a migration app. From Shopify to WooCommerce, the same data moves the other way and the store is rebuilt on WordPress; see moving from Shopify to WooCommerce, and WooCommerce development for the build itself.

Either way, three things need the most care:

  • URLs. Shopify uses fixed paths: /products/, /collections/, /pages/ and /blogs/. Every old address that changes gets a 301 redirect to its closest new page, never a blanket redirect to the home page. 301 vs 302 redirects explains why.
  • Customer passwords. They cannot be carried between platforms, so customers either reset them or, on the newer Shopify customer accounts, sign in with a one-time code sent by email.
  • Timing. The switch happens in a quiet sales period, and the old store stays available until the new one has taken real orders.

Still weighing up the platforms? WooCommerce vs Shopify in India compares running costs, payments and control. Both have their place, and since I build on both, the advice is about your store rather than my preference.

Common questions

What do I get at handover?

A store in your own Shopify account with a completed launch checklist, a walkthrough of adding products and processing orders, and a note of every app installed and why. My staff or collaborator access can then be removed whenever you choose.

Should I choose Shopify or WooCommerce?

Shopify suits you if you want a hosted platform where Shopify looks after hosting, security and updates, and you accept the monthly plan and app costs. WooCommerce suits you if you want lower running costs, full control and room for content and custom features. I build on both, so the advice is about your store, not my preference.

Have you built Shopify stores before?

Yes. My most recent Shopify build is a store for a fashion brand that is launching soon: I set up and customised the theme, added the products and collections, configured payments, shipping and checkout, and set up the apps, speed and SEO basics. The client's details stay private until they are ready to share, and my WordPress and WooCommerce projects are in the case studies.

Can you customise my existing Shopify theme?

Yes. I work on a duplicate of your live theme, edit sections and templates in Liquid, CSS and JavaScript, and publish only after you have checked it, so the live store is never broken mid-edit.

Which payment options work on Shopify in India?

Indian gateways such as Razorpay, PayU, Cashfree and PhonePe connect to Shopify as payment providers, and Cash on Delivery can be enabled. Availability and fees change, so check the current options and charges for your account before choosing.

Can you move my store to Shopify, or from Shopify to WooCommerce?

Yes. Products, collections, images and customers are moved across, past orders where the platform allows exports, and every old URL gets a 301 redirect so search visibility is protected. Customer passwords cannot be moved, so customers set a new one.

Why is my Shopify store slow?

The usual causes are too many apps loading scripts on every page, a heavy theme, large images and third-party widgets. I audit what each app loads, remove what is not earning its place and lighten the theme.

Who owns the store?

You do. The Shopify account, domain and payment provider accounts are in your name, and you pay Shopify and app subscriptions directly. I work through staff or collaborator access, which you can remove at any time.

How much does a Shopify store cost?

It depends on the Shopify plan, any paid theme or apps, and the amount of setup and customisation. Share your requirements and you will get a free review and a fixed quote.

Do you work with Shopify Plus?

Yes, for theme work, sections, apps, speed and SEO, which work the same way on Plus as on other plans. Plus-only features such as checkout extensibility and Shopify Functions are scoped case by case after a free review, and you will be told plainly if a job needs a specialist Plus agency instead.

Can you build a custom Shopify app?

Most stores do not need one: a custom theme section, metafields or an existing app usually does the job for less. For a simple private integration, such as sending orders to a sheet or a courier, I review what you need and say honestly whether I am the right fit before you pay anything. Large public apps suit a dedicated app team better.

Do you offer monthly Shopify support?

Yes. Monthly support covers theme edits, new sections and banners, app checks and clean-up, speed checks and help when something breaks, through your collaborator access. The scope is agreed up front and you get a fixed quote after a free review.

Do you work with Shopify stores outside Pune?

Yes. Samverse is based in Pune and works remotely with stores across India, over WhatsApp, calls and screen sharing. If you are in Pune, we can meet in person when that helps.
