What you can outsource

Most WordPress work can be outsourced if it can be described in writing and tested on a staging site. Common requests are custom theme development from a Figma file, custom plugins for business logic that no off-the-shelf plugin covers, WooCommerce checkout, shipping and product changes, migrations between hosts, speed work and monthly maintenance.

Agencies often outsource the build stage only: they keep design, strategy and the client relationship, and hand over the development. That is covered on the white-label page for agencies, with no Samverse branding on the work.

Dedicated vs per-project outsourcing

Per-project outsourcing suits a defined job: one site, one plugin or one batch of fixes, with a fixed quote and a finish line. Dedicated outsourcing suits a steady flow of work, such as an agency with several WordPress builds a month or a business with a long feature list. It runs as a monthly retainer with an agreed number of hours or a scope agreed each month.

To be clear about what dedicated means: Samverse is one freelance developer, not a company with a bench of staff. A dedicated arrangement gives you one consistent developer who knows your setup, not a team that grows on demand. If you need several developers working in parallel, an agency is a better fit.

Time zones and communication

I work from Pune on India Standard Time. That overlaps with the UK morning and the Australian afternoon, and lets US teams send a brief at the end of their day and see progress the next morning. Communication is over email, WhatsApp, Slack or your project tool, with video calls booked when a conversation is quicker than a thread.

Replies normally come within the working day. Urgent issues such as a site that is down get looked at as soon as I see them, and the scope of any out-of-hours support is agreed in writing up front.

Pricing models

There are three models. Fixed price for a defined scope, which is what most projects use. Hourly for small, unpredictable tasks such as investigating a bug, with an agreed cap so the bill cannot run away. Monthly retainer for ongoing or dedicated work. Every quote is written for your project after reading the brief, so there are no package prices on this page.

Code quality, security and NDA

Code follows the WordPress coding standards, with input sanitised and output escaped, nonces on forms and capability checks in admin screens. Theme changes go in a child theme or a custom theme, never into a parent theme that an update will overwrite. Work is tracked in Git where your setup allows it.

I am happy to sign your NDA before seeing the brief. Logins should be shared through a password manager or a temporary account, and my access can be removed when the work is done. You own all the code written for you.

Who it is for, and who it is not for

It suits agencies that need a reliable WordPress developer for overflow work, businesses with an existing WordPress or WooCommerce site and a list of changes, and teams that want one person who knows their codebase. It is not the right fit if you need a large team working in parallel, round-the-clock staffed support, or work on platforms other than WordPress and Shopify. In those cases I will say so at the first call.

FAQ

Common questions

What does dedicated WordPress outsourcing mean here?

It means one consistent developer works on your projects every time, usually on a monthly retainer. Samverse is a single freelance developer, so you are not assigned a rotating team, and you are not buying a team that can scale to several people.

Can I outsource WordPress plugin development?

Yes. Custom plugins for integrations, custom post types, admin tools, WooCommerce extensions and custom blocks are common requests. Each one is scoped in writing, built to the WordPress coding standards and delivered with the source code.

How do you handle time zone differences?

Work runs on India Standard Time with overlap into UK and Australian hours. For US clients, briefs sent at the end of your day are usually worked on while you sleep, with an update waiting in the morning.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. I can sign your NDA before you share the brief, and for agency work nothing is published or credited without your permission.

How do I pay for outsourced work?

Projects usually start with an advance, with the balance before launch. Retainers are billed monthly in advance. International clients can pay in their own currency through common payment services.

Can I start with a small task to test the fit?

Yes, and it is a good idea. A small fix or a single page build shows how communication, code quality and delivery work before you commit to a larger project or a retainer.
