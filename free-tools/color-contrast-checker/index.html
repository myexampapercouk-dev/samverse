    FAQ

    Colour contrast questions

    What contrast ratio do I need?

    WCAG 2 level AA asks for at least 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text. Level AAA asks for 7:1 and 4.5:1.

    What counts as large text?

    Text of at least 18 point (about 24px), or 14 point (about 18.66px) when bold.

    Which colour formats can I enter?

    HEX like #7C3AED or #fff, rgb(124, 58, 237), and hsl(262, 83%, 58%).
