    FAQ

    Open Graph questions

    What image size should I use?

    1200x630 pixels works well for most platforms. Use a JPG or PNG under a few hundred KB and keep text away from the edges.

    Why does Facebook or WhatsApp still show the old preview?

    Platforms cache previews. Use the Facebook Sharing Debugger to scrape the URL again; WhatsApp usually updates after some time or with a new URL.

    Do Open Graph tags help SEO?

    They do not change rankings directly, but a clear preview can earn more clicks when your pages are shared.
