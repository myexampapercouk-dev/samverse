FAQ

Image resizer questions

What size should an Open Graph image be?

1200x630 pixels is the widely used size for Facebook, LinkedIn and WhatsApp link previews. Keep important text away from the edges.

What is the difference between crop and fit?

Crop to fill covers the whole frame and trims the edges that do not fit. Fit inside keeps the whole image and fills the empty space with a colour.

Why do my WooCommerce images look different sizes?

Products look uneven when the photos have different aspect ratios. Cropping every photo to the same square size, such as 800x800, keeps the shop grid neat.
