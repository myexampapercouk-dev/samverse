FAQ

Common questions

Do you work with Nagpur businesses?

Yes. I'm based in Pune and work with businesses across Maharashtra and India over WhatsApp, calls and video, with a staging link you can check at any time.

Can patients from nearby towns book appointments on our hospital website?

Yes. Each department or doctor page can have its own appointment form or WhatsApp button, and directions and contact details are shown clearly for patients travelling in.

Do you build both WordPress and Shopify websites for Nagpur businesses?

Yes. WordPress suits service businesses, clinics, manufacturers and content-led sites, and WooCommerce adds a store when you need one. Shopify suits product brands that want a hosted store with payments, shipping and apps ready to go. I help you choose based on what you sell and how you want to manage it, then build on that platform.

Can you work with my Nagpur business without meeting in person?

Yes. Projects run over WhatsApp, phone and video calls with screen sharing. You see progress on a private staging link from your own phone, and approve each step before launch.

How much will my website cost?

It depends on the number of pages, features and whether you need a store. The website cost calculator gives a quick estimate, or send a short note on WhatsApp for a fixed quote, usually within 24 hours.

Will I own the website, domain and store?

Yes. The domain, hosting, Shopify store and payment accounts are registered in your business name, and you get full admin access. I work inside your accounts as a collaborator, so you are never locked in.
