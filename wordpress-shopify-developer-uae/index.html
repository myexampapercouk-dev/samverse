FAQ

Common questions

Can you build an Arabic right-to-left website?

Yes. WordPress handles Arabic RTL natively, and I check the theme so menus, sliders, forms and icons mirror correctly. With WPML or Polylang each page has Arabic and English versions. On Shopify I use a theme that supports RTL and Shopify Markets for translations. You supply or approve the Arabic copy.

Can my store accept cash on delivery, Tabby and Tamara?

Yes. COD can be limited by emirate, city or order value, and Tabby and Tamara both offer plugins for WooCommerce and apps for Shopify. Each provider approves your business directly, and I install and test the checkout once your accounts are live.

Do you work with businesses in Saudi Arabia, Qatar and the rest of the GCC?

Yes. The same setup applies across the GCC, with the VAT rate, currency and shipping zones changed for each market. Saudi Arabia and Qatar are 2.5 hours behind India, so the working day still overlaps well. Keep in mind the UAE weekend is Saturday and Sunday, while Saudi Arabia and Qatar take Friday and Saturday.

How do I pay a developer in India from the UAE?

You are invoiced in AED and can pay by international bank transfer or a common online payment method agreed at the start. Every invoice is itemised against the written scope, and larger projects are split into milestones.

Will I own the website, domain and store?

Yes. The domain, hosting, Shopify store, payment accounts and apps are registered in your business name and you pay those providers directly. I work inside your accounts as a collaborator or staff user, so you can remove my access at any time.

How much will my website cost?

It depends on the number of pages, features and whether you need a store. Send a short note through the contact page or WhatsApp with what you need, and you get a written scope and fixed quote, usually within 24 hours.
