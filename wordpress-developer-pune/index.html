What Pune businesses usually need from a website

Pune is several markets in one city: clinics and coaching classes in Kothrud and Aundh, professional services around Baner and Hinjewadi, manufacturers in Chakan and Bhosari, and shops and restaurants everywhere. The details change, but four needs keep coming up.

A website that agrees with your Google Business Profile

Many local customers find you on Google Maps first and tap through to your website second. Your name, address, phone and hours should match exactly, and the website should answer what the profile cannot: what each service involves and how to get started. The Google Business Profile checklist covers the profile side.

A page for each service

One "Services" page with a list of bullet points rarely ranks for anything. A physiotherapy clinic offering sports injury rehab, back pain treatment and home visits needs a page for each, because those are three different searches. How to write service pages that rank and convert explains what goes on each one.

Enquiries on WhatsApp, not just forms

Plenty of customers would rather send a WhatsApp message than fill in a form. A click-to-chat button with a pre-filled message, a tap-to-call number and a short backup form cover everyone. Adding WhatsApp to your website covers the setup and how to track those clicks.

Speed on mobile data

Customers check your site on a phone, often on a patchy signal. Light pages, compressed images and fewer plugins matter more than heavy animation, and every build is tested on real phones. Website speed on Indian mobile networks explains why.

Choosing a developer in Pune: a quick checklist

Use this on everyone you speak to, including me. The full version, with questions for the first call, is in how to choose a WordPress developer or agency in Pune.

  • Open three of their live sites on your phone, on mobile data. Do they load quickly, and are the call and WhatsApp buttons easy to find?
  • Ask what they personally did on each site: design, build, content or only small fixes.
  • Ask who will actually build your website, and who you will talk to after launch.
  • Get a written scope listing pages, features, revision rounds and what is excluded.
  • Make sure the domain, hosting and Google accounts will be in your business name, with an administrator login for you.
  • Ask how they keep sites fast on mobile and which SEO basics are included at launch.
  • Ask what support looks like after launch.

Local SEO basics every Pune site should launch with

  • The same details everywhere. One name, address and phone number on your website, Business Profile, Justdial, Sulekha and industry listings.
  • Areas served, stated honestly. Name the parts of Pune and PCMC you really cover on your service and contact pages. Skip the copy-paste page for every locality: those are doorway pages, and local landing pages done right explains what to do instead.
  • A contact page that helps people arrive. Landmarks, an embedded map, parking notes and your weekly off, because Pune addresses often depend on lane numbers and society names.
  • Reviews you can ask for and show. A direct review link to send customers on WhatsApp, and a few genuine reviews on the matching service pages.
  • Tracking that shows what works. WhatsApp and call clicks recorded in analytics, and an optional "your area" field on the form.
  • The right language mix. English, Marathi or Hindi, depending on your customers. Multilingual sites are no problem in WordPress; Our Temples, for example, includes Telugu content.

FAQ

Common questions

Are you based in Pune?

Yes. I'm a freelance WordPress developer based in Pune, working with businesses in Pune, Pimpri-Chinchwad and Mumbai, and with clients across India and abroad online.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages, the features and whether you need an online store. The website cost calculator gives a quick estimate, or message me for a fixed quote within 24 hours.

How long does a website take?

Most business websites take a few weeks, depending on the number of pages and how quickly content is ready. You get a clear timeline with the quote.

Can you help my business show up on Google in Pune?

Yes. I build websites with local SEO foundations, service pages and structured data, and can help your Google Business Profile and website work together. Nobody can honestly guarantee rankings, but these foundations give you the best chance.

Do you work with businesses outside Pune?

Yes. Most of my work happens over WhatsApp, calls and video, so I work with clients across India and abroad, including a restaurant in the UK.

Can you build my website in Marathi or Hindi as well as English?

Yes. WordPress handles multilingual sites well, with each language on its own pages so Google can show the right version. Translations should be written or checked by a fluent speaker, and it usually makes sense to start with the home page, main services and contact page rather than translating everything.

Can you take over a website another developer built?

Yes. I start with a quick review of the setup, plugins and hosting, fix anything urgent, and then take care of changes and support. It helps to have the WordPress admin, hosting and domain logins ready.

Will I own my website, domain and hosting?

Yes. The domain and hosting should be registered in your business name, and you get an administrator login to WordPress. I can work inside your accounts or help you set them up in your name, so you are never locked in.

Can you set up my Google Business Profile?

I can help you create or improve it and link it properly to your website. The profile itself should be owned by your business Google account, and Google may ask you to complete verification yourself.
