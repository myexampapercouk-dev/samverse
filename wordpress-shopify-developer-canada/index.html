Hiring a WordPress or Shopify developer for a Canadian business

Canadian sites have two details that generic builds often get wrong: sales tax that changes by province, and a French version that needs to be as good as the English one. Both are part of the plan from the first call.

Sales tax by province

Depending on where your customer is, an order may carry GST alone, HST, or GST plus PST or QST. Shopify calculates these automatically once your registrations are entered; WooCommerce needs a rates table by province or a tax plugin. Either way I test checkout with addresses in several provinces before launch, and make sure your registration numbers show on invoices.

Bilingual English and French sites

  • A page for each language with its own URL, such as /fr/, so both versions can be found in search.
  • WPML or Polylang on WordPress, or Shopify Markets with a translation app.
  • Translated menus, forms, emails and checkout text, not just page content.
  • A switcher that keeps visitors on the same page in the other language.

Businesses in Quebec have specific French-language rules for commercial websites; I build the structure so French can be complete, and you confirm the copy with someone who knows those rules.

Shipping and cross-border sales

Canada Post and courier rates, plus separate zones, duties messaging and currency if you sell into the US. If the US is a large market for you, the USA page covers sales tax there.

Working across the time difference

India is 9.5 to 10.5 hours ahead of Eastern time and 12.5 to 13.5 hours ahead of Pacific. Feedback sent in your afternoon is usually done by your morning, and calls fit in your early morning. See Shopify development or WooCommerce development for more on each platform.

FAQ

Common questions

Can you build a bilingual English and French website?

Yes. On WordPress I use WPML or Polylang so each page has an English and a French version with its own URL, and the language switcher keeps visitors on the same page. On Shopify I use Shopify Markets with a translation app. You supply or approve the French copy; I do not machine-translate it and call it finished.

Can you set up GST/HST on my store?

Yes. Shopify and WooCommerce can both charge GST, HST, PST or QST based on the customer's province. I set the rates and tax display your accountant confirms, and make sure your registration numbers appear on invoices.

When can we have a call?

Calls work best in your early morning, roughly 7am to 10am Eastern, which is my evening. Vancouver clients usually prefer email, Loom videos and WhatsApp, with the occasional early call.

How do I pay a developer in India from the Canada?

You are invoiced in CAD and can pay by international bank transfer or a common online payment method agreed at the start. Every invoice is itemised against the written scope, and larger projects are split into milestones.

Will I own the website, domain and store?

Yes. The domain, hosting, Shopify store, payment accounts and apps are registered in your business name and you pay those providers directly. I work inside your accounts as a collaborator or staff user, so you can remove my access at any time.

How much will my website cost?

It depends on the number of pages, features and whether you need a store. Send a short note through the contact page or WhatsApp with what you need, and you get a written scope and fixed quote, usually within 24 hours.
