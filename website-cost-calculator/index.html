    FAQ

    About website costs

    Why do website prices vary so much?

    Prices depend on the number of unique page designs, features like payments or bookings, whether content is ready, and how much speed and SEO work is included. A clear brief gets you an accurate fixed quote.

    What yearly costs should I budget for?

    A domain (roughly ₹500–₹1,200 a year), hosting (roughly ₹2,500–₹15,000 a year) and any premium plugin licences. Maintenance plans are optional but recommended.

    Is the estimate a fixed price?

    No. It's a guide based on typical market ranges. Share your requirements and you'll get a fixed quote within 24 hours.

    Do I own the website after it's built?

    Yes. With WordPress you own your website, content and data, and you get full admin access.
