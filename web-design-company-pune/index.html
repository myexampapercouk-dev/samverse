Web design company in Pune or a freelance designer?

A web design company in Pune usually has a sales contact, a designer and a separate developer, which suits large projects with many stakeholders. For most small and mid-sized businesses the bigger risk is the design getting lost between the mock-up and the live site. Here one person plans the layout, designs it and builds it, so what you approve is what goes live.

If you need a team of designers for a large portal or round-the-clock support staff, an agency is the better fit, and you will hear that honestly on the first call.

How the design process works

1. Content first. Before any colours or fonts, the pages are listed with what each must answer: services, prices or a quote route, proof, location and contact. Your existing site, Google Business Profile reviews and the questions customers ask on the phone feed into this.

2. Wireframe on a phone. The home page and one service page are sketched for a phone screen first, because most Pune visitors arrive on mobile data from Google or Instagram.

3. Visual design. Your logo, colours and real photos are turned into a design for those two pages. If you prefer, the design is done in Figma for review; for many small sites it is quicker to review it directly on a staging link.

4. Build and review. The rest of the pages follow the approved design, on a staging copy you can check on your own phone before launch.

UI/UX choices that matter for local businesses

Good UI/UX for a local business is mostly about removing friction: a sticky call and WhatsApp button on mobile, the service area and timings visible without scrolling, one clear action per page, and forms short enough to fill with a thumb. Text is sized to be read without zooming, buttons are large enough to tap, and contrast is checked so the site works outdoors in sunlight.

Design also has to stay fast. Heavy sliders, autoplay video and large uncompressed photos are the usual reasons a nice-looking Pune site loads slowly on 4G, so images are resized and compressed, and animations are kept light. See speed optimization for what is checked.

Common questions

Are you a web design company in Pune?

Samverse is a freelance web design and development studio based in Pune rather than a company or agency. You deal directly with the person who designs and builds your site, with a fixed quote before work starts.

How much does website design cost in Pune?

A 5 to 8 page business website is about ₹15,000 to ₹40,000 to design and build, and a larger custom design about ₹40,000 to ₹1,00,000. You get a fixed quote after a free review of what you need.

Do you design in Figma first?

On request. Larger projects and teams with several reviewers usually prefer Figma. For smaller sites, reviewing the design on a staging link is often quicker. If your designer already made a Figma file, it can be built as it is.

Will the design work on mobile?

Yes. Layouts are designed for phones first and tested on real Android and iOS devices before launch, including buttons, forms and loading speed on mobile data.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. Content and pages that already rank are kept, redirects are set up where URLs change, and the new design is reviewed on staging before it replaces the old site.

How long does website design take?

Most business websites take two to five weeks from brief to launch, depending on the number of pages and how quickly content and photos are ready.

Will I own the design and the website?

Yes. The domain, hosting and website are in your business name, and you get the design files and an administrator login.
