WooCommerce Store Launch Checklist for Indian Stores: Settings, Test Orders and Launch-Day Steps

A WooCommerce launch checklist for Indian stores: store settings, products, payments, shipping, GST, emails, policies, test orders and launch-day steps.

Key takeaways
  • Start the slow tasks early: payment gateway KYC, courier accounts, GST rates from your CA and policy pages can hold up a launch more than the build itself.
  • Run test orders for every path, including failed and abandoned payments, COD, out-of-state GST, coupons and refunds, before switching the gateway to live keys.
  • On launch day, place and refund one real low-value order, delete test orders, open the store to search engines, then check orders against the gateway daily for two weeks.

Before a WooCommerce store goes live, confirm the core store settings, check every product has a price, weight, tax class and stock level, run test orders through every payment method (including failed and cancelled payments), check shipping charges, GST and order emails, publish your policies and take a full backup. On launch day, switch the payment gateway to live keys, place one real low-value order and refund it, delete the test orders and open the store to search engines.

The order matters as much as the list. This checklist follows the same test-and-launch stages as my WooCommerce development projects: test orders with the gateway in test mode, then live keys and a real low-value order to confirm the whole flow works. Payments, shipping, GST and security each have their own detailed guide, linked below, so this page stays a checklist.

1. Core store settings

  • WooCommerce > Settings > General: the store address should be the address you're GST-registered at, because WooCommerce treats it as your base for tax. Set selling and shipping locations to India unless you ship abroad, the currency to Indian rupee, and the number of decimals you want shown.
  • Settings > Products: set the weight unit to kg and dimensions to cm to match your courier. Under Inventory, switch on stock management, set the low-stock and out-of-stock notification email and threshold, and check the "Hold stock" time, after which unpaid pending orders are cancelled and stock released.
  • Settings > Accounts & Privacy: allow guest checkout, decide whether customers can create accounts, and set the privacy text shown at checkout.
  • Settings > Advanced: check the cart, checkout and My Account pages are assigned, and choose your terms and conditions page.
  • Site visibility: newer versions of WooCommerce have a "Coming soon" mode under Settings. Keep it on while you test, and note it for launch day.

2. Products and catalogue

  • Every product has a price, SKU, weight, dimensions, tax class, stock quantity, category and photos
  • Every variation has its own price and stock; a variation without a price can't be bought
  • Sale prices and scheduled offers start and end when they should
  • Sample or demo products from the theme are deleted
  • Categories, search and filters return what a shopper expects
  • Product pages answer price, delivery and returns questions near the Add to cart button; see product page optimisation

3. Payments

  • Gateway account in your business name, KYC approved and settlement bank account verified
  • Successful test payments by UPI, card and net banking, each leaving the order as Processing
  • Failed and cancelled payments leave the order as Failed or Pending payment, never Processing
  • Webhooks configured, so a paid order is marked paid even if the shopper closes the browser before returning to your site
  • A refund from the WooCommerce order screen reaches the gateway
  • Cash on delivery switched on or off deliberately, with any limits you need

Choosing and connecting the gateway is covered in accepting online payments on WordPress in India.

4. Shipping and COD

  • Shipping zones in the right order, with the charges you expect for your city, your state and the rest of India
  • Free-shipping threshold working, including when a coupon is applied
  • COD offered only where your courier accepts it, with any COD fee shown clearly
  • Unserviceable pin codes blocked at checkout

Zones, weight-based rates and courier integrations are explained in WooCommerce shipping setup for India.

5. GST and invoices

  • Tax classes and rates match your CA's figures, and prices display the way you chose, usually inclusive of GST
  • An order to your own state shows CGST and SGST; an order to another state shows IGST
  • Invoices carry your GSTIN, HSN codes, the tax split and a consecutive invoice number
  • Business buyers can enter their GSTIN at checkout, if you sell to businesses

Step-by-step settings are in WooCommerce GST setup for Indian stores.

6. Order emails

  • WooCommerce > Settings > Emails: the New order email goes to whoever packs and dispatches orders, and the From name and address use your business name and domain
  • Processing, Completed and Refunded emails branded and readable on a phone
  • Emails sent through SMTP or a transactional email service, so they reach inboxes rather than spam
  • WhatsApp or SMS order updates tested, if you use them

Branding and delivery are covered in customising WooCommerce order emails.

7. Policies and legal pages

  • Terms and conditions, privacy policy, refund and cancellation policy, and shipping and delivery policy, each linked from the footer and checkout
  • A contact page with your registered business name, address, phone and email
  • Any details India's consumer protection rules for e-commerce expect sellers to display; check the current rules with your CA or lawyer

Payment gateways commonly check these pages before approving a website. Writing the returns policy is covered in returns and refunds for WooCommerce. None of this is legal advice.

8. Security, speed and tracking

  • Strong passwords and two-factor authentication for administrators, Shop Manager accounts for staff, and test users deleted; the full list is in the WooCommerce security checklist
  • Daily off-site backups scheduled, because a store's orders change every day
  • Cart, checkout and My Account pages excluded from page caching, including any server or CDN cache, so shoppers never see stale totals or someone else's cart
  • Product, category, cart and checkout pages tested for speed on a phone
  • Unique titles and descriptions for categories and key products, and the sitemap submitted to Search Console
  • Analytics recording purchases once each, with the right value, from a test order

Launch day, in order

  1. Take a full backup.
  2. Switch the gateway to live keys, and check the live webhook too; many gateways keep test and live webhook settings separately.
  3. Place a real low-value order by UPI, check the money arrives in the gateway dashboard, then refund it from WooCommerce.
  4. Delete test orders and test customers, and reset stock levels the tests changed.
  5. Turn off Coming soon mode, and untick "Discourage search engines" under Settings > Reading.
  6. Clear every cache, then check the store as a logged-out visitor.
  7. Submit the sitemap in Search Console and tell your existing customers.

The first two weeks

  • Compare each day's orders with the gateway dashboard. A paid order stuck as Pending payment usually means a webhook problem.
  • Check WooCommerce > Status > Logs for payment gateway errors.
  • Make sure order emails are arriving, and ask early customers whether they received theirs.
  • Watch stock levels against what's actually on your shelves.
  • Turn repeated customer questions into answers on product pages or an FAQ.

FAQs

How do I take a WooCommerce store live?

Switch the payment gateway from test to live keys, turn off Coming soon mode, untick "Discourage search engines", clear caches, then place and refund one real low-value order to confirm payments, emails and stock all work.

How do I test WooCommerce checkout before launch?

Put the gateway in test mode and run successful, failed and cancelled payments, a COD order, an out-of-state order, a coupon order and a refund. After going live, confirm the real flow with one small genuine order.

Should I delete test orders before launch?

Yes. Test orders distort sales reports and stock levels and can confuse your accounts. Delete them, along with test customer accounts, after your final checks, and reset any stock they changed.

How long does it take to launch a WooCommerce store?

Usually 2–4 weeks once product details and photos are ready, depending on the number of products and features. Payment gateway approval runs on the provider's timeline, so apply early.

Which pages does an Indian online store need before launch?

Terms and conditions, a privacy policy, a refund and cancellation policy, a shipping policy and a contact page with your business details. Gateways often check for them, and your CA or lawyer should review the wording.

Can I launch with only a few products?

Yes. A small catalogue with complete, well-photographed product pages sells better than a large one full of gaps. Add products in batches once orders are flowing.
