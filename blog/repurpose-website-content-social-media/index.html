How to Repurpose Website Content for Social Media

Get more from each article or case study: turn website content into LinkedIn posts, Instagram carousels, WhatsApp updates, short videos and newsletters, and drive traffic back to your site.

Sameer Gupta 27 September 2026 · 3 min read

  2. Case studies work especially well
  3. Make it native to each platform
  4. Drive traffic back

Key takeaways
  • Turn each article into LinkedIn posts, carousels, short videos, WhatsApp updates and newsletters.
  • Make posts native to each platform with real value, not just links.
  • Link back to the specific article and track visits with UTM tags.

Writing one good article takes effort. Repurposing turns that effort into a week's worth of social content, and brings readers back to your website where they can enquire.

One article, many formats

  • LinkedIn post: the key insight plus three takeaways, with a link
  • Instagram or LinkedIn carousel: a checklist or step-by-step, one point per slide
  • Short video or reel: explain one tip in 30–60 seconds
  • WhatsApp update: a short summary to customers or groups who've opted in
  • Newsletter: a monthly round-up of new articles
  • Quote graphics: one strong line from the article

Case studies work especially well

Before-and-after visuals, the client's challenge and the result make engaging posts (with the client's permission).

Make it native to each platform

  • Don't just paste a link. Share real value in the post itself.
  • Use the platform's formats (carousels, short video)
  • End with a reason to visit the full article

Drive traffic back

  • Link to the specific article, not just your homepage
  • Use UTM tags to see which platforms send visitors
  • Make sure the article has a clear next step (enquiry, WhatsApp)

Keep a simple workflow

  1. Publish the article
  2. Create 3–5 social pieces from it
  3. Schedule them over the following week or two
  4. Reshare evergreen articles every few months

Plan it all with a content calendar, and use the article's cover image as a share image, as this site does for every article.
