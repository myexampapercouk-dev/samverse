Websites for Home Services: Plumbers, Electricians and AC Repair

What home service businesses (plumbers, electricians, AC and appliance repair) need on their websites to get more calls: service pages, areas served, pricing guidance, booking and reviews.

Sameer Gupta 27 September 2026 · 3 min read

Key takeaways
  • Make calling instant with tap-to-call, WhatsApp, hours and emergency info.
  • Create a page per service, list areas served and give pricing guidance.
  • Reviews, photos of work and a strong Google Business Profile win local searches.

When a pipe bursts or the AC stops working, people search on their phones and call the first trustworthy business that shows up. For home service businesses, the website's job is simple: be found, look reliable and make calling effortless.

Make contact instant

  • A tap-to-call button always visible on mobile
  • WhatsApp for sending photos of the problem
  • Working hours, and whether you offer emergency or same-day service
  • A short booking form for non-urgent jobs

Service pages

One page per service: AC repair, AC installation, water purifier service, plumbing repairs, electrical wiring, appliance repair and so on. Explain common problems you fix, what a visit includes, and typical timings.

Device repairs work the same way; see websites for mobile and laptop repair shops.

Areas served

List the areas and localities you cover. Create separate location pages only where you have genuine local detail; see local pages without doorway pages.

Working from home or a van rather than a shop? See SEO for service-area businesses for the right Business Profile settings and when area pages are worth building.

Pricing guidance

Visiting charges and "starting from" prices for common jobs reduce price-shopping calls and build trust. Be clear about what's extra (parts, gas refill).

Pest control businesses face similar urgency; see websites for pest control companies.

Trust signals

  • Genuine Google reviews and photos of completed work
  • Technician verification and training
  • Service warranty on repairs
  • Brands serviced

Brand names need extra care for water purifier businesses, where fake helpline numbers are common; see websites for water purifier and RO service businesses.

If AC repair, installation and maintenance contracts are your main business, see the more detailed guide to websites for AC repair services.

Local SEO is everything

  • A complete Google Business Profile with service areas, hours and photos
  • Steady reviews after each job; see how to get more Google reviews
  • Target "{service} near me" and "{service} in {area}"

Running a plumbing business on your own or with a small team? See websites for plumbers for emergency call buttons, explaining visit charges and before-and-after job photos.

Seasonal campaigns

AC servicing before summer, geyser repairs before winter: dedicated landing pages for seasonal offers make ads far more effective.

A fast, mobile-first site with one-tap calling is one of the best investments a home service business can make; see WordPress website development.
