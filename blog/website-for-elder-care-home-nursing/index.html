Website for Elder Care, Home Nursing and Assisted Living Services

How elder care homes, home nursing and caregiver services can build a website families trust: services, staff and safety, care process, pricing, virtual tours, enquiries and privacy.

Sameer Gupta 27 September 2026 · 3 min read

Key takeaways
  • Families want services, trained and verified staff, emergency procedures and pricing.
  • Use real photos and family testimonials, and get consent before showing residents.
  • Offer phone, WhatsApp and video calls, accessible design and local SEO.

Choosing care for a parent is an emotional decision. Often it's adult children, sometimes living in another city or abroad, who research options online. Your website needs to reassure them that their loved one will be safe, respected and well looked after.

What families want to know

  • What care do you provide, and for which conditions?
  • Who are the caregivers and nurses? Are they trained and verified?
  • How are medical emergencies handled?
  • What does it cost?
  • Can we visit, or get regular updates?

Essential pages

  • Services: home nursing, attendants, physiotherapy, post-hospital care, dementia care, assisted living, each explained clearly
  • Our team: training, background verification and supervision
  • Safety and medical support: doctor visits, hospital tie-ups and emergency procedures
  • How it works: assessment, care plan, start of care, reviews
  • Pricing: ranges or packages and what's included
  • Facility tour (for residential care): photos and video of rooms, dining and common areas
  • FAQs and contact

Build trust

  • Real photos of your facility and team, never stock images of smiling seniors
  • Testimonials from families, with permission
  • Registrations and certifications you hold
  • Clear information about how families receive updates

Respect residents' privacy

Only use photos of residents with their informed consent (or that of their legal guardian). Never share health details on the website or social media.

Make contacting you easy

  • A prominent phone number and WhatsApp, as families often need urgent help, for example after a hospital discharge
  • A simple assessment request form
  • Video call options for families living far away

Design for all ages

Some visitors will be older themselves. Use large text, strong contrast and simple navigation; see making websites easier for older users.

Local SEO

Families search for things like "home nursing in {area}" or "old age home near me". Create pages for your main services and areas, and keep your Google Business Profile complete with photos and reviews; see Google Business Profile checklist.
