Websites for Bakeries and Cake Shops: Custom Cake Orders, Delivery Dates and Slots, Eggless and Allergen Details, WhatsApp Orders and Festival Demand

Plan a bakery website that takes cake orders: custom cake forms, delivery dates and slots, eggless and allergen labels, pin code checks and WhatsApp.

Key takeaways
  • Separate ready-to-order cakes, which can be bought straight from the menu, from custom cakes, which start with a detailed form, a quote and an advance payment.
  • Make the delivery date, time slot, pin code and daily capacity part of the order itself, so the website never accepts a cake your kitchen cannot bake or deliver on time.
  • Label eggless options, allergens and FSSAI details clearly, show real photos of your own cakes, and plan festival pre-orders before the rush starts.

A bakery website earns its keep by taking cake orders that would otherwise come in as phone calls and long WhatsApp chats. Customers need to see what you bake with real photos and prices, pick a weight, flavour, message and delivery date, know straight away whether you deliver to their area, and trust that the cake will arrive on time for the occasion. Custom and theme cakes need a different route: a detailed request form, a quote and an advance payment.

Online ordering with a date, a time slot and payment is where WooCommerce development comes in: a product catalogue, Indian payment gateways with UPI, delivery areas and charges, coupons and order notifications, all on a site you own. If you also run a cafe counter or want a menu-first site with table bookings, my websites for restaurants, cafes and bakeries cover menus, photos, Google Maps and local search. This guide is about what is specific to cakes and bakes.

Four kinds of bakery order, four different flows

Most bakeries handle several types of order, and forcing them all through one form causes mistakes. Decide how each one should work:

Order typeExamplesBest way to take it onlinePayment
Ready-to-order cakesYour standard designs in set weights and flavoursAdd to cart with weight, flavour, message and delivery slotFull payment online, or COD if you allow it
Custom and theme cakesKids' themes, tiered wedding cakes, photo cakes, logo cakesA request form with a reference image, followed by your quoteAn advance through a UPI or payment link, the balance before delivery or at pickup
Daily bakesBreads, pastries, cookies, puffsA menu with prices for pickup, or a small delivery order with a minimum valueOnline or at the counter
Bulk and corporateCupcake boxes, office celebrations, festive hampersAn enquiry form with quantity, date, branding and budgetQuote, then invoice or advance

The website then has two clear buttons on every cake page: "Order this cake" for ready designs and "Request a custom cake" for everything else.

The custom cake request form, field by field

A good custom cake form saves several rounds of messages. Ask for what your baker needs to quote, and nothing more:

  • Occasion and date, with the date picker blocking days you are already full or closed
  • Delivery or pickup, with the time slot and, for delivery, the pin code
  • Size: weight or number of guests, and tiers for larger cakes
  • Flavour and filling, from your actual list rather than a free-text box
  • Egg or eggless, plus any allergies the baker must know about
  • Theme, colours and the message on the cake, with a character limit for the message
  • Reference images, uploaded from the phone gallery or an Instagram screenshot
  • Budget range, so you can suggest a design that fits it
  • Name and WhatsApp number for the quote

State your minimum notice for custom designs right above the form, so nobody requests a three-tier cake for tomorrow morning. Once the request arrives, a simple routine keeps it on track:

  1. Reply on WhatsApp with the design, price and what the advance covers
  2. Send a UPI or payment link for the advance, and confirm the order only once it is paid
  3. Send a reminder the day before with the delivery slot or pickup time
  4. Share a photo of the finished cake before dispatch, if you offer that

Weights, flavours and add-ons in WooCommerce

Ready-to-order cakes work well as variable products: weight as one attribute and flavour as another, each combination with its own price. Options that do not change stock are usually better as product add-ons:

  • Eggless, with any price difference shown before checkout
  • Message on cake, a short text field with a character limit
  • Photo print, with an image upload for photo cakes
  • Extras: candles, a knife, a greeting card, balloons or flowers, each with a price

Show a rough "serves" guide next to each weight, agreed with your kitchen, because most customers think in guests rather than kilograms. Keep the menu manageable: a dozen strong designs with clear photos sell better than a hundred near-identical ones.

Delivery dates, time slots and daily capacity

Cakes are bought for a fixed moment, so the date and slot matter more than in most online shops. A delivery date and slot plugin, or a small custom feature, can handle the rules that keep you out of trouble:

  • Slots such as morning, afternoon and evening, with a limit on orders per slot so your riders can cope
  • A daily order limit for each type of cake, after which that date closes on the website
  • A same-day cut-off time, after which the earliest date moves to tomorrow
  • Blocked dates for holidays, staff leave and days already full of custom orders
  • Midnight delivery, popular for birthdays, only if you genuinely run it, with its own fee and limited slots
  • Pickup with a ready time, so walk-in customers are not left waiting

Check the delivery area before customers choose a cake, not at the end of checkout. A pin code delivery checker shows whether you deliver to them, the fee and the earliest date. Fresh cream cakes rarely travel well by courier, so keep them to local delivery and pickup, and offer dry cakes, cookies, brownies and rusks for shipping further if they hold up in transit.

Eggless, allergens and food safety details

Egg is the first question many Indian customers ask, so make the answer impossible to miss:

  • Eggless labels on product cards, product pages and the cart, not only in the description
  • A filter for eggless items, so vegetarian customers can browse only what they can order
  • Separate preparation: say how you keep eggless bakes separate only if you actually do, and if they share equipment, say that too
  • Allergens such as nuts, gluten, dairy and soy, listed by your kitchen for each item, with "may contain" notes where cross-contact is possible
  • Careful health claims: words like "sugar-free" or "healthy" should match the actual recipe

Every food business in India, home bakeries included, generally needs an FSSAI registration or licence, and the number is expected to be visible to customers who order online as well as at the counter. A line in the footer and a mention on the order pages cover it. Packaged items such as cookies, rusks or gift boxes may also have labelling requirements, including veg or non-veg marks, so ask your FSSAI consultant how the current rules apply to what you sell.

A gallery that sells your custom work

For custom cakes, the gallery is your portfolio. Organise it so customers can find ideas for their own occasion:

  • By occasion: birthdays, kids' themes, anniversaries, weddings, baby showers and corporate events
  • Details with each photo: flavour, weight or tiers and the theme, so customers can ask for "something like this"
  • An "order something similar" button that opens the custom form with that photo already attached
  • Only your own work, photographed in good daylight; using other bakers' photos misleads customers and is easily spotted

Cartoon characters and brand logos may be protected, so think carefully before featuring them in your advertising. Compress gallery images so the page stays quick on mobile; preparing photos for your website covers sizes and formats.

Festival and seasonal demand

Bakery demand is uneven: birthdays and anniversaries keep coming all year, but Christmas, New Year, Valentine's Day, Mother's Day, Raksha Bandhan and Diwali bring sharp peaks. Plan each peak on the website:

  • Pre-order pages for plum cakes, festive hampers and gift boxes, opened well before the date
  • Capacity limits that close dates automatically when you are full, instead of staff cancelling orders later
  • A last order date shown clearly on the festival page and the homepage
  • One permanent page per festival, updated every year rather than a new page each time

Seasonal and festival campaigns covers timelines, offers and tracking in more detail.

WhatsApp orders that do not get lost

Many bakeries will always take some orders on WhatsApp. The website can make those orders tidier:

  • An "Order on WhatsApp" button that sends the cake name, weight, flavour, date and slot as a pre-filled message
  • One business number for orders, so requests do not end up on personal phones
  • Saved replies for confirmations, payment links and delivery updates
  • New website orders sent to the kitchen phone by WhatsApp, SMS or email, so nobody has to keep checking the dashboard; see order notifications by email, SMS and WhatsApp

If you also run a delivery-only kitchen alongside the bakery, websites for cloud kitchens covers direct ordering and delivery apps.

Getting found by local customers

People search with the occasion, the dietary need and the area together, such as "eggless cake delivery in Wakad" or "custom birthday cake in Baner". Work those phrases into page titles and headings where they fit, create separate birthday, wedding and kids' cake pages with real examples, and add Bakery structured data with your hours and address. A complete Google Business Profile with fresh photos, accurate hours and the website link often brings more orders than any other channel, and reviews from real customers you ask help both.

A test run before you go live

  1. Order a ready cake for each delivery slot, and one for pickup
  2. Try a date that should be blocked, and a same-day order after the cut-off
  3. Enter a pin code outside your delivery area
  4. Choose eggless, add a message at the character limit and upload a photo
  5. Submit a custom cake request with reference images and check where it arrives
  6. Complete a payment, fail one on purpose, and check the emails and kitchen notification for each

FAQs

Can customers choose a delivery date and time slot on my bakery website?

Yes. A delivery date and slot plugin, or a small custom feature, adds a date picker and slots at checkout, with cut-off times, blocked dates and a limit on orders per slot.

Can the website stop taking orders when a day is full?

Yes. A daily or per-slot order limit closes that date automatically, so customers pick another day instead of you cancelling orders later.

Should I take full payment or an advance for custom cakes?

Many bakeries confirm custom cakes only after an advance, with the balance paid before delivery or at pickup. Ready-to-order cakes are simpler to take in full at checkout.

Should my FSSAI number appear on the bakery website?

Yes, if you take orders online. Show it in the footer and wherever customers place orders, and ask your FSSAI consultant which current rules apply to your registration or licence.

Can I deliver cakes across India by courier?

Fresh cream cakes are best kept to local delivery and pickup. Dry cakes, cookies and similar bakes can often be shipped further if they are packed well and stay fresh for the journey.
