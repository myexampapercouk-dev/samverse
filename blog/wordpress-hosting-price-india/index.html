WordPress hosting price in India, for a typical small business website, is roughly ₹2,500 to ₹15,000 a year across shared and managed plans, according to the WordPress website cost guide on this site. VPS and cloud plans vary too widely to give a fair range. The headline monthly price you see in adverts is usually an introductory rate for a long upfront term; what you actually pay depends on renewal pricing, GST and add-ons.

Hosts change plans and offers often, so check current prices on each host's own site before deciding. If you are already facing a steep renewal or a slow site, WordPress migration covers how I move sites without losing email or rankings.

Renewal rateThe regular price the same plan renews at, often shown in the comparison table or terms
GSTWhether prices are shown before or after GST
Add-onsBackups, email, extra sites, security, CDN, control panel licence on a VPS

The figure that matters is the total over three years, including at least one renewal. Why hosting renewals cost more explains your options when the renewal invoice arrives.

Intro price vs renewal price

The low advertised price usually:

  • Applies only to the first term, after which the plan goes back to its standard price, sometimes much higher
  • Needs a long term paid upfront
  • Comes with a "free" domain that is free for the first year only; after that the cost guide gives roughly ₹500 to ₹1,200 a year for a .com or .in

Longer upfront terms can still be good value if you are sure about the host. Just do the arithmetic for years two and three before you commit, and set a reminder before renewal so you can compare calmly.

VPSDedicated resources on a shared machine; on unmanaged plans you or a developer look after itVaries with resources and managementBusier sites, several sites, custom server needs
CloudResources on a network of servers, fixed or usage-based billingVaries widely, can grow with usageSites with spiky or growing traffic and someone to manage it

What drives the price within a type

  • CPU and RAM: how many visitors and admin users the site can handle at once
  • Storage type and size: NVMe or SSD, plus any limit on number of files (inodes)
  • Number of websites allowed on the plan
  • PHP workers or entry processes: the limit on simultaneous uncached page loads, which matters for WooCommerce carts and checkout
  • Server location: servers in or near India help if most visitors are here
  • Support level: ticket only, or chat with WordPress knowledge

"Unlimited" plans still have fair-use limits. A WooCommerce store or busy site needs more than a five-page brochure site, and a plan that is too small moves the cost into lost enquiries. How to choose WordPress hosting in India goes deeper.

GST and billing currency

Indian hosting prices are often shown before GST, which is added at checkout, so make sure every figure you compare either includes or excludes it. If your business is GST-registered, enter your GSTIN at checkout and ask your CA about input tax credit. Some hosts bill in US dollars; your card may add conversion charges and the tax treatment can differ, so check a sample invoice.

Compare plans fairly: a three-year worksheet

  1. Decide the hosting type your site needs, and compare only plans of that type.
  2. Write down the price for the term you will buy, the renewal price, and whether each includes GST.
  3. Add the extras you need: backups, email, panel licence, CDN, security.
  4. Total three years, including at least one renewal.
  5. Compare resources, server location and support, not just the total.
  6. Read the refund period and upgrade or downgrade terms.

When to move host

Price alone is rarely a good enough reason; a move takes time and carries some risk. It usually makes sense when:

  • The site has outgrown the plan: frequent resource-limit warnings, slow admin, timeouts during sales
  • Support cannot or will not fix recurring problems
  • Renewal pricing makes a better-fitting plan elsewhere sensible over three years
  • Your audience is in India and the server is far away, with real speed impact
  • The host lacks something you need, such as staging, SSH or proper backups

Before moving, rule out a slow theme or plugins as the cause, since a new host will not fix those; fixing a slow Hostinger site shows the checks. If you do move, plan the order: full backup, copy files and database, test on the new server, switch DNS, then check email, forms and SSL. Ongoing checks after the move fit into WordPress maintenance.

WordPress hosting price in India: matching the plan to your site

Most overspending and underspending on hosting comes from choosing a plan before knowing what the site needs. A rough guide:

SiteUsually enoughSigns you need more
Brochure site, a few pages, contact formA good shared planSlow admin, resource-limit emails
Service business with blog and landing pagesHigher shared or managed WordPressSlow uncached pages, staging needed
Small WooCommerce storeManaged WordPress or a higher plan with more PHP workersSlow cart and checkout, timeouts during sales
Several sites or a busy storeVPS or cloud with someone managing itShared plan limits reached repeatedly

A site that is slow because of a heavy theme or too many plugins will stay slow on a bigger plan. Check that first; WordPress speed optimisation covers what can be fixed on the site itself.

Questions to ask a host before you pay

  • What is the renewal price of this exact plan, and does it include GST?
  • How often are backups taken, how long are they kept, and is a restore free?
  • Where is the server, and can I choose an Indian or nearby location?
  • What are the CPU, RAM and process limits behind "unlimited"?
  • Does support help with WordPress issues, or only the server?

FAQs

What is the WordPress hosting price in India for a small business?

The cost guide on this site gives a range of about ₹2,500 to ₹15,000 a year across shared and managed plans. Check current prices, including renewal and GST, on each host's site.

How much does WordPress hosting cost after the first year?

Usually more, because introductory rates apply only to the first term. Find the renewal price for the same plan before buying.

Is managed WordPress hosting worth the extra cost?

For business sites without someone technical in-house, often yes, because backups, staging and WordPress-aware support save time. For a small brochure site, a good shared plan can be enough.

Do I need a VPS for WooCommerce?

Not always. Many small stores run well on a good managed or higher shared plan. Move up when resource limits start affecting checkout.

Will changing host affect my Google rankings?

A careful move with the same URLs normally does not. Downtime, broken links or a slower server can, which is why the move needs testing.

Is cheap WordPress hosting in India good enough for a business site?

For a small brochure site with modest traffic, an entry shared plan from a reputable host is often enough. Check the renewal price, backups, support response and server location before you buy, and move up a tier when the site slows down under real traffic or you add a store.
