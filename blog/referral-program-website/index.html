How to Set Up a Customer Referral Programme on Your Website

How to set up a referral program on your website: rewards, referral links, WooCommerce and Shopify options, tracking, fraud checks and clear terms.

Key takeaways
  • A referral programme works when the reward is simple, sharing is easy on WhatsApp and both the referrer and the new customer get something.
  • WooCommerce and Shopify both support referral links or codes through plugins or apps; service businesses can run it with a form and a tracking sheet.
  • Write clear terms, check for self-referrals and fake accounts, and track referred sales so you know whether the programme is worth running.

A referral program on your website turns happy customers into a source of new ones. The basics are simple: give each customer a referral link or code, reward them when a friend buys or books, give the friend a welcome benefit, and make sharing easy on WhatsApp. The details (what reward, how to track it and how to stop abuse) decide whether it works.

This guide covers how to plan a referral programme, set it up on WooCommerce, Shopify or a service website, and measure whether it is worth running.

How a referral program website works

  1. An existing customer gets a unique referral link or code, usually in their account or by email after purchase.
  2. They share it with friends, mostly on WhatsApp, Instagram or in person.
  3. The friend uses the link or code and gets a welcome benefit, such as a discount on the first order.
  4. Once the friend completes a qualifying purchase or booking, the referrer gets their reward.
  5. The system tracks who referred whom and which rewards are pending, approved or used.

Choose a reward that fits your business

Reward typeSuitsWatch out for
Store credit or discount for bothOnline stores with repeat purchasesMargin on low-price products
Fixed coupon for the friend onlyNew stores building first ordersReferrer has less reason to share
Free product or upgradeBrands with low-cost add-onsStock and shipping cost
Free session or serviceSalons, clinics, gyms, coachingCapacity at busy times
Cash or bank payoutHigh-value servicesTax, KYC and payment admin
Donation to a causeMission-led brandsClear reporting needed

Think about timing too. A reward that arrives weeks after the friend's order feels disconnected, so tell referrers clearly when they will receive it and send an email the moment it is approved. Keep the reward easy to explain in one line. "Give your friend a discount on their first order and get store credit when they buy" works. Complicated tiers and points rarely get shared.

Work out what you can afford per new customer before choosing the amount. Think about your margin, average order value and how often customers come back. Do not copy what large brands offer if your margins are different.

Setting it up on WooCommerce

WooCommerce does not include a referral programme by default, but several plugins add one. Typical features to look for:

  • Unique referral links and codes for each customer.
  • Rewards as coupons or store credit, issued automatically after the referred order is completed (not just placed).
  • A referral section in My Account showing links, referrals and rewards.
  • Emails to referrers when rewards are earned.
  • Rules for minimum order value, excluded products and expiry.
  • Fraud checks such as blocking self-referrals by email, address or payment details.

The rewards usually work through coupons, so WooCommerce coupons and discounts is useful background. Test plugins on a staging site before going live, and check they work with your theme, checkout and caching.

Setting it up on Shopify

On Shopify, referral programmes run through apps from the Shopify App Store. Most offer referral links, discount codes for the friend, rewards for the referrer and a customer-facing widget. Compare apps on how rewards are issued, whether they work with your checkout and discount setup, and what the plan limits are. Keep the number of apps small; each one adds scripts that can slow the store. Shopify payment gateways in India is worth checking too if rewards interact with COD orders.

Referral programmes for service businesses

Clinics, coaching institutes, salons, interior designers and other service businesses can run referrals without an e-commerce plugin:

  • A referral page explaining the offer, with a form where the referrer enters their name and the friend's details (with the friend's agreement).
  • Or a simple referral code each customer quotes when booking.
  • A CRM or tracking sheet to record referrals, bookings and rewards.
  • Rewards applied manually when the friend completes a qualifying service.

For regulated professions such as healthcare and legal services, check your professional body's rules on incentives for referrals before offering rewards.

Make sharing easy

Most referrals in India happen on WhatsApp. Make that one tap:

  • A "Share on WhatsApp" button with a pre-written message including the link.
  • Copy-link and copy-code buttons.
  • A reminder in the order confirmation email and on the thank-you page. Thank-you pages are an underused spot for this.
  • A gentle reminder after the customer has received and used the product, when they are most likely to recommend it.

Write clear referral terms

Terms prevent disputes and abuse. Cover:

  • Who can refer and who counts as a new customer.
  • What qualifies: completed and not returned orders, minimum value, eligible products.
  • When the reward is issued and when it expires.
  • Limits on the number of rewards, if any.
  • That self-referrals and fake accounts are not allowed and rewards can be cancelled.
  • That you can change or end the programme with notice.

If you pay cash rewards, ask your CA about tax treatment and record keeping.

Prevent fraud and abuse

  • Issue rewards only after the return window closes.
  • Block referrals where email, phone, address or payment details match the referrer.
  • Watch for many new accounts from the same device or address.
  • Exclude COD orders from rewards until delivered and paid, if returns are high.

Measure whether it is worth it

Track referred orders, revenue from referred customers, rewards issued and how many referred customers buy again. Compare the cost per referred customer with what you spend on ads per new customer. If referrals bring loyal customers at a reasonable cost, promote the programme more; if few people share, test a simpler or more attractive offer.

Promote the programme where customers will see it

A referral programme that lives on one hidden page rarely gets used. Customers need to be reminded at the moments they feel good about you:

  • A dedicated referral page linked from the footer, account area and FAQ, explaining the offer in one line, then how it works in three steps.
  • The order confirmation and delivery emails, with the customer's own link already filled in.
  • Packaging inserts with a short code or QR code that opens the referral page. QR codes for business websites explains how to set them up so they keep working.
  • Your newsletter, occasionally, rather than in every issue. Email newsletters for small businesses covers frequency.
  • In person, for service businesses: a small card at the counter or after a consultation.

Avoid pop-ups that push the referral offer on first-time visitors. They have not bought yet, so asking them to refer friends feels premature and gets in the way of the first order.

A simple launch checklist

  1. Decide the reward, qualifying rules and budget per referred customer.
  2. Choose the plugin, app or manual process that fits your platform.
  3. Write the terms and the referral page copy.
  4. Set up sharing buttons, emails and the account section.
  5. Place test orders on staging as a referrer and as a friend, including a return, to check rewards are issued and cancelled correctly.
  6. Check page speed after installing anything new.
  7. Launch quietly to existing customers first, then promote more widely once it works.
  8. Review results after a month or two and adjust the offer if needed.

Starting small lets you fix problems with a few referrals rather than hundreds. It also gives you real data on how many customers share and how many friends buy, so later decisions are based on your own numbers rather than guesses.

Getting a referral programme set up

A referral programme can be set up on a WooCommerce store or a Shopify store, connected to your emails and checkout, and tested before launch. You can also hire a WordPress and Shopify developer in India to choose the right plugin or app and configure the rules.

Whichever platform you use, review the programme every few months. Check which customers refer most, whether referred customers return, and whether the reward still makes sense for your margins and current prices.

FAQs

How do I set up a referral program on my website?

Choose a reward, install a referral plugin (WooCommerce) or app (Shopify), configure links, rewards and rules, add sharing buttons and terms, then test the full flow on staging before launch.

What is a good referral reward?

One that is simple to explain and affordable for your margins, often a discount for the friend and store credit for the referrer. Work it out from your margin and average order value.

Can a service business run a referral programme?

Yes. A referral form or code plus a tracking sheet or CRM works well. Regulated professions should check their rules on incentives.

How do I stop people abusing referral rewards?

Issue rewards after the return window, block self-referrals by matching details, cap rewards if needed and state clear terms.

Do referral plugins slow down a website?

Some add scripts on every page. Choose lightweight options, test speed after installing and load widgets only where needed.
