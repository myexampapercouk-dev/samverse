WooCommerce Gift Cards and Store Credit: Setup, Rules, GST Questions and How Shopify Compares

How to sell WooCommerce gift cards and store credit in India: plugin options, terms, GST questions for your CA, fraud checks and Shopify gift cards.

Key takeaways
  • WooCommerce needs a plugin for gift cards and store credit, while Shopify includes gift cards on its plans, so the platform affects setup effort more than features.
  • Write clear terms for validity, partial use, refunds and lost codes before launch, and ask your CA how GST applies to gift card sales and redemptions.
  • Protect gift cards from misuse with random codes, payment checks on high-value orders and limits on how cards can be bought.

WooCommerce gift cards need a plugin: it creates a gift card product, generates a unique code after payment, emails it to the recipient and lets them redeem the balance at checkout. Shopify, by contrast, includes gift cards as a built-in feature. On either platform, the hard part is not the setup but the rules: validity, partial use, refunds, GST and fraud checks.

This guide explains how to sell gift cards and offer store credit on an Indian online store, and how WooCommerce and Shopify compare.

Why sell gift cards and offer store credit

  • Gifting without guessing: shoppers can give something from your store without knowing the recipient's size or taste, which suits festivals such as Diwali and Raksha Bandhan, weddings and birthdays.
  • Corporate gifting: businesses buying for staff or clients often prefer gift cards to physical hampers.
  • Returns without refunds: store credit keeps the value inside your business when a customer returns an item, if your returns policy offers it as an option.
  • Goodwill: a small credit after a delayed order can be quicker and cheaper than a full complaint process.
  • New customers: recipients often discover your brand for the first time through a gift.

How WooCommerce gift cards work

WooCommerce has no gift card feature in its core plugin. You add it with an extension, either the official gift cards extension or one of several third-party plugins. Most work in a similar way:

  1. You create a gift card product with fixed amounts, a custom amount field or both.
  2. The buyer enters the recipient's name, email and a message, and can often choose a delivery date.
  3. After a successful payment, the plugin generates a unique code and emails it to the recipient.
  4. The recipient enters the code at checkout, or the plugin applies it to their account balance.
  5. Any unused balance stays on the card for the next order.

Choose a plugin that works with your theme, your payment gateway and your checkout, especially if you use a customised checkout page or block-based checkout. Test it on a staging site before going live.

Shopify gift cards: what is built in

Shopify includes gift cards as a standard feature on its plans. You create a gift card product with set denominations, customers buy it like any other product, and Shopify emails the code. Staff can also issue gift cards manually from the admin, which is handy for goodwill credit. Codes are redeemed at checkout, and balances carry over.

For more control, such as scheduled delivery, custom designs or bulk corporate orders, Shopify stores typically add an app. If you are choosing a platform partly for gifting, see WooCommerce vs Shopify for Indian stores.

Store credit vs gift cards

They look similar but serve different purposes. A gift card is bought with money and can be given to anyone. Store credit is issued by you, usually against a return or as a goodwill gesture, and is tied to a customer account. Keep them separate in reporting so you know how much value is outstanding from each. Spell out in your returns and refunds policy whether customers can choose a refund or store credit, and never force credit where a refund is due.

Terms to write before launch

  • Validity: how long the card can be used. Check current rules on prepaid instruments and gift voucher validity with your CA or lawyer, and state the period clearly.
  • Partial use: whether the balance carries over.
  • Combining: whether gift cards can be used with coupons or sale prices.
  • Refunds: what happens if an order paid by gift card is returned, usually a refund back to the card.
  • Lost codes: how customers can request a resend and what proof you need.
  • Exclusions: whether gift cards can buy other gift cards.
  • Cash: make clear the card cannot be exchanged for cash, unless you choose otherwise.

Put these terms on the gift card product page, in the gift card email and in your website terms and conditions.

GST and accounting questions for your CA

The GST treatment of vouchers and gift cards depends on whether they can be used for one type of goods or many, and on when tax applies, at sale or at redemption. The rules have been the subject of clarifications, so ask your CA how to treat them for your store, and set up invoices and reports to match. If you already generate invoices with a plugin, check how it handles a gift card product and orders part-paid by gift card; the guide on WooCommerce GST invoices covers the basics. Shopify stores can read Shopify GST invoices in India.

Also track outstanding balances: unredeemed gift cards are value you owe customers, and your accountant will want a report of it.

Fraud and misuse checks

Gift cards are attractive to fraudsters because they behave like cash. Sensible protections:

  • Use long, random codes, never sequential numbers.
  • Send codes only after payment is confirmed, never on cash on delivery orders.
  • Review unusually large or repeated gift card purchases before releasing codes.
  • Limit attempts at the code field to stop guessing.
  • Keep a log of when each card was issued, emailed and redeemed.

Disable cash on delivery for gift card products specifically; the guide to COD in WooCommerce explains how to restrict payment methods by product.

Designing the gift card email and page

The gift card email is the recipient's first contact with your brand, so treat it like a product, not a system message. A good gift card email includes:

  • The sender's name and personal message near the top.
  • The amount and code, large and easy to copy on a phone.
  • A clear button to start shopping, linking to a popular collection rather than the homepage.
  • Validity and the short version of the terms, with a link to the full version.
  • Your support WhatsApp or email for problems with the code.

Check that the email lands in the inbox rather than spam, because a missing gift card turns a gift into a complaint. Sending store emails through proper SMTP with SPF and DKIM set up helps; see customising WooCommerce emails for the template side.

On the product page, show sample card designs, explain how delivery works and when the code arrives, and let buyers choose a delivery date for birthdays or anniversaries. Add a balance check page where recipients can enter a code and see what is left, which cuts down support messages.

Testing before you launch

  1. Buy a card with each payment method you allow and confirm the code arrives only after payment.
  2. Redeem part of a card, then the rest, and check balances.
  3. Combine a card with a coupon and confirm your rule is applied.
  4. Return an order paid by gift card and check where the refund goes.
  5. Check the invoice for a gift card purchase and an order part-paid by card.
  6. Try an invalid code several times to confirm limits work.

Promoting gift cards on your store

Gift cards sell when people see them at the right time. Add a gift card link in the header during festive seasons, a banner on product pages for last-minute gifting, a section on your corporate or bulk orders page, and a mention in your order confirmation emails. Because gift cards are delivered by email, they suit last-minute buyers: say so clearly in the run-up to festivals, when physical delivery may not arrive in time.

FAQs

How do I add gift cards to WooCommerce?

Install a gift card plugin, create a gift card product with your amounts, set up the email template and terms, then test buying and redeeming a card on a staging copy before launch.

Does Shopify have built-in gift cards?

Yes. Shopify includes gift cards on its plans, and staff can issue cards manually. Apps add extras like scheduled delivery or bulk orders.

Can customers use a gift card and a coupon together?

It depends on your settings and plugin. Decide your rule, configure it and state it in the terms.

Is GST charged on gift cards in India?

It depends on the type of voucher and when it is redeemed. Ask your CA for the current treatment for your store.

Should gift card codes be sent for COD orders?

No. Send codes only after online payment is confirmed, and disable COD for gift card products.
