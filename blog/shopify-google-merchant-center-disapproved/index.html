Shopify Products Disapproved in Google Merchant Center? How to Fix Price, GTIN, Image and Misrepresentation Issues

Shopify products disapproved in Google Merchant Center? Read the exact issue, fix price, stock, GTIN and policy problems in Shopify, then request a review.

Key takeaways
  • Read the issue on each product in Merchant Center before changing anything.
  • Fix data in Shopify, because the Google and YouTube app overwrites Merchant Center edits.
  • If every product is disapproved, fix account issues: contact details, policies and a working checkout.

If your Shopify products are disapproved in Google Merchant Center, open Merchant Center, go to the products section and read the exact issue Google lists for each product. In Indian stores the usual causes are a price or availability mismatch between the feed and the product page, missing GTIN or brand details, missing shipping or return settings, unverified contact details, or a website that fails Google's checks on policies and checkout.

This guide explains how the Shopify to Google connection works, how to read the disapproval reasons, and how to fix the most common ones without re-sending the same broken data.

Shopify Google Merchant Center products disapproved: the quick fix

  1. Find the reason. In Merchant Center, open Products and filter by disapproved or limited items. Click one product to see the specific issue, which attribute is affected and whether it applies to free listings, Shopping ads or both.
  2. Fix it in Shopify, not in Merchant Center. Shopify sends product data to Google through the Google and YouTube app. Edits made only in Merchant Center are usually overwritten at the next sync.
  3. Check the account-level issues first. If the whole account is suspended or has a misrepresentation warning, product fixes will not help until the account issue is resolved.
  4. Wait for the next sync, then request a review where Merchant Center offers one. Product updates can take some time to be processed.

How Shopify connects to Google Merchant Center

Most stores use Shopify's Google and YouTube app (sometimes still called the Google channel). It creates or links a Merchant Center account, verifies and claims your domain, and syncs products from your Shopify catalogue. Before checking individual products, open the app in Shopify and confirm:

  • The correct Merchant Center account is linked, and it is the only feed sending these products. Two feeds, for example from the app and an old third-party feed app, can conflict.
  • Your store is set to sell in India, with INR as the currency.
  • Products are published to the Google sales channel. A product not available on the channel will not sync.
  • The domain shown is your primary custom domain, not the myshopify address.

Price mismatches

Google compares the price in your feed with the price on your product page. Mismatches happen when a currency converter app changes displayed prices, when a discount app shows a different price after the page loads, or when the theme shows a price that differs from the variant Google receives. Keep one clear price for INR visitors, and if you run sales, use Shopify's compare-at price so the feed and page update together.

Availability mismatches

If quantity tracking is off, Shopify may tell Google a product is always in stock even when your page says sold out through an app or a manual badge. Turn on inventory tracking for each variant, or make sure the page and feed both reflect the same stock status.

GTIN, brand and identifier problems

For branded products sold by many retailers, Google expects a GTIN (the barcode number on the packaging). Enter it in the variant's Barcode field in Shopify. For your own brand, handmade products or custom items, there may be no GTIN; make sure the brand (vendor in Shopify) is filled in correctly and do not invent barcode numbers. Wrong GTINs cause more trouble than missing ones.

Image problems

The main product image should show the product clearly on a plain background, without logos, watermarks, price text or "sale" badges added on top. Lifestyle and promotional images can still be used as additional images.

Account-level issues: misrepresentation and website checks

When every product is disapproved, or the account shows a misrepresentation or website needs improvement warning, Google is judging the store, not individual items. Check:

  • Contact information. A real address, phone number and email address on a contact page, and matching business details in Merchant Center.
  • Policies. Clear refund and return, shipping, privacy and terms pages, linked from the footer, written for your business rather than copied text.
  • Checkout. Google checks that products can actually be bought. A store behind a password page, a broken checkout or a gateway in test mode fails this. If payments are failing, see payment gateways on Shopify in India, and remove the password page before connecting Google.
  • Honest claims. Remove claims you cannot support, fake countdown timers, exaggerated discounts or reviews you did not collect.
  • Secure site. The whole store should load on HTTPS on your custom domain.

After fixing these, request a review from the account issue in Merchant Center. Do not request reviews repeatedly without changes, because repeated failed reviews can add waiting time.

Shipping and returns settings for India

Merchant Center needs shipping costs and, increasingly, return policy details. Set a shipping service for India that matches your Shopify rates: free shipping over a threshold, a flat fee, or rates by weight. If your Shopify rates and Merchant Center shipping disagree, shoppers see a different cost at checkout, which can cause disapprovals. Add your return window and return cost under the return policy settings. For the Shopify side of shipping, see Shopify shipping in India.

Improve data so products show more often

Approved is not the same as visible. To give products a better chance in free listings and Shopping results:

  • Write product titles that start with what the item is, then brand, key attribute, size or colour. Avoid titles that are only a design name.
  • Fill in product type and Google product category where the app allows it.
  • Add variant details (size, colour, material) as proper variant options instead of putting them only in the description.
  • Keep descriptions factual and specific.

The same data improves your normal search results too; see the Shopify SEO checklist. If you also run a WooCommerce store, the approach differs; our Google Merchant Center for WooCommerce guide covers that side.

Before connecting a new store to Google

Connecting a store that is not ready is the fastest route to disapprovals. Before installing the Google and YouTube app, make sure the store is open to the public, payments work, every product has a real price, stock status and main image, and the footer links to your policies. Our guide to setting up a Shopify store in India lists the steps in order. If products load slowly, Google may also struggle to crawl them, so check how to fix a slow Shopify store as well.

How long fixes take

After you edit products in Shopify, the app syncs changes to Merchant Center, and Google then re-processes and may re-crawl the pages. Product-level fixes are often reflected within a few days. Account reviews can take longer. Check the diagnostics page rather than editing the same product again and again.

When to get help

Get help if the account is suspended for misrepresentation, if hundreds of products show mismatches caused by apps or theme code, or if you sell many variants and need a cleaner product structure. A Shopify developer can audit the feed, theme and apps together and fix the root cause. FAQs

Why are my Shopify products disapproved in Google Merchant Center?

Each product lists its own reason. Common ones are price or availability mismatches, missing GTIN or brand, image problems, missing shipping settings, or account-level issues such as unclear policies or a checkout that does not work.

How do I fix a price mismatch in Google Merchant Center on Shopify?

Make sure the price on the product page matches the price Shopify sends. Currency converters, discount apps and theme scripts that change prices after loading are the usual cause.

What is a GTIN and do I need one on Shopify?

A GTIN is the product's barcode number. Branded products from other manufacturers usually need one, entered in the Barcode field. Your own brand or handmade products may not have one; fill in the brand instead and never invent numbers.

What does misrepresentation mean in Google Merchant Center?

Google is not confident the store is trustworthy. Add full contact details, clear refund and shipping policies, a working checkout and honest claims, then request a review.

Should I edit products in Merchant Center or in Shopify?

In Shopify. The Google and YouTube app syncs data from Shopify, so changes made only in Merchant Center are usually overwritten.

How long does Google take to approve Shopify products after a fix?

Product fixes often show within a few days after the next sync and review. Account-level reviews can take longer, so make all the changes before requesting one.
