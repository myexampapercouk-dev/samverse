Breadcrumbs Explained: What They Are, the Three Types, How They Help SEO, BreadcrumbList Schema and How to Add Them in WordPress

Breadcrumbs are the small trail of links near the top of a page, such as Home / Blog / SEO / this article, that shows where the page sits on your website and lets visitors jump back to a section in one tap. They help Google understand how your pages are organised, add useful internal links to your category and service pages, and, with BreadcrumbList structured data, can be shown in search results instead of a raw URL. On WordPress, Rank Math, Yoast and WooCommerce can all generate them for you.

Breadcrumbs and their structured data are part of the setup in my WordPress SEO services, alongside schema for your business, services and articles, checked with Google's testing tools. The audit also looks for the two most common breadcrumb problems: trails that don't match the site's real structure, and two plugins outputting competing markup for the same page.

This guide covers breadcrumbs specifically. For structured data in general, see schema markup explained, and for links between pages beyond the breadcrumb trail, see internal linking explained.

What breadcrumbs are

The name comes from the fairy tale in which children drop breadcrumbs to find their way home. On a website, the trail runs from the homepage down to the page you're on, with every level except the current page as a link:

  • Blog article: Home / Blog / SEO / Breadcrumbs explained
  • Service page: Home / Services / AC Repair in Pune
  • Online store product: Home / Shop / Kurtas / Cotton Anarkali Kurta
  • Case study or project: Home / Work / Project name

The homepage doesn't need a trail, because there's nothing above it. Breadcrumbs are a secondary aid: they sit alongside your main menu, not in place of it. If your menu itself is confusing, fix that first; how to structure your website navigation covers menus and hierarchy.

The three types of breadcrumbs

TypeExampleUse it when
Hierarchy (location-based)Home / Services / Waterproofing / Terrace WaterproofingAlmost always. It shows where a page sits in the structure, and it is what SEO plugins generate
Attribute-basedHome / Sarees / Silk / RedOnline stores and listings where visitors filter by attributes such as fabric, size, colour or location
History (path-based)Pages this visitor clicked through in this sessionRarely. The browser's back button already does this, and the trail changes for every visitor

For a small business website, hierarchy breadcrumbs are the right choice in nearly every case. Attribute breadcrumbs make sense on stores with filtered category pages, but be careful which filtered URLs you let Google index, or you can end up with thousands of near-identical pages; SEO for category and tag pages explains how to decide.

How breadcrumbs help visitors

  • Orientation: someone who lands on a deep page from Google, an ad or a WhatsApp link immediately sees which section of the site they're in.
  • One-tap navigation: a visitor reading about one waterproofing service can tap "Waterproofing" to see all of them, instead of going back to the homepage or opening the menu.
  • Less effort on phones: a short trail at the top is quicker than opening a hamburger menu and working through nested items.
  • Context for the page: "Home / Kurtas / Cotton Anarkali Kurta" tells a shopper what kind of product they're looking at before they read a word of the description.

How breadcrumbs help SEO

  • Internal links to section pages: every article links to its category and every product to its collection, with descriptive anchor text, on every page. That's a steady supply of links to the hub pages you most want to rank.
  • A clear hierarchy: the trail, and the BreadcrumbList markup behind it, tell Google how your pages relate, which helps it understand that a page about terrace waterproofing belongs to your waterproofing services.
  • Shallower click depth: section pages that every deep page links to are rarely buried or orphaned.
  • How results look: Google can show the breadcrumb trail in a result in place of the URL. At the time of writing, this applies to desktop results: in early 2025 Google said it would show only the site name and domain on mobile results and stop showing breadcrumbs there. The markup is still supported and still helps Google understand the page, so check Google's current documentation rather than relying on how results looked a year ago.

Breadcrumbs won't make a weak page rank. They are a small, reliable improvement that supports good content and a sensible structure.

BreadcrumbList schema: a JSON-LD example

The visible trail is for people. BreadcrumbList structured data describes the same trail for search engines. On WordPress your SEO plugin usually writes it, but this is what it looks like in the page's code for a service page:

<script type="application/ld+json">
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "BreadcrumbList",
  "itemListElement": [
    {
      "@type": "ListItem",
      "position": 1,
      "name": "Home",
      "item": "https://example.com/"
    },
    {
      "@type": "ListItem",
      "position": 2,
      "name": "Services",
      "item": "https://example.com/services/"
    },
    {
      "@type": "ListItem",
      "position": 3,
      "name": "AC Repair in Pune"
    }
  ]
}
</script>
  • position starts at 1 for the top level and counts down the trail.
  • name is the label visitors see; keep it short and identical to the visible trail.
  • item is the full URL of each level. Google's documentation says it can be left out for the last item, the current page, in which case the page's own URL is used.
  • Match the visible trail. Markup describing a trail that isn't on the page, or a different one, breaks Google's structured data guidelines.
  • Follow how people browse, not just the URL. Google recommends trails that represent a typical path to the page, which doesn't have to mirror the folders in the URL. A product listed in two categories may have more than one trail.

How to add breadcrumbs in WordPress

Most WordPress sites already have everything needed; the job is choosing one source and placing it in your templates. Menu names change between plugin versions, so treat these steps as a guide at the time of writing.

Rank Math

Turn the feature on under Rank Math's General Settings, in the Breadcrumbs section, where you can also set the separator, the homepage label and whether categories appear in the trail. Then display it, either through your theme's built-in support for Rank Math breadcrumbs, the shortcode, or a line of PHP in a child theme template:

[rank_math_breadcrumb]

<?php if ( function_exists( 'rank_math_the_breadcrumbs' ) ) {
    rank_math_the_breadcrumbs();
} ?>

Yoast SEO

Yoast includes BreadcrumbList in its structured data by default. To show a visible trail, enable breadcrumbs in Yoast's settings, under the Advanced section, then add it with your theme's option, the Yoast breadcrumbs block, the shortcode or the PHP function:

[wpseo_breadcrumb]

<?php if ( function_exists( 'yoast_breadcrumb' ) ) {
    yoast_breadcrumb( '<p id="breadcrumbs">', '</p>' );
} ?>

If a post sits in several categories, both Yoast and Rank Math let you set a primary category in the editor, which decides the category shown in the trail. Choose the one that matches the hub page you want the post to support. Yoast vs Rank Math vs All in One SEO compares the plugins more broadly.

WooCommerce

WooCommerce has its own breadcrumb, usually Home / Category / Product, which many themes show on shop, category and product pages, and it outputs BreadcrumbList markup for that trail. If your SEO plugin also outputs breadcrumb markup, you can end up with two competing versions on product pages. Pick one: keep WooCommerce's trail, or replace it with your SEO plugin's trail through the theme or plugin settings, then check the page source for duplicate BreadcrumbList blocks. WooCommerce SEO covers product and category pages in more depth.

Themes, page builders and block themes

Many themes have a breadcrumb option in the Customizer, often with a choice of which plugin's trail to use. Elementor Pro has a Breadcrumbs widget that, at the time of writing, displays the trail generated by your SEO plugin. In a block theme, add your SEO plugin's breadcrumbs block or shortcode to the post, page and product templates in the Site Editor, so every page gets it without editing each one.

Placement and styling

  • Above the main heading, near the top of the content area, where visitors expect it. Below a large hero image it is easily missed.
  • Small but readable: smaller than body text is fine, but links must be legible and easy to tap on a phone.
  • Simple separators such as a slash or a chevron, added with CSS so screen readers don't read them aloud.
  • Short labels: "Waterproofing", not "Our Waterproofing Solutions for Homes and Offices". Long titles can be shortened or hidden for the current page.
  • Consistent everywhere: same position, same style and the same logic on every template.
  • Mobile: let a long trail wrap or truncate the current page's title, rather than pushing the layout sideways.

For accessibility, wrap the trail in a navigation element with a label, use a list, and mark the current page:

<nav aria-label="Breadcrumb">
  <ol>
    <li><a href="https://example.com/">Home</a></li>
    <li><a href="https://example.com/services/">Services</a></li>
    <li><span aria-current="page">AC Repair in Pune</span></li>
  </ol>
</nav>

Common breadcrumb mistakes

  • Two trails on one page, for example the theme's and the SEO plugin's, or duplicate BreadcrumbList markup from two sources
  • Markup that describes a different trail from the one visitors see
  • "Uncategorised" appearing in article trails because posts were never given a proper category
  • Trail links pointing to category pages that are set to noindex or redirect elsewhere
  • Breadcrumbs on the homepage, or a trail that only says "Home"
  • Labels that are far too long, or stuffed with keywords

Testing your breadcrumbs

After setting breadcrumbs up, paste a few URLs from different templates, such as an article, a service page and a product, into Google's Rich Results Test and check that a breadcrumb item is detected with the right labels and URLs. Over the following weeks, Search Console shows breadcrumb errors and warnings across the site in its breadcrumb enhancement report. How to test structured data and rich results walks through both tools.

FAQs

Do breadcrumbs help SEO?

Yes, modestly. They add internal links to your section pages, make your site's hierarchy clear and, with BreadcrumbList markup, can show a trail in search results. They support good pages rather than making weak ones rank.

Does Google still show breadcrumbs in search results?

At the time of writing, breadcrumb trails appear in desktop results, while mobile results were simplified in 2025 to show the site name and domain. Google still supports the markup, so it remains worth adding.

Should the current page be a link in the breadcrumb trail?

No. Every level above the current page should be a link, and the current page shown as plain text and marked as the current page for screen readers. In the markup, the last item can leave out its URL.

Do I need breadcrumb schema if I use Yoast or Rank Math?

You don't need to add it yourself. Yoast includes BreadcrumbList by default, and Rank Math adds it when its breadcrumbs feature is on. Check that your theme or WooCommerce isn't adding a second version.

Should breadcrumbs follow my URL structure?

Not necessarily. Google recommends trails that reflect how people typically reach a page. If your URLs are flat but your content is organised in sections, the trail can show the sections.
