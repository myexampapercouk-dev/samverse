Selling Subscriptions With WooCommerce in India: Plugins, Recurring Payments, Billing Cycles, Trials, Pausing and Retention

Key takeaways
  • WooCommerce needs a subscription plugin for recurring products, and the gateway integration must support automatic renewals with that plugin, so confirm both together before you build.
  • In India, automatic charges generally run on a mandate the customer approves once, so many stores combine automatic renewals with prepaid plans or emailed renewal links.
  • Easy pausing, honest renewal reminders, a clear cancel option and a GST invoice for every renewal keep subscribers longer and cause fewer payment disputes.

WooCommerce doesn't sell subscriptions on its own. You add a subscription plugin, set up each product's billing cycle, trial and renewal rules, and connect a payment gateway whose WooCommerce integration supports automatic renewals with that plugin. In India, automatic charges generally run on an e-mandate the customer approves once, so many stores combine automatic billing with prepaid plans or emailed renewal links.

Subscriptions are one of the features I scope and quote separately as part of WooCommerce development: the plugin and gateway are checked together before the build, and renewals, failed payments and emails are tested in the gateway's test mode before the store takes real orders. What you can sell on subscription

  • Repeat consumables: tea, coffee, oils, flours, pet food or skincare refills, often with a lower subscribe-and-save price.
  • Meal and tiffin plans: weekly or monthly plans; see websites for tiffin and meal subscriptions.
  • Curated boxes: snacks, books, stationery or hobby kits.
  • Memberships: clubs, gyms, communities or members-only content.
  • Services: annual maintenance contracts, retainers or monthly coaching.
  • Digital access: software licences, template libraries or course access.

Prepaid plans, manual renewals or automatic billing?

Automatic recurring billing is powerful, but it adds moving parts. Sometimes a simpler setup does the job:

SetupHow it worksBest whenMain risk
Prepaid planA three-month or yearly pack sold as a normal productCustomers are happy to pay up frontForgetting to remind them before it runs out
Manual renewalThe plugin emails a renewal invoice with a payment link each cycleA small customer base, or no gateway support for automatic renewalsCustomers who don't get round to paying
Automatic billingThe customer approves a mandate once, and renewals are charged automaticallyMany subscribers and predictable cyclesFailed charges, cancelled mandates and plugin compatibility

Many businesses start with prepaid plans or manual renewals and move to automatic billing once demand is proven. A prepaid pack doesn't even need a subscription plugin; a simple product or a variation for each plan length is enough.

Choosing a plugin for WooCommerce subscriptions in India

The official WooCommerce Subscriptions extension is the most established option. Others include YITH WooCommerce Subscription and several plugins in the WordPress.org directory, with free and paid tiers. Features and pricing change, so compare them on the points that matter to your store:

What to compareQuestions to ask
Gateway supportDoes your Indian gateway's WooCommerce plugin support automatic renewals with this subscription plugin, or only manual ones?
Billing optionsWeekly, monthly, yearly or custom intervals? Fixed-length plans? Trials and sign-up fees? Renewals on one date for everyone?
Customer controlsCan customers pause, cancel, switch plans and update their payment method from My Account?
Failed paymentsAre failed renewals retried, and is the customer told?
EmailsWhich renewal, reminder and cancellation emails are built in?
CompatibilityDoes it work with your invoice, shipping and checkout plugins?
ReportsCan you see active subscribers, upcoming renewals and cancellations?

With WooCommerce Subscriptions, for example, you choose a simple or variable subscription product type when adding a product, set the price, billing interval, length, trial and sign-up fee on the product, and manage store-wide options under WooCommerce > Settings > Subscriptions. Other plugins follow a similar pattern with different menus.

Recurring payments in India: mandates, UPI AutoPay and gateways

This is where Indian stores need the most care. Under RBI rules for recurring payments, an automatic charge generally needs a mandate the customer registers once with extra authentication, a notification a set period before each charge, and authentication again for charges above a certain limit. Customers can also cancel a mandate from their bank, card or UPI app. The details are updated from time to time, so check the current position with your gateway.

  • Mandate types: gateways may offer card mandates, UPI AutoPay and eNACH (bank account debits), each with its own limits, sign-up flow and suitability for different plan values.
  • Plugin support is the real test: a gateway offering recurring payments doesn't mean its WooCommerce plugin handles renewals for your subscription plugin. Ask the gateway in writing which subscription plugins it supports, and test it.
  • International gateways: some have limited availability for Indian businesses or for recurring charges on Indian cards, so confirm the current position before planning around one.
  • A manual fallback: keep renewal invoices with a payment link switched on for customers whose mandate fails or who prefer to pay each time.

Test it before launch

  1. Put the gateway in test mode and buy a subscription as a customer would, on a phone.
  2. Trigger a renewal from the subscription's admin screen, which most plugins allow for testing, and check that the order, payment and emails all appear.
  3. Simulate a failed renewal and check what the customer and you receive.
  4. Cancel, pause and switch plans from the customer's My Account page.
  5. Check that a GST invoice is created for the renewal order, not only the first one.

Billing cycles, trials and sign-up fees

  • Billing interval: match it to how the product is used. A monthly coffee plan and a quarterly skincare refill have different rhythms.
  • Plan length: a plan can renew until cancelled or end after a fixed number of cycles, such as a six-month course of supplements.
  • Synchronised renewals: charging everyone on the same date makes packing and dispatch easier; the first payment can be adjusted for the shorter first period.
  • Free trials: useful for services and digital access, less so for physical goods. How a trial works with a mandate depends on the gateway, so test it.
  • Sign-up fees: suit a starter kit or a one-off setup, and should be shown clearly at checkout.
  • Switching plans: decide whether upgrades and downgrades apply immediately with a price adjustment, or from the next renewal.

Pausing, skipping and cancelling

Flexibility keeps subscribers who would otherwise leave. Someone travelling for a month may pause rather than cancel, and someone overstocked may skip one delivery. Depending on the plugin and settings, customers can suspend a subscription themselves from My Account, with limits you choose.

Make cancelling easy to find and explain what happens next: whether access or deliveries continue until the end of the paid period, and that cancelling on your site stops renewals but a mandate may also be visible in the customer's bank or UPI app. Hidden cancel options lead to chargebacks and complaints. Consumer protection rules on auto-renewals also apply, so check the current requirements with your lawyer or CA.

Renewal emails and reminders

  • Before a renewal: a friendly reminder with the date and amount builds trust and reduces disputes, even when the bank or gateway also sends a pre-debit notification. Check whether your plugin sends one; if not, an add-on or your email tool can.
  • After a renewal: the order confirmation and invoice, plus delivery details for physical products.
  • Manual renewals: the renewal invoice with a payment link, followed by a gentle reminder.
  • Failed payments: what happened and a one-click way to pay or update the payment method.
  • Cancellations and expiry: confirmation, and an easy way back.

Brand these emails like the rest of your store and check they reach the inbox; customising WooCommerce emails covers both, including WhatsApp and SMS alongside email.

Running physical product subscriptions

  • Stock planning: active subscriptions tell you roughly what you'll need next cycle.
  • Shipping: check that shipping charges and delivery areas apply correctly to renewal orders, not only the first one.
  • Invoices: each renewal is a new order, so it needs its own GST invoice; see GST invoices in WooCommerce.
  • Price changes: decide how existing subscribers are treated when prices rise, and tell them well in advance.

Retention: keeping subscribers longer

  • Make the first cycle great, because the first delivery or month largely decides whether people stay.
  • Offer pause, skip and a smaller plan before a cancellation goes through.
  • Ask why people cancel with a one-question survey, and fix the common answers.
  • Reward loyalty with small perks for long-term subscribers.

Be clear at checkout and in your terms

  • Show the recurring price, billing frequency and any sign-up fee on the product page and at checkout.
  • State when the first and later payments will be taken.
  • Explain how to pause, cancel or switch, and your refund rules for renewals; see returns and refunds for WooCommerce stores.

FAQs

Does WooCommerce support subscriptions without a plugin?

No. WooCommerce sells one-off products out of the box. Recurring billing needs a subscription plugin, although a prepaid pack can be sold as a normal product.

Can I use UPI AutoPay with WooCommerce subscriptions?

It depends on your gateway and on whether its WooCommerce plugin supports automatic renewals with your subscription plugin. Several Indian gateways offer recurring payments, so ask specifically about your plugin and test it in test mode.

What happens when a renewal payment fails?

Typically the renewal order stays unpaid and the subscription goes on hold until the customer pays. Many plugins can retry the charge and email the customer a link to pay or update their payment method.

Can customers pause their subscription?

With most subscription plugins, yes, either from My Account or by asking you. You can usually limit how often or how long they pause.

Do I need a GST invoice for every renewal?

Each renewal is a new order, so set your invoice plugin to create one for renewals too. Ask your CA to confirm the rates and format for your products.

How do I set up WooCommerce subscriptions in India?

Install a subscription plugin, create a subscription product with its billing cycle, trial and sign-up fee, then connect a payment gateway whose WooCommerce integration supports automatic renewals with that plugin. Test sign-up, renewal, a failed payment and a cancellation before launch, and check current mandate rules with your gateway.
