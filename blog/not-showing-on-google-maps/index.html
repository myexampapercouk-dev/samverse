Why Is My Business Not Showing on Google Maps? Causes and Fixes

Business not showing on Google Maps in India? Check verification, suspension, category, address, service area and reviews, then fix each cause.

Key takeaways
  • First confirm whether the listing is missing, unverified, suspended or simply ranking low, because each problem has a different fix.
  • Most visibility issues come from verification, the wrong primary category, address or service area problems, or a thin profile with few reviews.
  • A website with the same name, address and phone number, plus genuine local pages and reviews, strengthens the listing over time.

If your business is not showing on Google Maps, it is usually for one of five reasons: the profile is not verified, it has been suspended, it is set up with the wrong category or address, it is a service-area business with a hidden address, or it exists but ranks too low for the searches you are trying. Start by searching your exact business name; if it appears, the problem is ranking, not visibility.

This guide walks through each cause, how to check it, and how to fix it for Indian businesses, from shops and clinics to home service providers.

Step one: work out which problem you have

"Not showing" can mean very different things. Check these in order:

  1. Search your exact business name on Google Maps, on a phone with location on and while signed out.
  2. Open your Business Profile dashboard while signed in as the owner and look for status messages such as "verification required" or "suspended".
  3. Search a general term such as "dentist near me" or "AC repair Wakad" standing near your business.

Cause 1: the profile is not verified

An unverified profile may not appear publicly, or may appear with limited information. Google offers different verification methods depending on the business, such as a video recording, phone, email or postcard, and the options shown can change. Follow the method offered in your dashboard.

For video verification, Google typically wants to see the location, signage or equipment, and proof that you manage the business. Record in one continuous take, show the street or landmark, the business signage, and something only an owner can access, such as the shop counter or business documents. Many verifications fail because the video does not clearly link the business to the address.

Cause 2: the profile has been suspended

Suspensions happen when Google thinks the profile breaks its guidelines. Common triggers include:

  • Keywords added to the business name, such as "Best Plumber Pune 24x7".
  • Using a virtual office, co-working desk or home address that does not meet the rules for that business type.
  • Multiple listings for the same business at the same address.
  • Sudden large edits to name, address and category at once.
  • A business type Google treats as higher-risk, such as locksmiths or some repair services, with extra checks.

Fix the cause first, for example by removing keywords from the name so it matches your real signage, and then submit a reinstatement request with evidence such as shop photos, utility bills or GST registration details showing the business name and address. Be accurate and patient; repeated requests without fixing the underlying issue rarely help.

Cause 3: wrong category or incomplete profile

The primary category is one of the strongest signals for which searches you appear in. A dental clinic set up as "Medical clinic" may not appear for "dentist near me". Choose the most specific primary category that matches your main business, then add a few relevant secondary categories.

Then complete everything else:

  • Opening hours, including holiday hours.
  • Services or products, using the words customers search for.
  • A business description that explains what you do and where.
  • Photos of the exterior, interior and real work.
  • Website link, ideally to a page matching the location.

The Google Business Profile checklist covers every field.

Cause 4: address, pin and service area problems

Indian addresses can be complicated: building names, wings, landmarks, lanes and multiple local names for the same area. Make sure the address in your profile is the one on your signage and documents, and check the map pin sits on your actual building, not the middle of the road or a neighbouring society.

Home service businesses such as plumbers, electricians, pest control and tutors who visit homes often hide their address and set a service area. That is correct if customers do not visit you, but it can reduce visibility for searches far from your base. Set realistic service areas based on where you actually work; listing a whole state does not help.

If you moved, update the address rather than creating a new listing, and update your website and directory listings to match.

Cause 5: the listing exists but ranks low

Google ranks local results mainly on relevance, distance and prominence. You cannot change distance, but you can improve the other two:

  • Relevance: correct categories, services, description and a website with clear pages for each main service.
  • Prominence: reviews, consistent citations, mentions on local sites and a website that Google trusts.

Reviews matter a lot. Ask every satisfied customer, make it easy with a direct review link sent on WhatsApp, and reply to every review. See how to get more Google reviews.

Consistency matters too. Your business name, address and phone number should match across your website, Google profile and directories such as Justdial and IndiaMART. The guide on business directories and citations in India explains which to use.

Activity helps conversion and keeps the profile current. Regular Google Business Profile posts and new photos show customers you are open and active.

How your website affects Maps visibility

A Google profile without a website is weaker than one backed by a good site. The website should:

  1. Show the same business name, address and phone number as the profile, ideally in the footer and on the contact page.
  2. Include an embedded map on the contact page; see adding Google Maps to your website.
  3. Have a page for each main service and, if you have several branches, a page per location.
  4. Use LocalBusiness schema markup with correct details.
  5. Load fast on mobile, since most local searches happen on phones.

If the website itself is not indexed, fix that too; see WordPress site not showing on Google.

Duplicates and edits by others

Old listings created by previous owners, staff or customers can split reviews and confuse Google. Search for variations of your name and old phone numbers. Use the "suggest an edit" option to mark a duplicate or closed listing, or request ownership if the listing is genuinely yours.

Anyone can suggest edits to a public profile, and Google sometimes accepts them. Check your profile regularly for changed hours, phone numbers or categories, and correct anything wrong from the dashboard.

Multiple branches and shared addresses

Businesses with several branches should have one verified profile per real location that is staffed and open to customers during its stated hours. Each branch needs its own phone number where possible, its own hours and a matching location page on the website. Do not create profiles for areas where you have no premises; that is a common reason for suspension.

Shared addresses cause their own problems. Doctors in a multi-specialty hospital, shops in a mall or offices in a business centre should include the unit, floor or shop number so the listing is clearly distinct. Where two different businesses genuinely operate from one address, keep names, categories and phone numbers clearly separate.

How long fixes take

Verification, reinstatement and edits can take anything from a few minutes to several weeks, depending on Google's checks. Ranking improvements from reviews, categories and website work build gradually. Avoid making many changes at once, as that can trigger fresh reviews of the profile; change one thing, wait, and check again.

FAQs

Why is my business not showing on Google Maps?

Usually because the profile is unverified, suspended, has the wrong category or address, uses a hidden service area, or ranks low for general searches. Search your exact name first to see which problem it is.

How do I get my business to show on Google Maps?

Create and verify a Business Profile, choose the right primary category, complete every field, add photos, collect reviews and back it with a website using the same details.

Why is my Google Business Profile suspended?

Common causes include keywords in the business name, an address that does not meet the rules, duplicate listings or sudden major edits. Fix the cause, then request reinstatement with evidence.

Why does my business show for its name but not for near me searches?

That is a ranking issue. Improve categories, services, reviews, citations and your website's local pages, and check how far the searcher is from you.

Does having a website help Google Maps ranking?

Yes. A website with matching details, local pages and schema markup strengthens the trust and relevance signals behind your listing.
