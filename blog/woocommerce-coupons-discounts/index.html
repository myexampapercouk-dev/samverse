WooCommerce Coupon Types and Discounts: Set Up Offers That Sell Without Killing Margins

WooCommerce coupon discount types explained: percentage, fixed cart and fixed product, plus usage limits, free shipping coupons, auto-apply, BOGO and sale tips.

Key takeaways
  • WooCommerce has three built-in coupon discount types: percentage discount, fixed cart discount and fixed product discount, and any of them can also unlock free shipping.
  • Usage restrictions and limits (minimum spend, products, sale items, individual use, allowed emails and per-customer limits) protect your margins and stop codes leaking.
  • Automatic discounts, coupon links and buy-one-get-one offers need an extension; for festival sales, set expiry dates in the right timezone, test at checkout and measure profit, not just orders.

Discounts can bring first-time buyers, clear old stock and recover abandoned carts. Used carelessly, they train customers never to pay full price and eat into your profits. WooCommerce's built-in coupons give you plenty of control once you know what each setting does.

In short: WooCommerce has three coupon discount types, percentage discount, fixed cart discount and fixed product discount, and any coupon can also grant free shipping. You create them under Marketing, then Coupons, and control who can use them with usage restrictions and limits. Automatic discounts and "buy one, get one" offers need an extension. Menu names can change between versions, so check WooCommerce's current docs if something has moved.

The three WooCommerce coupon discount types

Discount typeWhat it doesExampleGood for
Percentage discountTakes a percentage off the eligible items in the cart10% off everything, or 15% off one categorySite-wide sales, category offers, first-order codes
Fixed cart discountTakes a fixed amount off the whole cart, once per order₹200 off the orderMinimum-spend offers, abandoned cart codes
Fixed product discountTakes a fixed amount off each eligible item, for every unit in the cart₹50 off each T-shirtProduct launches, clearing specific stock

The difference between the two fixed types catches people out. A fixed cart discount applies once, however many items are in the cart. A fixed product discount applies to every eligible unit, so five T-shirts get five discounts. Use the "Limit usage to X items" setting to cap that.

Coupons reduce a price, while gift cards and store credit hold value a customer has paid for or earned; for those, see selling gift cards and store credit in WooCommerce.

How to create a coupon in WooCommerce

  1. Check coupons are switched on: WooCommerce, Settings, General, "Enable the use of coupon codes"
  2. Go to Marketing, Coupons and click Add coupon
  3. Enter the code customers will type, or generate a random one
  4. In the General tab, choose the discount type and amount, tick free shipping if needed, and set an expiry date
  5. Set usage restrictions and usage limits (below), then publish
  6. Test the code in a private window with a real cart before announcing it

Usage restrictions: who and what a coupon applies to

  • Minimum and maximum spend: for example, a fixed amount off orders above a set cart value. A maximum stops a percentage code being used on very large orders.
  • Individual use only: the coupon can't be combined with other coupons
  • Exclude sale items: avoids double discounts. Product-level coupons won't apply to sale items, and cart-level coupons only work if the cart has at least one item not on sale.
  • Products and categories: include or exclude specific items, such as new arrivals or low-margin products
  • Allowed emails: restrict a code to specific customers, such as a list of past buyers or one corporate client

Usage limits: how many times

  • Usage limit per coupon: the total number of times the code can be used, useful for "first 100 orders" offers
  • Usage limit per user: usually 1 for welcome codes; WooCommerce checks this against the customer's account or billing email
  • Limit usage to X items: caps how many items a percentage or fixed product discount applies to

Public codes leak to coupon websites quickly. For private offers, use unique codes per customer, an allowed-emails list or a low usage limit.

Free shipping coupons

Free shipping needs two settings working together, which is why it often "doesn't work":

  1. On the coupon, tick "Allow free shipping"
  2. In WooCommerce, Settings, Shipping, add the Free shipping method to the right shipping zone, and set "Free shipping requires" to a valid free shipping coupon (or a coupon or minimum order amount)

The coupon can be a free-shipping-only code with a zero discount amount, or combined with a discount. If you ship across India with different courier rates, set up your zones first; see WooCommerce shipping setup for India.

Auto-apply coupons, coupon links and BOGO

Core WooCommerce coupons are typed in by the customer. For anything automatic, you need an extension:

  • Auto-apply and coupon links: coupon plugins such as Smart Coupons and Advanced Coupons can apply a code automatically when conditions are met, or from a link in an email or WhatsApp message
  • Buy one, get one (BOGO): offered by coupon extensions and by dynamic pricing plugins such as Discount Rules for WooCommerce
  • Tiered and bulk pricing: "buy 3, save more" rules are usually better handled by a dynamic pricing plugin than by coupons; for trade customers, see wholesale and dealer pricing

Features differ between free and paid versions and change over time, so check each plugin's current feature list. Also confirm it works with your checkout, especially if you use WooCommerce's block-based cart and checkout, and avoid running two discount plugins whose rules can stack.

Discount ideas that work

  • First-order discount for newsletter or WhatsApp sign-ups, limited to one use per customer
  • Free shipping above a cart value that raises average order size
  • Abandoned cart coupon in a follow-up message; see abandoned cart recovery
  • Loyalty discounts for repeat customers, using allowed emails
  • Prepaid discounts to reduce COD; see cash on delivery. Core coupons can't restrict by payment method, so this needs a plugin or custom code

Coupons can also power a customer referral programme; see how to set up a referral programme on your website. Another way to raise order value without a coupon is to sell sensible sets or suggest the right add-ons; see product bundles, upsells and cross-sells in WooCommerce.

Festival-sale tips for Indian stores

Diwali, Navratri, Raksha Bandhan, Independence Day and end-of-season sales bring the biggest coupon traffic of the year. Plan them properly; see festival campaigns for the wider campaign. For the coupons themselves:

  • Check the timezone: coupon expiry follows your store's timezone in WordPress, Settings, General, so make sure it's set to India before relying on a midnight cut-off
  • Decide how codes combine: if products already have sale prices, use "Exclude sale items" or "Individual use only" so discounts don't stack beyond your margin
  • Use separate codes per channel: one for Instagram, one for email, one per influencer, so you can see which worked
  • Show the offer clearly: a banner with the code, the minimum spend and the end date reduces "code not working" messages
  • Check GST invoices: ask your CA how discounts should appear on invoices; see WooCommerce GST setup and invoices
  • Test under load: run a full checkout with each code before launch, and make sure the store stays fast when traffic jumps; see WooCommerce speed optimisation

Watch out for

  • Constant sales: shoppers learn to wait for the next discount
  • Hidden margins: calculate profit after discount, shipping and payment fees
  • Coupon field distraction: an obvious coupon box can send shoppers off searching for codes; consider making it less prominent

Measure results

WooCommerce's Analytics section has a Coupons report showing how often each code was used and the total discount given. Compare orders and profit during campaigns with normal periods, and track campaign links with UTM tags.

FAQs

What are the coupon discount types in WooCommerce?

Three: percentage discount, fixed cart discount and fixed product discount. Free shipping is a separate tick box that can be added to any of them.

Can WooCommerce apply a coupon automatically?

Not with core coupons, which customers type in. Coupon and dynamic pricing extensions add auto-apply rules and coupon links.

Why is my WooCommerce free shipping coupon not working?

Usually because the Free shipping method isn't added to the customer's shipping zone, or isn't set to require a coupon. Both the coupon and the shipping method need setting up.

Can customers use more than one coupon in WooCommerce?

Yes, by default. Tick "Individual use only" on a coupon to stop it being combined with others.

How do I limit a WooCommerce coupon to one use per customer?

Set "Usage limit per user" to 1 in the coupon's usage limits. For extra control, restrict it to allowed emails.
