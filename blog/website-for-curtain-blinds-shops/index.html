Websites for Curtain and Blinds Shops: Swatches, Measuring and Installation

How to plan a website for a curtain and blinds shop in India: fabric swatches, blinds types, measuring visits, WooCommerce or Shopify, and local SEO.

Key takeaways
  • Show real fabric and blinds photos with honest colour, organised by room, type and light control, so buyers can shortlist before visiting.
  • Make a home measuring visit or swatch request the main call to action, with a form that asks for rooms, window count and area.
  • Sell online only what can be ordered without measuring, such as standard-size curtains, using WooCommerce or Shopify with clear size options.

A website for a curtain and blinds shop should help buyers browse fabrics and blind types with honest photos, understand light control, fabric care and motorisation options, and book a home measuring visit. For standard-size products, an online store on WooCommerce or Shopify can sit alongside the enquiry side.

Most customers come to you while doing up a new flat, renovating a room or replacing faded curtains. They have seen ideas on Instagram, they know roughly what they want, and they want to know if you can make it, how long it will take and whether you will come to measure. This guide covers what to include.

What customers want to know before they call

  • Which fabrics and blinds you offer, with real photos
  • Which options suit each room: blackout for bedrooms, sheers for living rooms, moisture-friendly blinds for kitchens and bathrooms
  • Whether you do stitching, tracks and rods, and installation
  • Whether you visit to measure, which areas you cover and whether there is a charge
  • Typical timelines from order to installation
  • What drives the price: fabric, lining, stitching style, track type and motorisation

Fabric catalogues with real swatch photos

Fabric is bought on colour and texture, so photography matters more than in most businesses. Photograph swatches in daylight against a neutral background, include a close-up of the weave and a hanging shot to show drape, and avoid heavy filters that change the colour. Add a note that colours vary slightly on screens and that you will bring physical samples.

Organise fabrics the way people choose: by room, by light control (sheer, dim-out, blackout), by colour family and by material such as cotton, linen look, polyester or velvet. For each collection, give care instructions and whether it suits sunny, west-facing windows. If you stock hundreds of fabrics, you do not need a page for each; collection pages with a selection of swatches work better.

Helping customers visualise their options

A gallery of real installations does more than catalogue photos. For each project, note the room, fabric or blind type, track or mechanism and the area. Before-and-after photos of the same window are particularly convincing. Short guides on topics people search, such as "blackout curtains vs blackout blinds" or "which blinds suit a kitchen window", bring in buyers early and give your staff something to share on WhatsApp.

For broader furnishing ranges, see websites for home decor stores.

Motorised blinds and curtains

Motorised tracks and blinds are a growing part of many shops' work, especially in new apartments and offices. Explain the options plainly: remote, wall switch, app or voice assistant control, wired or battery operated, and what the customer needs to arrange, such as a power point near the window. Mention which brands or systems you install only if you genuinely work with them, and explain warranty and service for the motor separately from the fabric.

Your measuring and installation service

For made-to-measure work, the home visit is usually the main call to action. Explain the process on its own page:

  1. Book a visit online, by phone or on WhatsApp
  2. Measurement and fabric selection at home, with samples
  3. Quote with fabric, stitching, tracks and installation listed
  4. Order confirmation and advance, if you take one
  5. Stitching or blind manufacturing
  6. Installation and a final check

Say which areas you cover, whether the visit is free or chargeable and whether the charge is adjusted against the order. Give typical timelines only if you can keep to them.

Sample requests and enquiry forms that ask the right questions

A good form saves a phone call. Ask for name, phone, area or pin code, number of windows, rooms involved, curtains or blinds or both, and preferred visit time. Optional fields for photos of windows help you prepare. If the form gets long, split it into steps; see multi-step forms for lead qualification. Add a WhatsApp button for people who would rather send photos straight away; see adding WhatsApp to your website.

Selling curtains and blinds online: WooCommerce or Shopify

Made-to-measure work rarely suits pure online checkout, but some products do: ready-made curtains in standard sizes, curtain rods and accessories, tie-backs, cushion covers and fabric sold by the metre. If you want to sell these online, both platforms can handle it.

Serving interior designers and builders

Interior designers, architects and builders can bring repeat and bulk work. Give them a separate page that explains how you handle trade projects, sample books, site coordination and timelines, with its own enquiry form. Keep any trade pricing private and share it directly. See websites for interior designers and architects to understand what they look for in suppliers.

Care, warranty and after-sales service

Curtains and blinds are used every day for years, so buyers want to know what happens after installation. A short page on care and service builds confidence and cuts down on repeat questions:

  • Care instructions for each fabric family: dry clean or washable, how to handle blackout linings, and how to clean blinds without damaging slats or mechanisms
  • Warranty on stitching, tracks, blind mechanisms and motors, stated separately and only as you actually offer it
  • Service requests: how customers report a jammed track, a loose bracket or a motor problem, and how quickly you usually respond
  • Add-on work: re-stitching, lining old curtains, adding new rooms or replacing fabric while keeping existing tracks

Satisfied customers often come back when they move house or redo another room, so make it easy for them to find you again. A clear service page, a saved WhatsApp chat and a note of their previous order help.

Offices, hotels and commercial projects

Commercial buyers such as offices, clinics, schools, hotels and showrooms have different needs from homeowners: many identical windows, fire-retardant or easy-clean fabrics where specified, consistent finishes across floors and installation outside working hours. If you take on this work, give it its own page with photos of commercial installations, the types of blinds you recommend for workspaces and how you plan large orders. Ask for floor count, number of windows and timelines in the enquiry form, so you can prepare a proper site visit.

Local SEO and speed

Buyers search "curtain shop near me", "blinds in Baner" or "motorised curtains Pune". Keep a complete Google Business Profile (see the Google Business Profile checklist) with showroom and installation photos and genuine reviews. Create separate pages for curtains, blinds and motorised options so each can rank for its own searches, and mention the areas you genuinely serve. Fabric galleries are image-heavy, so compress images, use modern formats and lazy-load long galleries to keep pages fast on mobile.

FAQs

What should a website for a curtain and blinds shop include?

Fabric and blinds catalogues with real photos, room-wise guidance, motorised options, a projects gallery, a measuring and installation page, trade enquiries and a simple booking form with WhatsApp.

Can I sell curtains online in India?

Yes, for standard sizes, accessories and fabric by the metre. Made-to-measure work usually works better as an enquiry followed by a measuring visit.

WooCommerce or Shopify for a curtain store?

WooCommerce suits shops that mix enquiries with online orders and need size-based pricing. Shopify suits stores focused on ready-made products with little technical upkeep.

Should I show curtain prices on my website?

Explain what drives the price, and show prices for ready-made items. For made-to-measure, give the final quote after measurement.

How do I show fabric colours accurately online?

Photograph swatches in daylight without filters, add close-ups and hanging shots, and note that screens vary and samples are available.
