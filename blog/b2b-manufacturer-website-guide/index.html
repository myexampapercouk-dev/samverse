How Manufacturers Can Get More B2B and Export Enquiries From Their Website

A practical guide for Indian manufacturers and industrial suppliers: what buyers look for, how to structure product catalogues, and how to turn your website into an enquiry machine.

Sameer Gupta 27 September 2026 · 3 min read

Key takeaways
  • B2B buyers want to confirm fit, capability and reliability quickly, then request a quote.
  • Organise products the way buyers search, with specifications, datasheets and quote buttons.
  • Show infrastructure and certifications, reply fast, and target specific product keywords.

For many Indian manufacturers, the website is still a digital brochure that nobody updates. Meanwhile, procurement teams and importers research suppliers online before they ever send an RFQ. A well-built website can quietly bring in enquiries every week, from India and abroad.

What B2B buyers look for

A buyer comparing suppliers wants to answer a few questions quickly:

  • Do they make exactly what I need? Clear product categories and specifications.
  • Can they deliver at my scale? Capacity, infrastructure and machinery.
  • Are they reliable? Certifications (ISO, CE, BIS), years in business, client logos.
  • How do I get a quote? A simple, visible way to enquire.

If your website answers these in minutes, you're ahead of most competitors.

Structure your product catalogue properly

  1. Organise by category, the way buyers search (for example "Control Panels", then "PLC Panels" and "MCC Panels").
  2. One page per product or product family, with photos, specifications, applications and available sizes.
  3. Downloadable datasheets and brochures in PDF.
  4. A "Request a Quote" button on every product page, pre-filled with the product name.

Separate product pages also help you rank for specific searches like "stainless steel storage tank manufacturer in Gujarat".

Show your capability

  • Factory and machinery photos and videos
  • Production capacity and quality control process
  • Certifications and test reports
  • Industries served and notable clients (with permission)
  • Case studies of projects delivered

Make enquiring effortless

  • Quote forms that ask for product, quantity and delivery location
  • WhatsApp and email on every page, with a clearly visible phone number
  • Fast replies. Enquiries go cold quickly, so make sure form emails reach the right person.

Selling through dealers? See websites for wholesalers and distributors.

Get found by international buyers

  • Write content in clear English with correct technical terms.
  • Mention export experience, countries served and shipping terms (FOB, CIF).
  • Consider additional languages for key markets.
  • Make sure the site loads fast internationally with good hosting or a CDN.

Selling to buyers in other countries? See multilingual WordPress websites.

SEO basics for manufacturers

  • Target specific product and "manufacturer/supplier in {location}" keywords.
  • Use descriptive page titles and meta descriptions for every product page.
  • Add product and organization schema markup.
  • Keep a consistent presence on IndiaMART, TradeIndia and your Google Business Profile, all linking to your website.

Exporting? Read websites for export businesses for what international buyers look for.

The bottom line

You don't need a flashy website. You need a clear, fast, well-organised one that proves you can deliver and makes it easy to ask for a quote. For most manufacturers, that single change turns the website from a cost into a steady source of enquiries.
