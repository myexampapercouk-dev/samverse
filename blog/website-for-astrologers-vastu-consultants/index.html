Website for Astrologers, Numerologists and Vastu Consultants: Consultation Types, Booking and Payments, Honest Claims, Hindi and Regional Content, WhatsApp and Reviews

Plan a website for an astrologer or vastu consultant: consultation types, booking and online payment, honest claims, Hindi content and real reviews.

Key takeaways
  • Treat each consultation as a product with a format, duration, fee, what the client must send beforehand and what they receive afterwards.
  • Avoid guaranteed outcomes such as assured marriage, cures or business success; describe what a consultation involves and let clients judge.
  • Content in Hindi or your regional language, evergreen guides rather than thin daily horoscopes, and genuine reviews build more trust than volume.

A website for an astrologer, numerologist or vastu consultant should make three things easy: understanding which consultation to book, booking and paying for it without a long chat, and trusting that you are a serious practitioner. That means clear service pages with fees and formats, a simple booking and payment flow, wording that avoids guaranteed results, and content in the language your clients think in.

The structure is similar to the websites I build for consultants and professionals: a profile, a page per service, a consultation request route and local SEO. For consultations promoted through ads, such as a matchmaking or new-home vastu campaign, a focused landing page with tracking set up converts better than sending the click to your homepage.

What you sell is a consultation, so define each one

Visitors often arrive unsure whether they need a kundli reading, a matchmaking session or a numerology report. Define each service so they can choose:

ServiceFormatClient sends beforehandClient receives
Birth chart readingVideo or phone call, or in personDate, time and place of birthThe session, and a written summary if you offer one
Kundli matching for marriageCall or written reportBirth details of both peopleCompatibility discussion or report
NumerologyCall or written reportFull name and date of birthReport or session, with name spelling suggestions if offered
MuhuratCall, message or written noteThe event, location and possible date rangeSuggested dates and times
Vastu for home or officeSite visit or remote reviewFloor plan, orientation, photosVisit or video review, with written recommendations

For each service page, add the duration, the fee, the languages you consult in, whether follow-up questions are included, and how long a written report takes to prepare, stated as a typical range rather than a promise.

Vastu site visits

For home and office visits, list the cities you cover, how travel is charged outside them, and what the client should have ready: the floor plan, the compass orientation, and access to every room. A remote option, with plans and a video walkthrough, extends your reach to clients in other cities and abroad.

Booking and payment, step by step

Many practitioners still take bookings through long WhatsApp threads. A website can do most of that work:

  1. The client chooses a service on its page and clicks "Book".
  2. They pick a date and time from your available slots, with the time zone shown for clients abroad.
  3. They enter their name, phone, email and the details that service needs, such as birth details.
  4. They pay through a payment gateway by UPI, card or net banking.
  5. A confirmation by email and WhatsApp states the time, how the call will happen and the rescheduling policy.

A booking plugin connected to your calendar can run slots; online appointment booking for websites compares the options. For gateways, payment gateways in India compared covers fees, settlement and onboarding. Some payment providers review certain business categories more closely during onboarding, so check with the provider before you build the checkout around it.

Publish a clear policy for cancellations, rescheduling and refunds, and say what happens if the client misses the call.

Claims: describe, do not guarantee

This is the area where astrology and vastu websites most often get into trouble. India has consumer protection law and advertising standards that cover misleading claims, and some laws prohibit advertising remedies for certain conditions. The details are for a lawyer, and the rules change, but the practical line is clear enough: describe what a consultation involves, and do not promise a result.

AvoidWrite instead
"Guaranteed marriage within six months""A kundli matching session covers compatibility factors and what to consider"
"100% accurate predictions""Readings based on the Vedic system, explained in plain language"
"Cure illness with gemstones""Gemstone suggestions are traditional guidance, not medical treatment"
"Business success assured with vastu""Recommendations for layout and placement, based on vastu principles"

Add a short note on service pages that consultations are traditional guidance and do not replace medical, legal or financial advice. If you sell gemstones, yantras or remedies, describe them factually, with prices, and avoid health or wealth claims. Take legal advice on wording if you are unsure.

About page: background without inflation

Clients want to know who they are speaking to: where you learned, which tradition or system you follow, the languages you consult in, and how you work. Write it in your own voice. Avoid titles and awards you cannot document, and avoid counting consultations or clients unless you keep a record. A photo of you, rather than a stock image of a zodiac wheel, helps more than most people expect.

Hindi and regional language content

Many clients search in Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Tamil, Bengali or another language, or type Hindi words in English letters, such as "kundli milan" or "grah dosh". A site with key pages in the languages you consult in can reach those searches directly. Put each language on its own URL so search engines can show the right one, and have the text written or checked by a fluent speaker rather than machine-translated. Hindi and English WordPress websites explains the setup. Choose a Devanagari font that stays readable at small sizes on phones and loads quickly.

Horoscope content that is worth reading

Daily horoscopes for twelve signs are everywhere, and twelve short posts a day quickly fill a site with near-identical pages. That kind of content rarely helps a small practice. Better options:

  • Evergreen guides: what a kundli shows, how matchmaking works, what manglik means, vastu basics for a new flat
  • Monthly or yearly overviews for each sign, written with care and updated each period
  • Articles around festivals, eclipses and planetary transits, published ahead of time and kept for next year
  • Answers to questions clients ask you repeatedly, written as articles

Each article can link to the related consultation. Keep the tone informative, with the same care about claims as the service pages.

WhatsApp, used deliberately

WhatsApp is how many clients prefer to ask a first question. Put a WhatsApp link on each service page that opens a chat already mentioning that service, for example a kundli reading, and decide whether you will answer questions free on WhatsApp or direct people to book. Broadcast messages should go only to clients who have agreed to receive them. WhatsApp marketing for small businesses covers consent and frequency.

Reviews, handled honestly

Reviews matter in this field because clients cannot judge quality in advance. Ask satisfied clients to leave a review on your Google Business Profile, show genuine reviews on the site with the client's permission, and never write, buy or edit reviews. Reviews describing specific predictions or results can read as claims, so choose ones that talk about how the consultation felt and how clearly things were explained. Showing Google reviews on a website explains the options.

Privacy for birth details and floor plans

Birth details, family information and home floor plans are personal data, and a floor plan also reveals the layout of someone's home. Collect only what each service needs, keep files out of public folders, delete them after an agreed period, and explain this in a privacy policy. A consent line on the booking form covers what India's data protection law expects at the point of collection.

Being found in your city

Many clients still prefer someone local, especially for vastu visits and in-person readings. A Google Business Profile with your exact name, address or service area, hours and phone, matching the website, helps you appear in map results. Add a page for each city where you make site visits only if you genuinely work there, with travel terms and local details, rather than copying one page and changing the city name. The Google Business Profile checklist walks through the setup.

FAQs

Can I take payment for consultations on my website?

Yes. A booking plugin with a payment gateway can take payment when the slot is booked. Check the gateway's onboarding requirements for your business category first.

Is it legal to promise results on an astrology website?

Promising a guaranteed outcome risks breaching consumer protection law and advertising codes. Describe the consultation instead, and take legal advice on any claims you want to make.

Should I post daily horoscopes?

Only if you can write them properly every day. Evergreen guides, monthly overviews and festival articles usually serve a small practice better.

Do I need the website in Hindi?

If many of your clients search or consult in Hindi or a regional language, key pages in that language help. Keep each language on its own URL.

Can clients abroad book consultations?

Yes. Show slot times in the client's time zone, accept international cards through your gateway if it supports them, and confirm how the call will be made.
