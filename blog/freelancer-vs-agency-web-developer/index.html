Hiring a Web Developer: Freelancer vs Agency (and 10 Questions to Ask)

Should you hire a freelance web developer or an agency? Compare cost, communication and risk, and use these 10 questions to choose the right developer for your website.

Sameer Gupta 27 September 2026 · 3 min read

Key takeaways
  • Freelancers usually cost less and offer direct communication; agencies suit large, multi-discipline projects.
  • Always check live work, what the quote includes, ownership and post-launch support.
  • Red flags: no portfolio, vague quotes, and refusing to give you admin or hosting access.

Choosing who builds your website matters as much as the website itself. The right developer delivers on time, communicates clearly and supports you after launch. The wrong one can leave you with a half-finished site and no way to reach them.

Freelancer vs agency at a glance

FreelancerAgency
CostUsually lower. No agency overheads.Higher. You pay for a team and management.
CommunicationDirect with the person building your siteThrough an account manager
SkillsDeep in their specialtyWider range: design, content, ads
CapacityBest for small to mid-size projectsBetter for very large, multi-discipline projects
FlexibilityHighMore process, less flexibility

When a freelancer is the better choice

  • You need a business website, landing page, store or redesign
  • You want to talk directly to the person doing the work
  • You want good quality without agency pricing
  • You need ongoing maintenance from someone who knows your site

When an agency is the better choice

  • You need branding, copywriting, ads and development all managed together
  • The project is very large, with many stakeholders
  • You need a big team working in parallel

Interestingly, many agencies outsource development to trusted freelancers, so hiring the freelancer directly can get you the same quality for less.

Some developers will suggest a custom-coded site instead of WordPress; see WordPress vs custom-coded websites to judge which you need.

10 questions to ask before hiring any web developer

  1. Can I see live websites you've built? Visit them on your phone. Are they fast and easy to use?
  2. What exactly is included in the quote? Pages, revisions, forms, SEO basics, training.
  3. What's the timeline, and what do you need from me?
  4. Will I own the website and have admin access? The answer should be yes.
  5. Which theme or builder will you use, and can I edit it myself?
  6. Will the site be mobile-friendly and fast? Ask how they test it.
  7. Do you handle basic SEO? Titles, descriptions, sitemap, Search Console.
  8. What happens after launch? Support period, maintenance options and costs.
  9. What are the payment terms? A common structure is 50% upfront and 50% before launch.
  10. How will we communicate? WhatsApp, email, calls, and how quickly they respond.

Already stuck with a developer who won't hand over access? See how to regain control of your website.

Red flags

  • No portfolio of live sites
  • Prices that seem too good to be true
  • Refusing to give you admin access or hosting details
  • Vague quotes with no list of what's included
  • Slow or unclear communication before you've even started

The bottom line

For most small and medium businesses, an experienced freelance WordPress developer offers the best balance of quality, cost and direct communication. Check their live work, ask the questions above, and get the scope in writing before you start.
