SEO for a New Website in India: What to Do in the First 90 Days (and What to Skip)

Key takeaways
  • A new website first needs to be indexable: submit the sitemap in Search Console, check robots settings and fix any pages Google excludes.
  • Start with specific service, location and question pages that match how Indian customers search, not broad head terms you cannot win yet.
  • New domains take months to build trust, so judge progress by impressions and indexed pages first, then by clicks and enquiries.

SEO for a new website starts with making sure Google can find and index every important page, then building a small set of specific pages that match real searches, such as a service in your city or a question your customers ask. Rankings for a new domain take months, not days, so the first 90 days are about foundations, local visibility and early signs in Search Console.

Here is the plan I follow when a client in India launches a new site, whether it is a WordPress service business or a Shopify store.

Step one: make sure Google can find and index it

Many new sites launch with indexing blocked by accident. Check these on launch day:

  • In WordPress, Settings > Reading: "Discourage search engines" must be unticked.
  • The robots.txt file should not block the whole site.
  • Add the site to Google Search Console and verify the domain.
  • Submit the XML sitemap and confirm Google can read it.
  • Use URL Inspection on the homepage and key service pages and request indexing.
  • Make sure only one version of the site loads (https, with or without www), and the others redirect to it.

Step-by-step help: setting up Google Analytics and Search Console, which also covers submitting your sitemap.

Why a new domain takes time to rank

Google has no history for a new domain: no links pointing to it, no record of how people engage with it, no sign that it is a real, active business. Until those signals build up, even well-written pages tend to sit on page three or beyond. That is normal. What you control is giving Google clear, useful pages to index and making the business easy to verify through consistent name, address and phone details.

A 90-day SEO plan for a new website

PeriodFocusWhat to check
Week 1Indexing, Search Console, analytics, Google Business ProfileSitemap read, homepage indexed, profile verified
Weeks 2 to 4Core service and location pages, titles and descriptionsPages indexed, first impressions appearing
Month 2Question-based articles, internal links, first directory and partner linksQueries you rank for, even on low positions
Month 3Improve pages that show impressions, add FAQs, ask clients for reviewsPositions moving up, first clicks and enquiries

Treat this as a sequence, not a deadline. Some sites move faster, many move slower, depending on competition and the authority you can earn.

Which pages to build first

Start where search intent is clear and competition is beatable:

  1. One page per main service, written for the way customers phrase it ("GST registration consultant", not "compliance solutions").
  2. Location pages only for areas you genuinely serve, each with real local detail. Thin pages that swap city names harm more than they help.
  3. An honest about page and a contact page with full details, which support trust.
  4. Answers to buying questions: cost factors, timelines, comparisons, what to prepare.

Keyword research for small businesses and long-tail keywords show how to find those phrases.

Quick wins that don't depend on domain age

  • Google Business Profile: for local businesses, this can bring calls well before the website ranks. Follow the Google Business Profile checklist.
  • Clear titles and descriptions: one main topic per page, with the searcher's wording.
  • Speed on mobile: Indian visitors often browse on mid-range phones and variable mobile data.
  • Internal links: link from the homepage and articles to the service pages you want to rank.
  • Schema markup: LocalBusiness or Organization details help Google understand who you are.

Local SEO for a new business website in an Indian city

Most new business sites in India win their first customers from local searches: "CA in Kothrud", "interior designer near me", "AC repair Wakad". Local results depend heavily on your Google Business Profile, your reviews and consistent details across the web, which is why a new site can get calls from the map pack long before it ranks in the normal results.

  • Use exactly the same business name, address and phone number on the website, the profile and directories such as Justdial or IndiaMART where relevant.
  • Mention the areas you serve naturally in service pages, with landmarks or neighbourhoods people actually use.
  • Embed a map and add opening hours on the contact page.
  • Ask every satisfied customer for a Google review, and reply to each one.
  • Add a few genuine photos of your team, office or work to the profile each month.

The local SEO guide for Indian small businesses goes into each of these.

Write content that shows real experience

Google increasingly rewards pages that clearly come from people who do the work. A new site cannot fake history, but it can show expertise in every page:

  • Explain your process step by step, including what can go wrong and how you handle it.
  • Use examples from the type of work you do, without naming clients who have not agreed to it.
  • Answer the awkward questions buyers ask on the phone, such as what affects the price or how long it really takes.
  • Add photos of real work instead of stock images wherever possible.

A good starting list of topics is simply the questions your team answered in the last month. Each one is a page that a real customer has already searched for in some form.

Early links from real relationships

Skip bought link packages. They waste money and can trigger problems. Instead, collect links that a real business naturally earns:

  • Suppliers, partners and distributors who list you on their site.
  • Your trade association, chamber of commerce or industry directory.
  • Local news or community sites when you do something genuinely newsworthy.
  • Clients who are happy to mention you, and reviews on Google that mention your brand.

Shopify and WooCommerce stores: extra steps

New online stores have specific SEO issues: duplicate product URLs, thin collection pages and copied supplier descriptions. Write your own descriptions for top products, add an intro to each main collection, and connect Google Merchant Center so products can appear in shopping results without waiting for organic rankings. See the Shopify SEO checklist or the WooCommerce SEO guide.

What to measure in the first months

In the first weeks, clicks will be low. Look at leading signs in Search Console instead:

  • Indexed pages, and whether any important page is "Crawled, currently not indexed".
  • Impressions growing week on week.
  • New queries appearing, especially those in positions 10 to 30, which are your best pages to improve.
  • Enquiries and calls, tagged by source if you can.

The page indexing errors guide helps when pages are excluded.

Review these numbers once a fortnight rather than every morning. Early data jumps around, and reacting to each dip by rewriting titles or moving pages usually resets the progress you were making. Keep a simple log of what you changed and when, so you can link later movements to real causes.

Mistakes that slow new sites down

  • Launching with indexing blocked, or leaving a staging copy indexed.
  • Dozens of near-identical city pages.
  • Publishing many short AI-written posts with no real experience in them.
  • Changing URLs repeatedly without redirects.
  • Chasing a broad national keyword in month one instead of local and specific searches.

FAQs

How long does SEO take for a new website?

Expect several months before steady rankings for competitive terms. Local and very specific searches can show results sooner, especially with a strong Google Business Profile.

What is the first step in SEO for a new website?

Make sure it can be indexed: remove any search engine block, verify Search Console, submit the sitemap and request indexing for key pages.

Should I write blog posts on a new website?

Yes, if they answer real questions your buyers ask and link to your service pages. A few useful posts beat many thin ones.

Do I need backlinks for a new website?

They help build trust over time. Start with genuine links from partners, associations and directories rather than paid packages.

Why is my new website not showing on Google?

It may be blocked from indexing, not yet crawled, or ranking too low to notice. Check URL Inspection in Search Console first.
