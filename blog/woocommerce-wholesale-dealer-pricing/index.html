WooCommerce Wholesale Pricing for Dealers and Distributors: A Setup Guide for Indian B2B Stores

Set up WooCommerce wholesale pricing for dealers in India: user roles, price tiers, MOQs, hidden prices, GST invoices, credit terms and testing.

Key takeaways
  • Write your pricing rules down first: who qualifies, which tiers exist, minimum quantities and how retail and trade customers differ.
  • Use user roles and an approval step so only verified dealers see trade prices, and capture GST details at registration.
  • Test every role, tier, coupon and invoice on staging, because pricing errors in a B2B store are expensive and easy to miss.

WooCommerce wholesale pricing lets one store show retail prices to the public and different prices to approved dealers, distributors or bulk buyers. The usual setup uses customer roles (such as dealer or distributor), an approval step at registration, price tiers by role or quantity, minimum order rules, and GST-ready invoices. It can be done with a wholesale plugin or custom code, depending on how complex your rules are.

This guide covers how I set it up for Indian manufacturers, distributors and brands that sell to both consumers and trade.

Write down your pricing rules first

Most wholesale projects go wrong because the rules live in someone's head or in old price lists. Before touching the store, answer these in writing:

  • Which customer types exist: retail, dealer, distributor, institutional, export?
  • How is each type's price worked out: a percentage off retail, a fixed price list, or quantity slabs?
  • Do some products have no trade discount, or special prices for specific dealers?
  • What are the minimum order quantities, pack sizes or order values?
  • Who can buy on credit, and on what terms?
  • Should retail customers see prices at all, or only "request a quote"?

User roles and dealer registration

Trade prices should only appear for verified buyers. A typical flow:

  1. A "Dealer registration" form asking for business name, GST number, address, contact person and business type.
  2. New registrations go into a pending state; your team checks the details.
  3. On approval, the account gets the right role (dealer, distributor) and the buyer gets an email.
  4. Prices, minimums and payment options update automatically when they log in.

Keep the registration form short and ask for documents only if you really check them. Explain how long approval takes, and give a WhatsApp or phone contact for urgent orders.

Decide who in your team approves accounts and how quickly. A dealer who registers and hears nothing for a week will go back to ordering by phone. A daily check of pending registrations, plus an email alert to the sales person for that region, keeps the process moving. Reject or hold doubtful applications politely with a reason.

When tiers depend on contracts, formulas or data from another system, plugins can run out of options; see WooCommerce custom pricing plugin development.

Setting up price tiers

Wholesale plugins usually let you set a price per role on each product or variation, or a global discount by role and category. Things to decide:

  • Whether trade prices are entered including or excluding GST, and stay consistent with your store's tax settings.
  • How variations are priced (each size or pack can have its own trade price).
  • Whether retail sale prices and coupons apply to trade buyers. Usually they should not.
  • How prices are updated: a bulk CSV import is far easier than editing hundreds of products.

Variation setup is covered in WooCommerce product variations, and discount rules in coupons and discounts.

Minimum quantities, pack sizes and order values

  • Minimum quantity per product: a dealer must order at least a set number.
  • Pack multiples: quantities must be in steps, such as a box or carton.
  • Minimum order value: the cart total must reach a level before checkout.

Show these rules on the product page and in the cart, with a clear message such as "Order in multiples of 12", not just an error at checkout.

Hiding prices from the public

Some businesses do not want retail visitors or competitors to see trade prices. Options:

  • Show retail prices to everyone and trade prices only after login (the most common).
  • Hide all prices until login, with a "Login to see prices" or "Become a dealer" button.
  • Make some categories visible only to trade accounts.

Whichever you choose, keep the dealer login link visible in the header so returning buyers find it instantly. Hidden prices reduce casual sales and can affect product listings on Google Shopping, so choose deliberately.

GST, invoices and payment terms

B2B buyers in India usually need tax invoices with their GSTIN to claim input credit. Make sure:

  • The GST number is captured at registration or checkout and printed on the invoice.
  • CGST and SGST or IGST are applied correctly by place of supply, as your CA advises.
  • HSN codes are on each product and appear on invoices.
  • Invoice numbering follows the series your accountant wants.

For payment, approved accounts may get extra options: bank transfer, cheque, or credit with a set period. Keep credit limited to accounts your team has approved, and record terms clearly. Details in WooCommerce GST invoices in India.

Quick ordering and reorders for dealers

Dealers order often and know exactly what they want. Make their experience faster than a phone call:

  • A quick order table: search or list products by SKU, enter quantities, add all to cart.
  • "Reorder" from past orders.
  • Saved addresses for multiple branches or godowns.
  • Downloadable price lists or catalogues for approved accounts.

Order handling after checkout is covered in WooCommerce order management workflow.

Schemes, festive offers and dealer margins

Indian distribution often runs on schemes: a free unit with a set quantity, a target-based bonus, or a festive discount for a few weeks. Before you try to build these into the store, decide which ones really need to be automatic:

  • Buy X get Y offers can usually be handled by a discount rules plugin, limited to trade roles.
  • Time-bound scheme prices work well as scheduled sale prices for a role, with start and end dates set in advance.
  • Target or turnover bonuses are usually better handled as credit notes by your accounts team than as live website logic.

Show active schemes on a dealer dashboard page so dealers do not have to ask on the phone. Keep retail offers and trade schemes separate, or a public coupon may stack with a dealer price and sell below cost.

Retail and wholesale in one store, or two?

OptionAdvantagesDrawbacks
One store with rolesOne catalogue, one stock count, one adminPricing rules must be tested carefully
Separate B2B portalClean separation, different design and checkoutTwo systems to maintain and keep in sync

For most small and mid-sized businesses, one store with roles is simpler to run. A separate portal makes sense when trade ordering works very differently, for example with no online payment at all, or when the trade catalogue differs heavily from retail.

If you also sell on Shopify, note that trade pricing there is handled through apps or higher plans; compare the two platforms in WooCommerce vs Shopify in India.

Plugin or custom code, and testing

A good wholesale plugin covers most needs: roles, prices, minimums and registration. Custom code makes sense when you have customer-specific price lists synced from ERP or billing software, complex scheme rules, or very large catalogues where plugin performance suffers. Read plugin vs custom code before deciding.

Whichever route, test on a staging copy with one test account per role:

  1. Log in as each role and check prices on simple and variable products.
  2. Check minimums and pack multiples in the cart.
  3. Confirm coupons and sales behave as intended for each role.
  4. Place test orders and inspect invoices for GST, HSN and buyer details.
  5. Check that a logged-out visitor sees only what they should.

FAQs

How do I set up wholesale pricing in WooCommerce?

Create customer roles, add a dealer registration with approval, set prices per role or quantity using a wholesale plugin, then test every role on staging.

Can WooCommerce show different prices to dealers and retail customers?

Yes. Prices can change by user role, so approved dealers see trade prices after login while the public sees retail prices.

How do I hide prices from non-dealers?

Use a wholesale or catalogue plugin to hide prices until login, with a button to register as a dealer.

Can I set minimum order quantities for dealers?

Yes, per product, as pack multiples, or as a minimum cart value, ideally only for trade roles.

Does WooCommerce support GST invoices for B2B orders?

With an invoice plugin, it can print the buyer's GSTIN, HSN codes and the correct tax split. Confirm the format with your CA.
