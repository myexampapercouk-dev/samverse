Returns and Refunds for WooCommerce Stores in India: Writing the Policy, Processing Refunds, COD Refunds and RTO

How to write a WooCommerce returns and refunds policy for an Indian store: what to cover, where to show it, COD refunds, partial refunds and fewer returns.

Key takeaways
  • A good returns policy states what can be returned or exchanged, the window from delivery, the condition required, how to ask, who pays return shipping, and how and when refunds are paid.
  • Issue refunds from the WooCommerce order screen so stock, totals and emails stay correct; refunds go back automatically only if your gateway plugin supports it.
  • For COD orders, collect bank or UPI details through a secure form, record the transaction reference, and treat RTO parcels separately from customer returns.

A clear returns and refunds policy for an Indian WooCommerce store says which products can be returned or exchanged, within how many days of delivery, in what condition, how customers ask for a return, who pays for return shipping, and how and when refunds are paid, including for cash on delivery orders. Publish it on its own page, link it from the footer, product pages, checkout and order emails, and issue every refund from the WooCommerce order screen so your stock, totals and records stay correct.

Shoppers check the returns policy before buying from a brand they don't know, so it is as much a trust signal as a legal page. When I set up a store through WooCommerce development, payments are tested end to end before launch, and the training call covers how to process orders and issue refunds; the policy wording itself needs your approval and, ideally, your lawyer's check.

This guide covers the policy and the process. For cash on delivery rules and reducing refused parcels, see cash on delivery in WooCommerce.

What the rules expect

India's Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 generally expect online sellers to display clear information about returns, refunds, exchanges, warranties, delivery and shipping, and how customers can raise a complaint, before they buy. The rules also generally don't allow a seller to refuse a return or refund when goods are defective, not as described, or delivered late against the stated schedule. Consumer law, payment network rules and your gateway's terms all change, so check the current position with a lawyer before you publish, and don't copy another store's policy without checking it fits how you work; website terms and conditions explains why copied templates cause problems.

What a WooCommerce returns and refunds policy should cover

Write it in plain language, short enough to read on a phone. Exclusions should be specific and reasonable: perishable food, personalised or custom-made items, and hygiene products are common ones. Sale items can have a different exchange rule, but faulty or wrongly sent items still need putting right.

Many Indian stores ask for an unboxing video for damage claims. Asking for one helps, but making it the only acceptable proof turns away genuine customers who didn't record one; accept clear photos of the parcel and item too.

Where to show the policy

  • A dedicated returns and refunds page, linked in the footer of every page.
  • On product pages, a one-line summary near the Add to cart button, such as "7-day returns, free pickup", linking to the full page. Different rules for some products, such as no returns on personalised items, should be stated on those products.
  • At checkout. On the classic checkout, set your terms page under WooCommerce > Settings > Advanced so shoppers see a terms checkbox; the block checkout has its own terms option in the editor. Link the returns page from the terms or a checkout notice.
  • In order emails, through the additional content or footer text in WooCommerce's email settings; see customising WooCommerce emails.
  • In the My Account area, beside past orders, if you add a return request button there.

Payment gateways commonly check for terms, privacy, refund and contact pages when reviewing a store for activation, so publish them before you apply; payment gateways in India compared covers what else they look at.

How to issue a refund in WooCommerce

  1. Open the order under WooCommerce > Orders and click Refund below the order items.
  2. Enter the quantity being refunded for each item, and any shipping or fees you are refunding. WooCommerce works out the amount, or you can type a different one.
  3. Tick Restock refunded items only if the goods are back and sellable.
  4. Add a reason, which is saved in the order notes.
  5. Choose the refund button. If your gateway plugin supports automatic refunds, a "Refund via" option sends the money back through the gateway. Otherwise, use the manual option, then issue the refund in the gateway dashboard, by bank transfer or by UPI.

A full refund moves the order to Refunded and sends the customer the refunded order email; a partial refund keeps the current status and sends a partial refund email. Test both on staging before launch, because some gateways handle partial refunds differently. If you issue GST invoices, ask your CA how refunds should be recorded, for example through credit notes; several invoice plugins can generate them, and WooCommerce GST setup covers invoices.

Refunds for cash on delivery orders

A COD order has no card or UPI payment to reverse, so the refund goes out by bank transfer or UPI, or as store credit if the customer prefers it.

  • Collect details securely, through a form on your site or a private message, never in a review or a public comment, and confirm the account holder's name before paying.
  • Record the payment. Use the manual refund option in WooCommerce and add the UTR or transaction reference to the order notes.
  • Tell the customer when it has been sent, with the reference, so they can check with their bank.
  • Offer store credit as a choice, not the only option, unless your policy and the rules allow otherwise.

Returns are not RTO

A return is a delivered order the customer sends back. RTO, return to origin, is a parcel that was never delivered, because the customer refused it, wasn't available or the address was wrong, and the courier brings it back to you. Treat them separately:

Customer returnRTO
Was it delivered?YesNo
Order statusRefunded or partially refunded, after inspectionCancelled, or a custom RTO status if you track them
MoneyRefund to the customer, by the method in your policyCOD: nothing collected, nothing to refund. Prepaid: refund according to your stated policy
StockRestock after checking conditionRestock after checking the parcel
What to learnProduct, sizing or description problemsCOD rules, address quality and courier performance

If you deduct shipping from prepaid RTO refunds, say so clearly in your policy and check the current rules first. WooCommerce order management shows how to fit returns, cancellations and failed deliveries into a daily routine.

Running returns smoothly

  • A return request form asking for the order number, item, reason and photos, or a returns plugin that adds a request button to the My Account page.
  • Reverse pickup through your courier or shipping aggregator, where they serve the customer's pin code; see WooCommerce shipping setup for India.
  • Status updates by email or WhatsApp at each step: approved, picked up, received, refunded.
  • Exchanges: WooCommerce has no built-in exchange flow, so either refund and let the customer reorder, create a replacement order at no charge with a note linking the two orders, or use an exchange plugin.
  • One person responsible, with a target time for each step written down.

Reduce returns in the first place

  • Accurate photos and colours, ideally in daylight as well as studio light.
  • Size charts and fit notes beside the size picker.
  • Detailed descriptions and FAQs; see writing product descriptions that sell.
  • Packaging that survives Indian courier networks.
  • A monthly look at return reasons by product, so recurring problems get fixed at the source.

FAQs

Is a returns and refunds policy mandatory for online stores in India?

The e-commerce rules generally expect online sellers to display return, refund, exchange and complaint information clearly, and payment gateways usually check for a refund policy. Ask a lawyer what applies to your business.

How do I refund a COD order in WooCommerce?

Collect the customer's bank or UPI details securely, send the money, then record it with the manual refund option on the order and add the transaction reference to the order notes.

Does WooCommerce send refunds to the customer automatically?

Only if your payment gateway plugin supports automatic refunds. Otherwise WooCommerce records the refund and you send the money from the gateway dashboard or by bank transfer.

How do I issue a partial refund in WooCommerce?

Open the order, click Refund, enter the quantities or amount being refunded, and confirm. The order keeps its status, and the customer receives a partial refund email.

Can I refuse returns on sale items?

You can set a different exchange or return rule for sale items if you state it clearly before purchase, but faulty, damaged or wrongly sent items still need putting right. Check the current rules for your category.

How do I write a WooCommerce returns and refunds policy?

List which products can be returned or exchanged, the time window from delivery, the condition required, how to request a return, who pays return shipping and how and when refunds are paid, including for COD orders. Publish it as a page, link it from the footer, product pages and checkout, and ask a lawyer to check it against current consumer rules.
