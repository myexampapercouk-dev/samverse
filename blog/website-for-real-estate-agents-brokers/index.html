Websites for Real Estate Agents and Property Brokers: Listings Without an IDX Feed, Locality Guides, RERA Agent Registration, WhatsApp Leads and Resale vs New Projects

Plan a real estate agent or broker website in India: listing options without IDX, locality guides, RERA agent details, WhatsApp leads and owner listings.

Key takeaways
  • India has no shared MLS feed of the kind many US agent sites use, so choose a listing method you can keep current: manual entry, spreadsheet import or a CRM feed.
  • Resale brokers and new-project channel partners need different sites; state area measures, RERA numbers and availability precisely on every listing.
  • Show your RERA agent registration where it applies, write locality guides only for areas you work, and make every listing one tap away from a WhatsApp message.

A real estate agent's or broker's website cannot outdo the big property portals on volume, so it has to win on what portals cannot offer: listings that are current and accurate, real knowledge of a few localities, a named and registered person to deal with, and a reply on WhatsApp within minutes. Built around those strengths, your own site turns portal browsers who search your name, and owners looking for an agent, into enquiries.

The tools are the ones I set up on real estate websites for builders and agents: listings you manage from the dashboard with photos, prices and an available or sold status, site-visit forms, brochure downloads, WhatsApp on every page and RERA details where they apply. If you promote a particular project or locality with ads, a focused landing page for that campaign keeps the ad's promise and asks for one thing, a call back or a site visit.

Resale broker or new-project channel partner?

Most Indian agents lean one way or the other, and the website should follow how you actually earn:

Resale and rental brokerNew-project channel partner
Where listings come fromOwners who give you their propertyDevelopers whose projects you are authorised to sell
What buyers need to seeReal photos, exact details, availability, the society and localityProject details, configurations, price sheet, possession timeline, RERA number
Compliance on the pageYour own registration where required, accurate area figuresThe project's RERA number and the state RERA website on every project listing
Main call to actionWhatsApp about this property, or book a viewingBook a site visit, or download the brochure
Content that helpsLocality guides, rental and resale adviceProject comparisons within the area, buying process guides

If you do both, keep them in separate sections. Use developers' renders, logos and brochures only with their permission, and never list a project you are not authorised to sell.

Listings in India: options without an IDX feed

Agent websites in the US often pull listings automatically from a shared MLS database through an IDX feed. India has no national equivalent at the time of writing, so listings have to come from your own records. These are the realistic options:

MethodHow it worksSuits
Manual entry in WordPressEach listing is added from the dashboard with fixed fields, photos and a statusAgents with up to a few dozen live listings
Spreadsheet importListings kept in a sheet are imported in bulk and updated in batchesTeams that already track inventory in Excel or Google Sheets
Feed or embed from a real estate CRMSome CRMs can publish listings to a website through a feed, plugin or embed; check with your providerLarger brokerages already using a CRM for leads
Links to your portal profilesYour site links to your listings on portals rather than repeating themA stopgap, not a replacement for your own listings

Whatever the method, do not copy listings, photos or descriptions from portals or other brokers. Portals' terms forbid it, owners object, and duplicated text does nothing for your search visibility.

What every listing should state

Buyers and tenants filter ruthlessly, and vague listings waste everyone's time. Use the same fields on every listing:

  • Area, with the measure named: carpet area, built-up or super built-up, because figures quoted without the measure cause disputes, and RERA requires carpet area for registered projects
  • Configuration, floor and total floors, facing
  • Price or rent, and for rentals the deposit and monthly maintenance
  • Age of the building and, for resale, whether it has an occupancy certificate as the owner states it
  • Parking, furnishing, lift and power backup
  • Availability: immediate, from a date, or the possession timeline for new projects
  • RERA number for listings in registered projects
  • A property ID that appears in every enquiry

Use real photos taken in good light, mark sold or let properties promptly, and remove them after a short period. A site full of "sold" badges from two years ago looks abandoned.

Locality guides that prove you know the area

Locality knowledge is what an agent sells, and a good guide shows it better than any slogan. For each area you genuinely work in, write about:

  • The kinds of homes available, such as older societies, new towers or independent houses, and who tends to live there
  • Commute options to the main business districts, with the routes residents actually use
  • Schools, hospitals, markets and parks, named
  • Water supply, traffic pinch points and other honest drawbacks residents mention
  • Price and rent trends described in words, or with a cited source and date, rather than figures you cannot support
  • Announced infrastructure such as metro lines or road projects, linked to the official announcement and described as planned until it is built

Link each guide to your current listings in that locality. Writing guides for areas you do not work in, or swapping the area name into the same text, creates doorway pages that help neither buyers nor rankings; local pages done right explains the difference.

RERA registration and other trust details

Under the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, agents who facilitate sales in registered projects generally have to register with the state RERA authority and quote their registration number. How this applies to resale and rental work, and the details of display, differ between states, so confirm with your state authority. Where you hold a registration, show the number and state in the footer and on your profile, and link to the authority's search page so visitors can check it.

Other details that help: your office address with a photo of the frontage, the people in your team with their roles, your GST number if registered, how brokerage is charged for buyers, sellers, tenants and owners, and genuine Google reviews displayed through a widget linked to your profile.

Lead capture that respects buyers

Buyers browse at night and on weekends, mostly on phones, and many prefer WhatsApp to a phone call. Lead capture should fit that:

  • A WhatsApp button on every listing that opens a message with the property ID and title already filled in, so you know exactly what they saw
  • A short form asking for name, phone and preferred viewing time, without forcing an email address
  • A "tell us what you are looking for" form for buyers with no listing in mind, asking locality, budget range, configuration and timeline
  • Brochure downloads in exchange for a phone number only for new projects, where the brochure is genuinely valuable
  • An EMI calculator on listing pages, if your buyers rely on home loans; calculators for lead generation explains how to use one without hiding results behind a form

Pre-filled WhatsApp messages, tracking and business hours are covered in adding WhatsApp to your website.

A form for owners who want an agent

Owners are your supply. Give them a "list your property with us" page explaining how you market a property, how you screen buyers or tenants, how viewings are arranged, and how brokerage works. The form should ask for locality, property type and size, sale or rent, expected price or rent, and the best time to call. Ownership documents can wait until you meet.

A weekly routine that keeps the site believable

An agent's website loses trust faster than most because listings change every week. A short routine, done on the same day each week by the same person, keeps it current:

  1. Mark properties that sold or let during the week, and remove those marked earlier
  2. Add new listings with their own photos, using the fixed fields above
  3. Update prices or rents that owners have changed
  4. Check that every enquiry from the website and WhatsApp received a reply
  5. Reply to new Google reviews and add one fresh photo or post to your profile

For a small brokerage this rarely takes long, and it is what separates an agent's site that brings enquiries from one that only exists.

FAQs

Can an Indian agent's website pull listings automatically like IDX?

There is no national shared listing feed at the time of writing. Listings can be added from the dashboard, imported from a spreadsheet, or fed from a real estate CRM that supports it.

Do real estate agents need to show a RERA number on their website?

Agents dealing in registered projects generally need RERA registration and should quote it, and project listings need the project's RERA number. Rules differ by state, so check with your state authority.

Should brokers show prices on listings?

For resale and rentals, yes where the owner agrees, because buyers filter by budget. For new projects, a starting price shown with the configuration and carpet area tells buyers early whether the project fits their budget.

Can I copy listings from property portals onto my site?

No. Portal terms generally forbid it, owners may object, and duplicated content does not help search visibility. Add your own listings with your own photos.

Is WhatsApp better than a contact form for property enquiries?

Offer both. A WhatsApp button with the property ID pre-filled suits most phone users, and a short form suits those who prefer a call back.
