Websites for Real Estate Agents and Property Brokers

By Sameer Gupta · 27 September 2026 · 3 min read

On this page
  1. Two audiences
  2. For buyers and tenants
  3. For property owners
  4. Show local expertise
  5. Build trust
  6. Local SEO
Key takeaways
  • Serve both buyers/tenants (listings, filters, WhatsApp) and owners (list-your-property).
  • Area guides for localities you genuinely cover show local expertise.
  • Keep listings current and build trust with registration details and reviews.

Property agents and brokers compete with large portals, but they have one big advantage: local knowledge and personal service. A good website shows that advantage and turns it into enquiries from both buyers or tenants and property owners.

Two audiences

  • Buyers and tenants looking for properties in your area
  • Owners and sellers looking for an agent to sell or rent their property

Your website should serve both, with clear paths for each.

For buyers and tenants

  • Listings: photos, price or rent, size, configuration, locality, furnishing, availability
  • Filters: buy or rent, budget, location, property type
  • Quick enquiry: WhatsApp and a short form on every listing
  • Remove sold or rented properties promptly. Outdated listings frustrate visitors.

For property owners

  • A "List your property" form
  • How you market properties and screen buyers or tenants
  • Your fees and process, explained clearly
  • Recent deals in the area (with permission)

Show local expertise

Area guides are your strongest content: schools, transport, markets, typical prices and rents, and upcoming developments for each locality you cover. They attract searchers and prove you know the area. Write them only for places you genuinely work; see local pages without doorway pages.

Build trust

  • Your photo, experience and registration details where applicable (for example RERA agent registration)
  • Client reviews and Google reviews
  • Office address and working hours

Local SEO

  • Target "property dealer in {locality}", "flats for rent in {area}" and "2BHK for sale {locality}"
  • A complete Google Business Profile with photos and reviews
  • Area guides and unique listing descriptions
