Websites for Lawyers, CAs and Consultants: What Clients Look For

By Sameer Gupta · 29 September 2026 · 3 min read

On this page
  1. What potential clients look for
  2. Essential pages
  3. Professional guidelines and tone
  4. Local SEO for professionals
  5. Design tips
  6. Next step

People looking for a lawyer, chartered accountant or consultant are usually worried about a problem such as a dispute, a tax notice or a business decision. They want someone credible, experienced and easy to talk to. Your website is often where they decide whether that's you.

What potential clients look for

  • Expertise in their specific problem. Not just "legal services", but "property disputes" or "GST registration and returns".
  • Credibility: qualifications, experience, memberships and the team behind the firm.
  • Clarity: how the process works and what to expect.
  • Easy contact: a simple way to request a consultation.

Essential pages

Profile / About

Your qualifications, enrolment or membership details, years of practice, areas of focus and a professional photo. For firms, add team profiles.

Practice area or service pages

One page per area (for example company incorporation, income tax, GST, audit, trademark registration, family law). Explain who it's for, common situations you handle, the process, and documents clients typically need. These pages are also how you get found on Google.

Articles and insights

Short, practical articles on common questions (like "What to do after receiving a GST notice") demonstrate expertise, build trust and bring in search traffic over time.

Consultation page

A clear consultation request form, phone and WhatsApp, office address with a map, and working hours.

Professional guidelines and tone

Professions such as law and chartered accountancy have their own rules on how services may be publicised. Keep your website factual and informative: describe your areas of practice and qualifications, avoid exaggerated claims or guarantees of outcomes, and review content against your professional body's current guidelines before publishing.

Local SEO for professionals

  • Mention your city and the areas you serve naturally in titles and content
  • Complete your Google Business Profile and encourage genuine client reviews where appropriate
  • Keep your name, address and phone consistent across directories
  • Answer common local questions in articles

Design tips

  • Clean, calm design with plenty of white space, conveying professionalism, not flashiness
  • Readable typography and a clear menu
  • Fast and mobile-friendly, since many clients search on their phones
  • A privacy policy, and forms that don't ask for sensitive details upfront
