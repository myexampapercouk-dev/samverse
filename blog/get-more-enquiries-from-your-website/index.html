12 Ways to Get More Enquiries From Your Business Website

Getting visitors but few calls? 12 practical ways to turn more website visitors into enquiries, from clear headlines and WhatsApp buttons to speed, trust signals and forms.

Sameer Gupta 27 September 2026 · 3 min read
On this page

  2. Remove friction
  3. Build trust

  5. Where to start
Key takeaways
  • A clear headline and a visible call to action tell visitors what you do and what to do next.
  • WhatsApp, tappable phone numbers and short forms remove friction.
  • Real proof (testimonials, photos, case studies) and service pages turn visitors into enquiries.

Many business websites get visitors but very few enquiries. Usually the problem isn't traffic. The website isn't making it easy or convincing enough for people to get in touch. Here are 12 practical fixes, most of which you can make in a day.

Want a structured approach? See conversion rate optimization basics.

Make it instantly clear what you do

1. Write a headline that says what you do and for whom

"Welcome to our website" wastes your most valuable space. Try something like "Orthopaedic clinic in Pune: same-week appointments" or "Industrial automation panels manufactured in India". Visitors should understand you in five seconds.

2. Put your main call to action above the fold

A clear button like "Get a Free Quote", "Book an Appointment" or "WhatsApp Us" should be visible without scrolling, especially on mobile.

Remove friction

3. Add a WhatsApp button

In India, many people prefer WhatsApp to forms or calls. A floating WhatsApp button with a pre-filled message can noticeably increase enquiries.

4. Make phone numbers tappable

On mobile, visitors should be able to tap your number to call. It's a small fix that removes a big obstacle.

5. Shorten your forms

Every extra field reduces submissions. Ask only for what you need to follow up: usually name, phone and a short message.

6. Speed up your site

Slow pages lose visitors before they see your offer. Compress images, use caching and good hosting, and test on PageSpeed Insights.

Build trust

7. Show real proof

Client logos, testimonials, Google reviews, project photos and case studies reassure visitors that you're genuine and good at what you do.

See how to collect and display testimonials that actually convince people.

8. Use real photos

Photos of your team, office, clinic or factory build more trust than stock images.

9. Answer common questions

An FAQ section handles objections (price, timelines, process) before visitors have to ask, and it helps SEO too.

If your form gets no submissions at all, check that it isn't broken. See why contact forms stop getting enquiries.

Reviews and photos are only part of the picture. For contact details, credentials, policies and security too, work through the website trust signals checklist.

Guide visitors to act

10. Create a page for each service

A dedicated page for each service lets you speak directly to that customer's needs and rank for those specific searches.

11. Repeat your call to action

Add a call to action after each main section, not just at the top and bottom. Visitors decide at different points.

Then turn those numbers into rupees; see how to measure website ROI.

12. Track what works

Set up Google Analytics to track form submissions, WhatsApp clicks and calls. You can't improve what you don't measure.

Not everyone is ready to enquire today. A useful lead magnet or newsletter keeps those visitors in touch.

Where to start

If you do only three things this week: add a WhatsApp button, rewrite your headline, and add testimonials. Those changes alone often make a visible difference to enquiries.
