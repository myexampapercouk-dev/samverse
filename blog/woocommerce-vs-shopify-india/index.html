WooCommerce vs Shopify in India: Costs, Payment Gateways, GST, COD and Shipping Compared for Online Stores

Key takeaways
  • WooCommerce suits Indian stores that want low running costs, only the gateway's fee on payments, flexible COD and GST rules, strong content and full ownership, with a developer handling upkeep.
  • Shopify suits stores that want hosting, security and updates handled, and accept a monthly plan, monthly app fees and any Shopify transaction fee that applies to third-party gateways.
  • Both work with Indian gateways, UPI, COD and couriers such as Shiprocket; the real differences are how features are added, how much you can customise and how easily you can leave.

For most small and growing Indian online stores, WooCommerce is the better fit: there is no platform fee, you pay only your payment gateway's charges, cash on delivery, GST and shipping rules can be set exactly how you work, and the store, data and content are fully yours. Shopify is the better fit if you want a hosted store where hosting, security and updates are handled for you, and you accept a monthly plan, monthly app costs and any Shopify transaction fee that applies to third-party gateways.

I build WooCommerce stores, so read this with that in mind; if Shopify genuinely suits a store better, I say so. Payment gateways, UPI and transaction fees

Both platforms can take UPI, cards, net banking and wallets through Indian gateways. The difference is in the fees and the setup:

  • WooCommerce: install the gateway's official plugin, such as Razorpay, Cashfree, PayU or PhonePe, add your API keys and set up webhooks so paid orders are marked paid even if the shopper closes the browser. WooCommerce adds no fee of its own.
  • Shopify: Indian gateways connect as third-party payment providers. At the time of writing, Shopify's own payment service isn't offered in India, and Shopify has charged its own transaction fee on orders paid through third-party providers, varying by plan. Check Shopify's current pricing page for India before you compare totals.

Gateway fees, settlement times and KYC requirements are separate from the platform; how to choose a payment gateway in India lists what to compare.

Cash on delivery

COD still matters to many Indian shoppers, and refused deliveries cost you shipping both ways. WooCommerce has COD built in, and plugins let you limit it by order value or pin code, add a COD fee and confirm COD orders before dispatch. Shopify supports COD as a manual payment method; COD fees, pin code restrictions and order confirmation usually need apps. Offering COD without losing money covers the rules worth setting on either platform.

GST and invoices

On WooCommerce, each GST rate gets its own tax class, tax is split into CGST and SGST or charged as IGST based on the shipping state, and an invoice plugin adds your GSTIN, HSN codes and invoice numbering. Shopify handles GST through its tax settings and relies on invoice apps for GST-format invoices. Either way, ask your CA to confirm the rates and invoice format; WooCommerce GST setup shows each step.

Shipping and couriers

Shipping aggregators such as Shiprocket offer both a WooCommerce plugin and a Shopify app, so you can generate labels, compare couriers and send tracking updates on either platform. Both support flat, weight-based and free-above-a-threshold rates. WooCommerce gives more control over unusual rules, such as different rates by pin code range or product type, and pin code delivery checkers on product pages. Shopify apps run on the app maker's side and are usually billed monthly. WooCommerce plugins run on your own hosting and are often free or billed yearly, but every plugin adds code to maintain, so a store with forty plugins becomes slow and fragile. Choose a few well-maintained ones.

Design, checkout and customisation

Shopify themes look good out of the box, and its checkout is fast and familiar, but deeper checkout changes are limited to its higher plans. WooCommerce lets a developer change almost anything: checkout fields, product configurators, B2B pricing, booking-style products or a shop that sits alongside other features. For Southern Flavours, a restaurant in the UK, I built a WooCommerce shop with more than 100 dishes in 13 categories alongside a table reservation form, all on one WordPress site.

If you choose Shopify and want more than the theme settings offer, see how Shopify theme customisation works, from sections and metafields to safe code changes on a duplicate theme.

Content, SEO and speed

Shopify's URLs always include /products/ and /collections/, and its blog is basic. WooCommerce gives full control over URLs, schema and as much content as you want, which helps stores that win customers through buying guides and recipes. Shopify's hosting is fast by default; WooCommerce can be just as fast on good hosting with caching and optimised images, and cheap hosting is the main reason WooCommerce stores feel slow.

Ownership and moving later

A WooCommerce store is yours: files and database can move to any host, and any WordPress developer can take over. Shopify lets you export products, customers and orders as CSV files, but the theme and apps stay behind, so leaving means rebuilding the design. If you're already on Shopify and the fees or limits are pinching, see moving from Shopify to WooCommerce.

Which should you choose?

  • Small or growing D2C brand watching costs: WooCommerce, with a good host, an Indian gateway and a fast theme.
  • Store that relies on content, SEO or buying guides: WooCommerce.
  • Store needing unusual rules: dealer pricing, configurators, pin code logic or bookings point to WooCommerce.
  • Brand with nobody technical and no developer: Shopify, if you accept the plan, app and transaction costs.
  • Already have a WordPress website: WooCommerce adds a store without a second platform.

FAQs

Is WooCommerce cheaper than Shopify in India?

Usually, over time. WooCommerce has no platform fee or transaction fee, so you pay for hosting, a domain, any premium plugins and optional maintenance. Shopify has a monthly plan, monthly apps and possibly its own transaction fee. Shopify can be cheaper if you'd otherwise pay a developer for every small change.

Does Shopify charge transaction fees in India?

Shopify has charged its own transaction fee on orders paid through third-party gateways, at a rate that depends on the plan, and its own payment service isn't offered in India at the time of writing. Check Shopify's current India pricing page, as this changes.

Can I use Razorpay with both WooCommerce and Shopify?

Yes. Razorpay has an official WooCommerce plugin and connects to Shopify as a third-party payment provider. Cashfree, PayU and PhonePe can also be used on both, subject to each gateway's approval of your business.

Which is better for cash on delivery?

Both support COD. WooCommerce makes it easier to limit COD by order value or pin code, add a COD fee and confirm orders before dispatch, often with free plugins. On Shopify those rules usually need paid apps.

Which is better for SEO?

Both can rank. WooCommerce gives more control over URLs, schema and content, which suits stores that publish guides and category content. Shopify covers the basics with a fixed URL structure and a simpler blog.

Can I move from Shopify to WooCommerce later?

Yes. Products, categories and images move across, along with customers and past orders where exports allow, the design is rebuilt, and old URLs are redirected to protect search visibility.
