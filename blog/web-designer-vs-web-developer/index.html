Web Designer vs Web Developer vs WordPress Developer: What Each Does and Who You Need

Key takeaways
  • A web designer decides how a site looks and how visitors move through it; a web developer makes it work; a WordPress developer is a developer who specialises in WordPress.
  • For a small business site, one person who designs and builds well is often enough; for fixes, speed, security, WooCommerce or custom features you need a developer.
  • Judge either by their live work: designers by how polished and on-brand it looks, developers by how fast, mobile-friendly and reliable the sites are.

A web designer decides how a website looks and how visitors move through it: layout, typography, colours, imagery and the user experience, usually designed in a tool such as Figma. A web developer turns that design into a working website and makes everything function: pages, forms, payments, integrations, speed, security and hosting. A WordPress developer is a web developer who specialises in WordPress. For most small business websites, one person who does both well is enough; you need a developer specifically for fixes, speed, security, online stores and custom features.

What a web designer does

  • Plans the structure of pages and how visitors move between them
  • Designs layouts for desktop, tablet and mobile
  • Chooses typography, colours, imagery and spacing that fit the brand
  • Designs calls to action, forms and navigation so they're easy to use
  • Hands over design files and a simple style guide, or builds the pages in a visual builder

Titles overlap. A UI or UX designer focuses on interfaces and user experience, often for apps as well as websites. A graphic designer works on logos, print and social media, which isn't the same skill as designing a responsive website. And in India, "web designer" is often used for someone who builds complete WordPress sites with Elementor, which is really a mix of design and development. Ask what they actually deliver.

What a web developer does

  • Front-end development: turning designs into responsive pages with HTML, CSS and JavaScript that work on every screen size and browser
  • Back-end development: the server side, such as PHP, databases, APIs, user accounts and payments
  • Integrations: payment gateways, CRMs, booking systems, WhatsApp and email tools
  • Performance and security: fast loading, safe updates, backups, hardening and cleanup after hacks
  • Launch and upkeep: hosting, domains, migrations, monitoring and fixing what breaks

A full-stack developer handles both front end and back end. Many developers work across several technologies; others specialise in one platform.

Where a WordPress developer fits

A WordPress developer specialises in the platform that runs a large share of business websites: themes and child themes, custom plugins, page builders such as Elementor, the block editor, WooCommerce stores, speed optimisation, security and migrations. Compared with a web designer, the difference is mostly in what happens under the surface:

On cost, my WordPress website cost guide puts most professionally built small business sites in India at ₹15,000 to ₹40,000. A fully custom design from a separate designer usually costs more than a developer adapting a theme, because you're paying for two specialists' time.

Can one person do both?

Yes. Many freelancers handle design and development for small and medium business sites, which keeps communication simple and usually costs less than hiring two specialists. The trade-off is depth: someone strong at both is less common than someone excellent at one. For large or complex projects, a designer and developer working together, or an agency, often produces a better result; see freelancer vs agency.

How designers and developers work together

  1. Brief and sitemap: what the site must achieve and which pages it needs
  2. Wireframes: simple layouts that agree what goes where, before any styling
  3. Visual design: desktop and mobile designs, revised with your feedback
  4. Handoff: design files, styles, images and notes on interactions
  5. Development on staging: the developer builds a working version that isn't public yet
  6. Content, testing and launch: real content added, everything tested on phones and browsers, then the site goes live

Handoffs go wrong when designs only exist for desktop or styles are inconsistent. What designers should prepare for a Figma to WordPress build lists what to get right.

How to check a designer's or developer's work

  • For design: is the work polished, varied and suited to each client's brand, and are there mobile designs, not just desktop?
  • For development: open their live sites on your phone. Do they load quickly, work properly and send forms? Run a few through PageSpeed Insights; see speed test tools explained.
  • For both: ask who registers the domain and hosting (it should be you), what happens after launch and who updates the site.

Hiring mistakes to avoid

  • Hiring a designer to fix technical problems. A slow, broken or hacked site needs someone who can read error logs, test plugins and work on the server, whatever their portfolio looks like.
  • Hiring a developer when the real problem is the message. If visitors arrive and don't enquire, the cause may be unclear content or weak calls to action rather than anything technical.
  • Approving desktop designs only. Many visitors to a business site arrive on a phone, so ask to see the mobile design before anything is built.
  • Assuming the designer's files are build-ready. Agree early who prepares images, icons and fonts, and who decides how the design behaves on tablets and small screens.
  • Leaving out who looks after the site. A designer may move on after launch; someone still needs to handle updates, backups and fixes.

FAQs

What is the difference between a web designer and a web developer?

A web designer works on how the site looks and how easy it is to use. A web developer builds the working site and handles its functionality, speed, security and hosting. Many freelancers do both for smaller sites.

Is a WordPress developer the same as a web designer?

No, though they overlap. A WordPress developer builds and maintains WordPress sites, including custom code, WooCommerce, speed and security. A web designer focuses on the look and experience, and may or may not build the site.

Do I need a web designer if I use a WordPress theme?

Not always. A good theme provides a professional starting design, and a developer can adapt it to your brand. A designer is worth it when you want a distinctive look that a theme can't give you.

Can a web developer design a website?

Many can, especially by adapting a well-made theme with your logo, colours and content, which suits most small business sites. For a distinctive custom look, a developer working from a specialist designer's files usually produces a stronger result.

Should I hire a designer or a developer first?

For a custom design, start with the designer, but involve the developer early so the design is practical to build. If you're adapting a theme, you can start with a developer.

Who matters more for SEO, the designer or the developer?

Both play a part. The developer controls technical SEO: speed, clean structure, mobile performance and indexing. The designer affects how easily visitors find information and whether they stay. Content matters as much as either.
