Cash on Delivery on Shopify in India: Setting It Up, COD Fees, Order Confirmation, RTO and Partial Prepaid

How to offer cash on delivery on Shopify in India: enabling COD, fees and limits via apps, confirming orders, and cutting RTO with pin code checks and part prepaid.

You switch on cash on delivery in Shopify by adding it as a manual payment method in the payments settings; each COD order then arrives unpaid and is marked as paid once the courier passes the cash on to you. Charging a COD fee, limiting COD by order value or pin code, or confirming orders over WhatsApp needs an app, because Shopify does not do those things by itself. The bigger job is keeping refused parcels, known as RTO, low enough that COD stays profitable.

Many Indian shoppers still prefer to pay on delivery, especially for a first order from an unfamiliar brand, so most stores offer it in some form. How COD works in Shopify

Shopify treats cash on delivery as a manual payment method, in the same family as bank transfer. That has a few practical effects worth understanding before you switch it on:

  • The order is created straight away with a payment status of pending, and stock is reduced as usual.
  • No money moves at checkout, so there is nothing for a payment provider to approve or decline.
  • Someone has to mark the order as paid after the courier or aggregator remits the cash, which often happens days after delivery.
  • Your sales reports count the order as soon as it is placed, so refused parcels inflate the figures until they are cancelled or refunded.

None of this is a problem once a routine is in place. It only causes trouble when nobody owns the job of reconciling COD remittances against orders.

Setting up COD step by step

  1. In the admin, open the payments settings and find the manual payment methods section.
  2. Add Cash on Delivery. Shopify lets you write a short note shown to the customer after checkout; use it to say what happens next, for example that you may call or message to confirm the order.
  3. Check how the checkout lists COD alongside your online payment provider. Prepaid options should come first if you want to encourage them.
  4. Place a test COD order on a phone and confirm the order email, the invoice and the courier app all handle it as unpaid.
  5. Agree who marks COD orders as paid and how often, using the remittance report from your courier or aggregator.

At the time of writing, this built-in COD is simply on or off for the whole store. Everything finer than that comes from apps or from a checkout tool provided by a logistics or payments company.

COD fees, limits and pin code rules

Three controls make most of the difference to COD costs. Shopify's checkout can be customised by apps through its extension system, but what is allowed depends on your plan and changes as Shopify updates checkout, so treat the app routes below as options to verify rather than guarantees.

ControlWhat it doesHow it is usually done on Shopify
COD feeAdds a charge for paying on deliveryAn app adds the fee as a line item or adjusts the order; check how it shows on the GST invoice
Order value limitHides COD above or below a set amountA payment customisation app that hides the COD option under conditions
Pin code rulesOffers COD only where your courier collects cashA pin code app or the courier's own app checking a serviceability list
Customer rulesWithholds COD from addresses with past refusalsSome COD and checkout apps keep a history; check how the data is stored

A COD fee is the bluntest tool. It discourages casual orders but also puts off genuine first-time buyers, so many stores prefer to offer a small saving for prepaid orders instead. Either way, the fee or discount has to appear correctly on the invoice, which is covered in GST on Shopify.

Pin code rules matter because couriers collect cash in fewer places than they deliver. If a shopper can choose COD for an address your courier cannot collect from, the order fails before it starts. Shopify shipping in India explains how serviceability lists work.

RTO: why COD parcels come back

Return to origin happens when a parcel cannot be delivered and goes back to you. On a COD order you usually pay for the outward journey and the return, collect nothing, and may get the product back damaged. The common causes are:

  • impulse orders the shopper has forgotten or regretted by the time the parcel arrives;
  • wrong or incomplete addresses and phone numbers;
  • the customer not having cash ready, or not being home;
  • long delivery times that give the shopper time to buy elsewhere;
  • fake or prank orders, sometimes several from the same number.

Most of these are caught by checking the order before it ships, keeping delivery quick and making it easy to prepay. Your courier's non-delivery reports also give you a short window to fix an address or arrange a second attempt, which is covered in the shipping guide.

Confirming orders before you ship

A confirmation step costs a few minutes per order and saves the price of a parcel going both ways. Pick the lightest method that works for your volume:

  • A phone call. Fine for a handful of orders a day, and it catches wrong addresses quickly.
  • A WhatsApp message with confirm and cancel buttons. Usually sent by an app using the WhatsApp Business Platform. Orders left unanswered after a set time can be held or cancelled. Check the app's message charges and opt-in rules.
  • An OTP at checkout. Some checkout apps verify the phone number with a one-time code before a COD order is accepted, which stops most fake numbers.
  • An order tag. Whichever method you use, tag confirmed orders so staff only pack those.

Keep the message plain: the items, the total payable in cash, the delivery address and two buttons. A message that looks like marketing is easy to ignore. For more on order messages, see order notifications on SMS and WhatsApp; it is written for WooCommerce, but the message design is the same.

Partial prepaid and nudging shoppers to prepay

Partial prepaid means the customer pays a small part of the order online at checkout and the rest in cash on delivery. Someone who has already paid something is far less likely to refuse the parcel. On Shopify this needs an app or a third-party checkout that can split the payment; check how the split shows in the order, in your gateway reports and on the invoice before you rely on it.

Gentler nudges also help:

  • show UPI as the first payment option, since it is quick on a phone;
  • offer a small prepaid discount or free shipping on prepaid orders only;
  • make the returns policy clear, because uncertainty about returns pushes people towards COD;
  • for a COD order, send a payment link after confirmation as an optional way to pay now.

The WooCommerce guide to COD and RTO goes deeper on the cost side, and the reasoning applies equally to Shopify.

A COD checklist for Shopify stores

  1. COD added as a manual payment method, with a customer note explaining confirmation.
  2. Prepaid options shown first, with any prepaid incentive visible before checkout.
  3. COD limited by order value and to pin codes where your courier collects cash.
  4. Any COD fee or prepaid discount shown correctly on the invoice.
  5. Every COD order confirmed by call, WhatsApp or OTP before packing.
  6. Unconfirmed orders held, then cancelled after a set time.
  7. Non-delivery reports checked daily and acted on within the courier's window.
  8. COD remittances matched to orders, and orders marked as paid, on a fixed day each week.
  9. Refused parcels cancelled in Shopify so stock and reports stay accurate.
  10. Your RTO rate reviewed monthly by pin code and product, and the rules adjusted.

FAQs

Does Shopify support cash on delivery in India?

Yes. COD is added as a manual payment method in the payments settings. Orders arrive with payment pending and are marked as paid once the cash reaches you.

Can I charge a COD fee on Shopify?

Not with the built-in COD option at the time of writing. Apps can add a fee, usually as a line item. Check what your plan allows at checkout and how the fee appears on the GST invoice.

Can I restrict COD to certain pin codes?

Yes, through an app or a courier or checkout app that checks the address against a serviceability list. Shopify's own setting is simply on or off.

How do refunds work for COD orders?

There is no card or UPI payment to reverse, so you refund by bank transfer or UPI to the customer and record the refund in Shopify. Say how this works in your refund policy; writing a returns and refunds policy covers the wording.

Should I stop offering COD to cut RTO?

Usually not completely, because many shoppers will not buy without it. Limiting where and when it is offered, confirming orders and rewarding prepaid tends to work better than switching it off.
