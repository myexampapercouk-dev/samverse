How to Add WhatsApp to Your Business Website: Click-to-Chat Links, Buttons, Tracking and the API

Add WhatsApp Business chat to your website with wa.me links and pre-filled messages, choose between the app and the API, and track WhatsApp clicks in GA4.

Key takeaways
  • A wa.me click-to-chat link with your number in international format and an optional pre-filled message is free, needs no API and works with the WhatsApp Business app.
  • Use page-specific pre-filled messages so you know what each lead wants, and place WhatsApp beside your main calls to action as well as in a small floating button.
  • Track WhatsApp clicks as a GA4 key event and label chats in WhatsApp Business, because a click is a strong signal but not the same as a conversation.

The simplest way to add WhatsApp chat to a business website is a click-to-chat link: https://wa.me/ followed by your full number with the country code (no plus sign, spaces, dashes or leading zero), optionally with a pre-filled message. Put that link behind a "Chat on WhatsApp" button next to your main calls to action and in a small floating button on mobile. It's free, needs no API, and works with the ordinary WhatsApp Business app.

For most small businesses that's all you need. The WhatsApp Business API only matters when a team shares one number, you want automation, or message volumes are high. A WhatsApp button with page-specific messages is included in the websites I build through WordPress website development, and in every landing page alongside the lead form.

Step 1: Build your click-to-chat link

The link format is the same whether you use the free app or the API:

https://wa.me/91XXXXXXXXXX
https://wa.me/91XXXXXXXXXX?text=Hi%2C%20I%20would%20like%20a%20quote
  • 91 is India's country code, followed by the 10-digit mobile number. Leave out the plus sign, spaces and the leading zero, or the link won't work.
  • The message must be URL-encoded: spaces become %20 and a comma becomes %2C. Any free URL encoder does this, or type the message into a link generator.
  • The older format, api.whatsapp.com/send?phone=91XXXXXXXXXX&text=..., still works, but wa.me is shorter.
  • Short links and QR codes: the WhatsApp Business app can generate a short link and QR code from its business tools at the time of writing, which is handy for brochures and shop counters.

As a button in HTML, it looks like this:

<a href="https://wa.me/91XXXXXXXXXX?text=Hi%2C%20I%20need%20a%20quote">Chat on WhatsApp</a>

Step 2: Choose where WhatsApp appears

A floating button

A small WhatsApp icon fixed to the bottom corner of every page is the most common setup and works well on mobile. Make sure it doesn't cover the cookie notice, an "Add to cart" button or form fields on small screens.

Buttons beside your main calls to action

Add "Chat on WhatsApp" next to "Get a Quote" or "Book Now" on the homepage, service pages, product pages, pricing and the contact page. Some visitors will never tap a corner icon but will tap a clear button in the content.

Page-specific pre-filled messages

Different messages per page tell you exactly what each lead wants before you reply, and save the visitor from typing:

PagePre-filled message
HomepageHi, I found you on your website and have a question.
Real estate project pageHi, I'm interested in the 2BHK flats at [project]. Please share the price list.
Clinic treatment pageHi, I'd like to book a consultation for [treatment].
Product pageHi, is [product] available in size M?
Service or pricing pageHi, I'd like a quote for [service].

Form-to-WhatsApp

A short form collects the name and requirement, emails it to you so you have a record, then opens WhatsApp with the details filled in. You keep the lead even if the visitor never presses send. This site's contact form works that way.

Where WhatsApp works especially well

  • Restaurants and cafes: orders, table bookings and catering questions
  • Real estate: price lists, floor plans and site-visit bookings
  • Clinics: appointment timings and directions, while keeping medical details for the consultation
  • Coaching institutes and schools: admission and batch timing questions from parents
  • Online stores: size and stock questions before buying, and confirming cash on delivery orders
  • B2B suppliers: catalogue and price list requests, where a buyer wants a quick answer before a formal quote

How to add it in WordPress

  • Elementor or the block editor: use a Button widget or block and paste the wa.me link into the link field. Use a different message on each page template.
  • A floating button plugin: plugins such as Joinchat and Click to Chat are popular on WordPress.org at the time of writing, with options for per-page messages, opening hours and several team members.
  • A plain link in the theme: a link with a small inline SVG icon adds almost nothing to page weight.

Avoid heavy third-party chat widgets just to show a WhatsApp icon. Some load external scripts on every page and slow the site down; see how third-party scripts slow your website.

WhatsApp Business app vs the WhatsApp Business API

WhatsApp Business appWhatsApp Business Platform (API)
CostFreeMeta charges for many message types, directly or through a provider; check the current rates
SetupInstall on a phoneThrough Meta's Cloud API or a provider, usually with business verification
Team useOne number on a phone plus a few linked devicesMany agents in a shared inbox through a provider's tool
AutomationGreeting and away messages, quick replies, labelsChatbots, CRM connections, automated order and appointment messages
Messaging customers firstManual messages and broadcast listsPre-approved templates; free-form replies within the 24-hour customer service window
Best forMost small businessesTeams, high volumes and automated notifications

The click-to-chat link on your website works the same with either. Moving a number to the API changes how it works in the app, so check what your provider supports before you switch. The app's catalogue, labels and broadcast lists are covered in WhatsApp marketing for small businesses, and automated order updates for stores in order notifications by email, SMS and WhatsApp.

Track WhatsApp clicks in Google Analytics

If you don't track WhatsApp, many of your website enquiries stay invisible in your reports. Two ways to do it:

  1. GA4 on its own: with enhanced measurement's outbound clicks switched on, GA4 records clicks to wa.me as click events with the link address. Create a new event in GA4 (Admin > Data display > Events > Create event, at the time of writing) called whatsapp_click, matching when the event name is click and link_url contains wa.me, then mark it as a key event.
  2. Google Tag Manager: a "Just Links" click trigger where the Click URL contains wa.me fires a GA4 event called whatsapp_click. This also makes it easy to send the same click to Google Ads or Meta as a conversion.

A tap isn't the same as a conversation, because some people open WhatsApp and change their mind. Label chats from the website in WhatsApp Business and compare the two numbers each month. More detail in tracking phone and WhatsApp enquiries and GA4 events explained.

Reply fast and handle leads properly

  • Set a greeting and an away message so people get an instant reply, with your hours in the away message.
  • Save quick replies for prices, location, timings and documents you send often.
  • Use labels such as New lead, Quoted, Follow up and Won, so nothing slips.
  • Only promise a reply time you can keep. WhatsApp creates an expectation of quick answers; see responding to enquiries fast.
  • Ask before adding anyone to a broadcast list. Messaging people who only asked one question annoys them and can get your number reported. India's data protection rules also apply to how you store and use customers' details, so check with your adviser if you collect a lot of personal data.

Common mistakes

  • The number written with a plus sign, spaces or a leading zero, so the link opens an error
  • A floating button covering content, the cookie notice or checkout buttons on phones
  • WhatsApp as the only option, when some visitors prefer a call or a form
  • A staff member's personal number, which leaves with them
  • No tracking, so WhatsApp leads never appear in your reports

If you're also running ads, compare sending people straight to a chat with sending them to a page first; see click-to-WhatsApp ads vs a landing page.

FAQs

How do I add WhatsApp Business chat to my website?

Create a wa.me link with your number in international format and an optional pre-filled message, then add it to a button on your key pages and a floating icon, either in your page builder or with a lightweight plugin. Test it on a phone and a desktop.

What is the wa.me link format?

https://wa.me/ followed by the country code and number with no plus sign, spaces or leading zero, for example https://wa.me/91XXXXXXXXXX. Add ?text= followed by a URL-encoded message to pre-fill the chat.

Is adding WhatsApp to a website free?

Yes. Click-to-chat links and the WhatsApp Business app are free. Costs only come in with the WhatsApp Business API, where Meta charges for many message types, and with paid plugins or chat tools.

Do I need the WhatsApp Business API for my website?

Not for a chat button. Most small businesses do well with the free app and a click-to-chat link. The API makes sense when several staff need to answer from one number, or you want chatbots and automated notifications.

Why does the WhatsApp button open WhatsApp Web on desktop?

On a computer, wa.me links open the WhatsApp desktop app if it's installed, or offer WhatsApp Web, which needs the visitor's phone linked. On mobile they open the app directly. That's normal behaviour, and it's why a form or phone number should sit alongside WhatsApp.

How do I track WhatsApp clicks in Google Analytics?

Create a GA4 event for clicks on links containing wa.me, either from the outbound click events GA4 already collects or with a Google Tag Manager click trigger, and mark it as a key event.
