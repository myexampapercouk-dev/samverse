Website Maintenance Cost in India: What You Pay For and What's Included

How much does website maintenance cost in India, and what should a WordPress maintenance plan include? Typical ranges, what's covered, what's extra and how to choose a plan.

Key takeaways
  • Freelance WordPress maintenance for small sites typically costs about ₹1,500–₹6,000 a month.
  • A good plan covers updates, off-site backups, security, uptime monitoring and small changes.
  • New features, big content uploads and licences are usually extra.

Once your website is live, it needs ongoing care: updates, backups, security and small changes. Many business owners are unsure what maintenance should cost or include. Here's a clear breakdown.

Typical costs

For small and medium WordPress websites in India, freelance maintenance plans typically range from about ₹1,500 to ₹6,000 per month, depending on the size of the site, how often changes are needed, and whether it's an online store. Agencies and large or business-critical sites cost more. These are typical market ranges, not fixed prices.

What a good maintenance plan includes

  • Updates: WordPress core, themes and plugins, applied safely after a backup
  • Backups: automatic, stored off-site, with restores when needed
  • Security: malware scans, firewall and login protection
  • Uptime monitoring: alerts if the site goes down
  • Performance checks: speed monitoring and fixes for regressions
  • Small content changes: text, images and prices, within an agreed amount of time
  • Form and functionality checks: making sure enquiries still arrive
  • Monthly report: what was done and anything you should know

What usually costs extra

  • New pages, new features or redesigns
  • Large content uploads (for example hundreds of products)
  • Premium plugin licences and hosting fees
  • Cleanup of a site that was already hacked before the plan started

Need more than upkeep? See maintenance vs management.

Why maintenance is worth it

  • Prevention is cheaper than repair. Cleaning a hacked site or rebuilding after data loss costs far more than regular upkeep.
  • Lost enquiries are invisible. A broken contact form can cost leads for weeks before anyone notices.
  • Speed and SEO decay. Sites slow down over time without attention.

How to choose a plan

  1. Check exactly what's included, especially how many content changes per month
  2. Ask how updates are tested and how quickly problems are fixed
  3. Confirm backups are stored off-site and that restores are included
  4. Prefer month-to-month plans so you're not locked in

DIY or hand it over?

You can do basic maintenance yourself with our WordPress maintenance checklist. If you'd rather focus on your business, a maintenance plan makes sure it happens every month without you having to remember.
