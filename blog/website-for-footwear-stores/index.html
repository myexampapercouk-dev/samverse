Websites for Shoe and Footwear Stores: Fit, Sizes and Exchanges

Building a website for footwear stores in India? Plan size variants, size charts, exchanges, COD rules and product photos on Shopify or WooCommerce.

Key takeaways
  • Footwear sells online when buyers trust the fit: clear size charts in UK/India sizes, foot-length guidance and honest notes on how each style runs.
  • Plan variants, stock by size and a simple exchange process before launch, on Shopify or WooCommerce, because size swaps are a normal part of the business.
  • COD, pin code checks and fast mobile product pages matter for Indian shoppers, alongside UPI and card payments.

A website for footwear stores has to solve one problem above all: helping shoppers pick the right size without trying the shoes on. That means clear size charts, accurate stock by size, good photos from every angle, and an easy exchange process. Shopify and WooCommerce both handle this well for Indian stores, with UPI, cards and cash on delivery.

This guide is for shoe shops, footwear manufacturers selling direct, and brands making leather shoes, sandals, sports shoes, kolhapuris, juttis or school shoes.

Sizes, size charts and fit guidance

Indian shoppers use a mix of UK, India, EU and US sizes, and different brands run differently. Make your size chart simple and specific:

  • Show UK/India sizes alongside EU and foot length in centimetres.
  • Explain how to measure foot length at home with paper and a ruler.
  • Add a fit note on each product: runs true to size, runs small, or suits wide feet.
  • For kids' shoes, suggest how much room to allow for growth.
  • Mention width where it varies, since this is a common reason for exchanges.

Put a size chart link right next to the size selector on every product page, not only in the footer. If you sell both men's and women's ranges, keep separate charts.

Product variants on Shopify and WooCommerce

Each shoe usually has size and colour options. On Shopify, these are product variants, each with its own SKU, stock and price if needed. Shopify has limits on the number of options and variants per product, so check current limits if you have many sizes and colours, and consider splitting colours into separate products. On WooCommerce, these are variable products with attributes for size and colour; large catalogues need a good setup so variation stock stays accurate and pages stay fast.

Photos that help people choose

Shoppers cannot touch the leather or check the sole, so photos must do it for them. Shoot every style from the side, front, back, top and sole, plus a close-up of material and stitching. A photo on foot helps show how the shoe sits. Use the same background and lighting across the catalogue so the store looks consistent.

Show each colour accurately; colour mismatches cause returns. Compress images and use modern formats so product pages stay fast on mobile; Shopify serves resized images automatically, while WooCommerce stores usually need an image optimisation plugin.

Exchanges, returns and COD

Size exchanges are a normal part of selling shoes online, so design the process rather than treating it as a problem. Write a clear policy: how many days, condition of the shoe and box, who pays reverse shipping, and whether you offer exchange, store credit or refund. Make it visible on product pages and at checkout. The guide to returns and refunds policies explains what to cover.

Cash on delivery remains popular in India, but COD orders that are refused or returned cost you shipping both ways. Common controls include limiting COD by pin code or order value, confirming COD orders by WhatsApp or call, and offering a small incentive for prepaid UPI payments. See the guides for Shopify cash on delivery in India and WooCommerce cash on delivery in India.

Shipping and delivery checks

Shoe boxes are bulky, so check how your courier calculates volumetric weight and set shipping rates accordingly. A pin code delivery checker on the product page tells buyers whether you deliver to them, whether COD is available and roughly when the order will arrive; the guide to pin code delivery checkers explains options for WooCommerce and Shopify. Shopify stores can also see the Shopify shipping in India guide.

SEO for footwear stores

People search for specific styles: men's leather formal shoes, kolhapuri chappals for women, school shoes in black, or running shoes for flat feet. Build category pages around these real groupings and write a short unique introduction for each. On product pages, write your own descriptions covering material, sole, closure, fit and care, rather than copying manufacturer text that appears on many other sites.

If you have a physical shop, link your Google Business Profile and add store details so local shoppers can visit, check sizes in person, or pick up orders.

Manufacturers and wholesale footwear sellers

Many footwear businesses in India, from leather shoe makers to sandal and slipper units, sell both to retailers and to consumers. If you do both, keep the two journeys separate. Retail shoppers need a normal store with sizes, prices and checkout. Wholesale buyers need minimum order quantities, size-set or carton details, trade pricing on request, and a clear enquiry or bulk order form. Mixing them on one product page confuses both.

A common setup is a retail store on Shopify or WooCommerce with a separate trade enquiry page, or a login-only wholesale area where approved buyers see trade prices. For footwear businesses in Agra and nearby, see the WordPress and Shopify developer Agra page.

Seasonal ranges and stock updates

Footwear demand shifts through the year: school shoes before the academic year, ethnic footwear around weddings and festivals, sandals and floaters in summer, and closed shoes in the monsoon and winter. Plan category pages and home page banners around these seasons, and keep the pages live year to year instead of creating new ones each time, so they can build up search visibility.

Stock accuracy matters even more in busy periods. If you also sell in a physical shop or on marketplaces, decide how stock will be kept in sync, whether through a POS that connects to your store, an inventory app, or a regular manual update. Overselling a size you no longer have leads to cancellations and unhappy customers. Mark discontinued styles clearly, and when a style is permanently gone, redirect its page to the closest category rather than leaving a dead page.

Store pickup and try-at-shop options

If you have a physical store, you can offer local pickup at checkout, or let customers reserve a size online and try it at the shop. Both Shopify and WooCommerce can be set up for local pickup. This suits shoppers who are unsure of fit but want to confirm availability before travelling, and it brings people into the store where they may buy more.

Mobile shopping experience

Most footwear browsing happens on phones. Check that product images can be swiped and zoomed, the size buttons are large enough to tap, the size chart opens without leaving the page, and the add-to-cart button stays visible. Keep checkout short, with UPI apps opening smoothly. Test the whole journey on a mid-range Android phone over mobile data, not only on office Wi-Fi.

FAQs

What should a website for footwear stores include?

Size and colour variants with accurate stock, a size chart with fit notes, photos from every angle, size and style filters, a clear exchange policy, COD and pin code checks, and WhatsApp help.

Is Shopify or WooCommerce better for a shoe store in India?

Shopify suits stores that want a hosted, low-maintenance setup. WooCommerce suits stores that want more control and strong content. Both support UPI, cards, COD and size variants.

How do I reduce wrong-size orders for shoes online?

Show a clear size chart next to the size selector, explain how to measure foot length, add fit notes for each style, and offer quick WhatsApp help before ordering.

Should I offer cash on delivery for footwear?

Many Indian shoppers expect it, but control the risk by limiting COD by pin code or value, confirming orders, and encouraging prepaid UPI payments.

How should a shoe store handle exchanges?

Publish a clear policy on time limits, shoe condition and reverse shipping, make it visible before purchase, and use an app or plugin to manage exchange requests.
