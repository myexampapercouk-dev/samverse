Solar Company Website Guide: Turning Visitors Into Quote Requests

By Sameer Gupta · 27 September 2026 · 3 min read

  2. Pages every solar website needs
  3. Build trust quickly

Key takeaways
  • Solar buyers want savings, subsidy, sizing and trust information before requesting a quote.
  • Quote forms should collect location, property type and monthly bill.
  • Project galleries, certifications and local SEO turn research into enquiries.

Homeowners and businesses considering solar do a lot of research before contacting an installer. They want to understand savings, subsidies, costs and whether they can trust you. A website that answers these questions clearly turns that research into quote requests.

What solar buyers want to know

  • How much will I save on my electricity bill?
  • What does it cost, and are there subsidies?
  • What system size do I need?
  • How long does installation take, and what about maintenance?
  • Is this company experienced and reliable?

Pages every solar website needs

1. Clear solution pages

Separate pages for residential rooftop, commercial and industrial solar, and related products like batteries or inverters, written in plain language for customers.

2. Savings and subsidy information

Explain how savings work and the current subsidy schemes and process in your area. Keep this information accurate and updated, as schemes change; link to official sources where possible.

3. Completed projects

A gallery of real installations with system size, location type and photos is one of the strongest trust signals you can offer.

4. A smart quote request form

Ask for the details you need to prepare a proposal: name, phone, location, property type, roof type and monthly electricity bill. Keep it short enough that people complete it.

5. FAQ

Answer questions about installation time, maintenance, warranties, net metering and monsoon performance.

Build trust quickly

  • Certifications, partnerships and brands you install
  • Genuine customer testimonials and Google reviews
  • Your team and service process
  • Warranty and after-sales support details

Get found locally

  • Target searches like "solar panel installation in {city}" with service area pages that have genuinely local content (projects and information for that area)
  • Keep a complete Google Business Profile with project photos
  • Publish helpful articles on savings, subsidies and system sizing

Rental and hire businesses in the power sector have their own needs; see equipment and generator rental websites.

Running ads?

Solar campaigns on Google and Meta work best with dedicated landing pages: one offer, a short quote form, trust signals and WhatsApp. Avoid the common landing page mistakes that waste ad budget.
