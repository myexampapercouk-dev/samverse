Clinic Website Checklist: 15 Things Every Doctor's Website Needs

By Sameer Gupta · 27 September 2026 · 3 min read

On this page

  2. Information patients need
  3. Make contacting you effortless
  4. Technical essentials
  5. Common mistakes to avoid
  6. Your Google Business Profile matters too
  7. Next step

Most patients now search online before choosing a doctor. They look at your qualifications, read reviews, check timings and decide in a few minutes whether to call you or the next clinic on the list. Your website is often the first impression.

Use this checklist to make sure your clinic website builds trust and turns visitors into appointments.

Trust and credibility

  1. A clear doctor profile. Name, photo, qualifications, registration details, specialisations and years of experience.
  2. Real clinic photos. Reception, consultation room and equipment. Real photos build far more trust than stock images.
  3. Patient testimonials. Genuine feedback (with permission) and a link to your Google reviews.
  4. Memberships, awards and affiliations. Hospital associations, professional bodies and recognitions.

Information patients need

  1. Treatments and services pages. A separate page for each major treatment explains it clearly and helps you rank for those searches.
  2. Timings and days. Up to date, including holidays, and easy to change.
  3. Address with Google Maps. Plus parking and landmark directions.
  4. Consultation fees or insurance info, if you're comfortable sharing it. It reduces repetitive calls.

Make contacting you effortless

  1. One-tap call button visible on mobile at all times.
  2. WhatsApp button, the preferred way for many patients to ask questions.
  3. Appointment enquiry form that asks only for what you need: name, phone, preferred date and concern.

Technical essentials

  1. Mobile-first design. Most patients will visit on their phone.
  2. Fast loading. Compress images and use good hosting, because patients won't wait.
  3. Local SEO. Page titles and content that mention your specialty and city, schema markup for your practice, and a linked Google Business Profile.
  4. Security and privacy. HTTPS, a privacy policy, and forms that don't collect more medical information than necessary.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • A single page with no detail about treatments
  • Outdated timings or phone numbers
  • Tiny text and buttons that are hard to tap on phones
  • No clear way to book, or only a phone number in the footer
  • Exaggerated claims. Keep medical content accurate and within professional guidelines.

Your Google Business Profile matters too

For local searches like "dermatologist near me", your Google Business Profile often appears above websites. Keep it complete, add photos, respond to reviews and link it to your website. The two work together.

Doctors who also coach, teach or run programs can go further with a personal brand website. Dental practices should also read what patients look for in a dental clinic website.

Next step

If your current website misses several of these points, a focused redesign can make a big difference to how many patients contact you. A good clinic website is one of the most cost-effective ways to grow a practice.
