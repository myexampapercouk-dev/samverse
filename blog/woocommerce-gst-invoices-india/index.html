WooCommerce GST Setup for Indian Stores: CGST, SGST, IGST, HSN Codes and GST Invoices

WooCommerce GST setup for Indian stores: tax classes, CGST/SGST vs IGST rates, place of supply, HSN codes, GSTIN at checkout and GST invoice plugins.

Key takeaways
  • Enable taxes, calculate tax on the shipping address, create a tax class per GST rate, and add CGST and SGST for your home state with IGST for every other state.
  • WooCommerce has no HSN field, GSTIN checkout field or GST invoice by default, so add them with a maintained invoice plugin or custom fields and test the PDF.
  • Rates, HSN codes, invoice format, shipping tax and e-invoicing depend on your products and turnover, so confirm every setting with your CA before launch.

To set up GST in WooCommerce, enable taxes, calculate tax on the customer's shipping address, create a tax class for each GST rate you charge, add CGST and SGST rates for your home state and IGST for every other state, assign each product its tax class, then add an invoice plugin that prints your GSTIN, HSN codes, the tax split and the place of supply. WooCommerce does the maths once it's configured, but it doesn't know Indian GST rules out of the box.

This guide covers the website side. The rate for each product, HSN codes, invoice format and whether e-invoicing applies depend on your products and turnover, so check every setting with your CA before launch. I set this up for Indian stores as part of my WooCommerce development work, with the CA's figures in hand.

What to get from your CA first

  • Your GSTIN and the state you're registered in
  • The HSN code and current GST rate for each product, or SAC codes for services. Rates have been revised over the years, so don't copy a rate list from an old article.
  • Whether your prices will include GST
  • How to tax shipping charges, COD fees and extras such as gift wrapping
  • Your invoice number format and series
  • Whether e-invoicing applies to your business invoices
  • How to handle international orders and returns

CGST and SGST vs IGST: place of supply

For goods sold online and delivered to the customer, the place of supply is generally where the delivery ends, which is the shipping address. That decides which tax applies:

Your registrationDelivery addressTax chargedExample for an 18% product
MaharashtraMaharashtraCGST + SGST9% CGST + 9% SGST
MaharashtraAny other stateIGST18% IGST
MaharashtraOutside IndiaExport rules applyAsk your CA

The customer pays the same total either way; only the split changes. Services and digital products follow different place-of-supply rules, so if you sell courses or downloads, confirm the approach with your CA.

Step 1: Turn on taxes and set the basics

In WooCommerce > Settings > General, make sure the store address shows the state you're registered in, because WooCommerce treats it as your base location. Tick "Enable tax rates and calculations" and save. A Tax tab then appears with these options:

  • Prices entered with tax: most Indian consumer stores choose "Yes, I will enter prices inclusive of tax", so the price shown is the price paid.
  • Calculate tax based on: "Customer shipping address".
  • Shipping tax class: set this the way your CA advises for shipping charges.
  • Rounding: decide with your CA whether tax is rounded per line or at subtotal, so invoices match your accounts.
  • Additional tax classes: add one per rate you charge, for example "GST 5" and "GST 18". WooCommerce's built-in "Zero rate" class suits exempt or nil-rated products.
  • Display prices in the shop and at checkout including tax, and set tax totals to display "Itemised" so CGST and SGST show separately.

Step 2: Add CGST, SGST and IGST rates

Open each tax class and add three rows. WooCommerce applies only one matching rate per priority, so CGST and SGST need different priorities for both to apply. For a store registered in Maharashtra, the GST 18 class would look like this:

Country  State    Rate %   Tax name  Priority
IN       MH       9.0000   CGST      1
IN       MH       9.0000   SGST      2
IN       (blank)  18.0000  IGST      1
  • Leave the IGST row's state blank so it covers every other state, and keep your home-state rows above it in the table. A customer in Maharashtra then gets CGST and SGST; a customer anywhere else gets IGST.
  • Use the state codes WooCommerce uses, such as MH for Maharashtra and KA for Karnataka.
  • Tick "Shipping" on the rows only if your CA says shipping should carry that tax.
  • Name the rows clearly, because the names appear on orders, emails and invoices.
  • With several rate classes, the "Import CSV" button on each tax class page is quicker than typing rows by hand.

Step 3: Assign a tax class to every product

In each product's Product data box, set Tax status to "Taxable" and choose the Tax class. Variations default to "Same as parent" but can have their own class if a size or pack falls under a different rate; see WooCommerce product variations. To change many products at once, use Bulk actions > Edit on the Products list. If you import products from a spreadsheet, include the tax class column so nothing falls back to the standard rate unnoticed.

Step 4: Add HSN codes

WooCommerce has no HSN field by default. The common options are:

  • An Indian GST invoice plugin that adds an HSN field to products and prints it on invoices
  • A custom field added with a plugin such as Advanced Custom Fields or a few lines of code, which the invoice template then reads
  • A product attribute, which works but is untidy and can show on the product page

How many digits of the HSN code you must show depends on your turnover, so ask your CA.

Step 5: Collect GSTIN from business buyers

If businesses buy from you, add optional "Company name" and "GSTIN" fields at checkout so they can claim input tax credit. A checkout field plugin or a small code snippet does this; if your store uses the newer block-based checkout, check that the method you choose supports it.

  • Validate the format: a GSTIN is 15 characters, starting with a two-digit state code followed by the buyer's 10-character PAN. A format check catches typos but doesn't prove the GSTIN is active.
  • Show the GSTIN on the invoice, the admin order screen and the order emails; see customising WooCommerce order emails.

On Shopify the tax ideas are the same but the tools differ; see GST on Shopify for Indian sellers for tax settings, HSN codes and invoice apps.

Step 6: Generate GST invoices

WooCommerce doesn't create tax invoices on its own. PDF Invoices & Packing Slips for WooCommerce is a widely used free plugin that attaches a PDF invoice to order emails and handles numbering, and there are India-specific plugins that add HSN fields and CGST, SGST and IGST columns. Whichever you choose, check that it's actively maintained and compatible with WooCommerce's newer order storage (HPOS).

A GST invoice typically needs, subject to your CA's confirmation:

  • Your business name, address and GSTIN
  • A unique, consecutive invoice number for the financial year, and the invoice date
  • The customer's name and billing and shipping addresses, plus their GSTIN for business buyers
  • HSN or SAC code, description, quantity and value for each item
  • Taxable value, rate and tax amount, split into CGST and SGST or shown as IGST
  • The place of supply

A common approach is to restart numbering each April with a prefix, for example INV/2026-27/0001. The rules cap invoice numbers at 16 characters at the time of writing, so keep the format short. For refunds and returns, you'll need credit notes, which some invoice plugins offer in their paid versions; see returns and refunds for WooCommerce.

If e-invoicing applies to your turnover, business invoices need an IRN and QR code from the government's e-invoice system. Standard WooCommerce invoice plugins don't do this; it usually runs through your accounting software or a GST service provider.

Shipping, COD fees, discounts and international orders

  • Shipping: how delivery charges are taxed depends on how you sell, so set WooCommerce's shipping tax class the way your CA advises; zones and rates are covered in WooCommerce shipping setup for India.
  • COD fees: if you add a cash on delivery charge, ask whether it's taxable and at what rate; see cash on delivery in WooCommerce.
  • Coupons: WooCommerce applies discounts before tax, so GST is calculated on the discounted price. Check that matches what your CA expects.
  • International orders: exports follow separate rules, and your CA will advise on the paperwork; see selling internationally from an Indian store.

Test before you go live

  1. Place an order to an address in your own state and check the CGST and SGST split.
  2. Place one to another state and check that only IGST appears.
  3. Try a cart with products at two different rates.
  4. Try an order with a coupon, and one with a COD fee if you charge one.
  5. Place a business order with a GSTIN and check it appears on the invoice.
  6. Open each invoice PDF and check every field against your CA's format.
  7. Refund a test order and check the credit note.

These sit alongside the payment and email checks in the WooCommerce launch checklist.

Reports for GST filing

WooCommerce > Analytics > Taxes shows tax collected by rate for any period, and an order export with the shipping state and tax lines gives your accountant what they need for returns. Many invoice plugins can export invoices in bulk. Agree a monthly routine with your CA: export orders and invoices, reconcile them with payment gateway settlements, and keep everything organised by financial year.

FAQs

Does WooCommerce support GST?

Yes, through its standard tax settings. You create tax classes for each GST rate and add CGST, SGST and IGST rates by state. HSN codes, a GSTIN checkout field and GST-format invoices need a plugin or custom work.

How do I set CGST, SGST and IGST in WooCommerce?

In each tax class, add a CGST row and an SGST row for your home state, each at half the total rate and with priorities 1 and 2, then an IGST row at the full rate with the state left blank, placed below the home-state rows. Test with orders to your own state and another state.

Which plugin creates GST invoices in WooCommerce?

PDF Invoices & Packing Slips for WooCommerce is a widely used free option that can be customised for GST, and several India-specific plugins add HSN codes and the CGST, SGST and IGST split. Pick one that's actively maintained, and have your CA check a sample invoice.

How do I add HSN codes to WooCommerce products?

Use a GST invoice plugin that adds an HSN field to products, or add a custom field and show it in the invoice template. Your CA can confirm the right code and how many digits you need to show.

Should prices on my Indian store include GST?

For consumer stores, showing prices inclusive of GST is the usual choice, because shoppers expect the displayed price to be the final price. Business-focused stores sometimes show prices before tax. Either way, the invoice shows the taxable value and tax separately.

Can WooCommerce generate e-invoices with an IRN?

Not on its own. If e-invoicing applies to your turnover, business invoices have to be registered with the government's e-invoice system, which usually happens through your accounting software or a GST service provider connected to the store.
