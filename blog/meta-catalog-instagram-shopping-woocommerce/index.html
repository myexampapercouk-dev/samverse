Connecting WooCommerce to Meta's Catalog for Instagram and Facebook Shopping

Connect WooCommerce or Shopify to Meta's catalog for Instagram and Facebook shopping and ads: setup options, feed fields, policies and stock sync.

Key takeaways
  • Connect WooCommerce through the official Facebook for WooCommerce plugin or a product feed, and Shopify through the Facebook and Instagram sales channel app.
  • Clean product data (titles, prices in INR, availability, images, brand and identifiers) decides whether items are approved and how ads perform.
  • Keep stock and prices in sync automatically, install the pixel and Conversions API, and check which shopping features Meta currently offers in India.

To connect WooCommerce to Meta's catalog, install the official Facebook for WooCommerce plugin or create a product feed, link it to a catalog in Meta Commerce Manager, and keep prices and stock in sync automatically. On Shopify, the Facebook and Instagram sales channel app does the same job. Once the catalog is approved, you can tag products in Instagram posts and run catalog-based ads.

This guide covers both setups, the product data Meta needs, common rejection reasons, and how Indian stores can use the catalog for ads and Instagram, whether they sell on WooCommerce or Shopify.

What Meta's catalog does for a store

A catalog is a list of your products held inside Meta, with titles, prices, images, links and stock status. Once it exists, you can use it to:

  • Tag products in Instagram posts, reels and stories, where available for your account.
  • Run catalog ads (Advantage+ catalog ads, formerly dynamic product ads) that show each person products they viewed or similar items.
  • Show a shop on your Facebook Page and Instagram profile, depending on which features Meta offers in your region.
  • Send people to product pages on your own website to buy.

Meta changes its shopping features from time to time, and availability differs by country. Check Commerce Manager for what is currently available for Indian businesses before planning around a specific feature. For most Indian stores, the main value is catalog ads and product tagging that link back to the store's own checkout.

Before you start: requirements

  • A Meta Business account (Business Manager) that the business owns, not an employee's personal account.
  • A Facebook Page and an Instagram professional account connected to it.
  • A verified domain in Business settings.
  • A live store with clear prices, a returns policy, contact details and working checkout.
  • Products that comply with Meta's commerce policies. Some categories are restricted or prohibited, so check the current list.

If several people manage ads and the store, give them access through Business Manager roles rather than sharing logins.

How to connect WooCommerce to Meta's catalog

There are two main approaches:

MethodHow it worksSuits
Facebook for WooCommerce pluginOfficial plugin connects your store, creates or links a catalog, syncs products and can install the pixelMost stores with standard products
Product feed pluginGenerates an XML or CSV feed URL that Commerce Manager fetches on a scheduleLarge catalogues, custom fields, stores that also feed Google Merchant Center
Manual uploadUpload a spreadsheet in Commerce ManagerVery small catalogues only; goes out of date quickly

Steps with the official plugin, in outline:

  1. Install and activate Facebook for WooCommerce from the WordPress plugin directory.
  2. Click connect and log in with the account that has admin access to the Business Manager.
  3. Choose the business, Page, Instagram account, catalog and pixel.
  4. Choose which products or categories to sync.
  5. Let the sync run, then check Commerce Manager for errors.

If you already send a feed to Google, a feed plugin that can produce both Google and Meta feeds saves duplicate work; see Google Merchant Center for WooCommerce.

Connecting a Shopify store

On Shopify, install the Facebook and Instagram sales channel from the Shopify App Store, connect your Business Manager, Page, Instagram account, pixel and catalog, and choose which products to share. Shopify syncs product updates automatically. Set the data sharing level so the pixel and Conversions API send purchase events. Check product availability in the sales channel so only products you want to advertise are shared. The Shopify store setup guide for India covers related settings.

Product data that gets approved

Meta reviews each item. Clean data is the difference between a catalog that works and one full of rejected items.

  • Title: clear and descriptive, such as "Cotton Kurta Set for Women, Blue, Size M", without all caps, emojis or promotional text.
  • Description: plain product details, not shipping offers.
  • Price: in INR, matching the website exactly, including any sale price and its dates.
  • Availability: in stock or out of stock, synced with WooCommerce or Shopify stock.
  • Image: a clear product photo, without large text overlays or watermarks, at a good resolution.
  • Link: the exact product page URL, which must load without errors or login.
  • Brand and condition: brand name and "new" for most items.
  • Variants: grouped correctly with an item group ID so sizes and colours appear as one product.
  • Category: Google or Meta product category where the plugin supports it.

Good product pages help here too; see WooCommerce product variations for setting up sizes and colours cleanly.

Pixel, Conversions API and tracking

Catalog ads rely on knowing who viewed which product and who bought. Install the Meta pixel and Conversions API so events such as ViewContent, AddToCart, InitiateCheckout and Purchase include product IDs that match the catalog. The official WooCommerce plugin and the Shopify channel can handle this, but check in Events Manager that events arrive, that purchase values are correct, and that duplicate events are deduplicated. The guide to Meta pixel and Conversions API explains the setup and consent considerations.

India-specific points

  • COD orders: purchase events fire when the order is placed, even if COD is later refused. Consider this when judging ad results, and track confirmed deliveries separately; see cash on delivery on Shopify for COD settings.
  • GST-inclusive prices: show the same price the customer pays on the product page.
  • Delivery promises: keep ad text consistent with real delivery times to the pincodes you serve.
  • Regional catalogues: if prices or availability differ by region, keep it simple at first; one accurate catalogue beats several inconsistent ones.

Using the catalog in ads and Instagram

Once products are approved, organise them into product sets in Commerce Manager, such as best sellers, new arrivals, a sale collection or a price band. Product sets let you run different ads for different groups instead of advertising the whole catalogue at once. Common uses for Indian stores:

  • Retargeting: show people the products they viewed but did not buy, with the current price and stock.
  • Prospecting: let Meta show relevant products from a set to new audiences.
  • Collection and carousel ads: group a festive edit or a gifting range.
  • Product tags in organic posts: tag items in reels and posts, where the feature is enabled, so viewers can tap through to the product page.

The product page the ad links to matters as much as the ad. It should load fast on mobile, show the same price, have clear delivery and COD information, and make it easy to add to cart. A slow or confusing product page wastes ad spend no matter how good the catalog setup is behind it.

Keeping the catalog healthy

Check Commerce Manager weekly for new errors, especially after changing prices, running a sale, adding products, or updating plugins or themes. Remove discontinued items rather than leaving them out of stock forever. Before big sale periods such as Diwali, test that sale prices and dates flow into the catalog correctly, because mismatched prices are a common reason for rejected ads at the worst possible time.

FAQs

How do I connect WooCommerce to Meta's catalog?

Install the Facebook for WooCommerce plugin, connect your Business Manager, Page, Instagram account, pixel and catalog, choose products to sync, and fix any errors shown in Commerce Manager. A product feed plugin is an alternative for large or custom catalogues.

Can I tag products on Instagram from a WooCommerce or Shopify store?

Once the catalog is connected and your account meets Meta's requirements, product tagging can be enabled where Meta offers it. Check Commerce Manager for current availability in India.

Why are my products rejected in Commerce Manager?

Usually because of policy restrictions, misleading wording, price mismatches, poor images or broken links. Commerce Manager lists the reason for each item.

Does the catalog update automatically when stock changes?

With the official plugin, a feed on a schedule, or the Shopify channel, yes. Manual uploads do not, so they go out of date.

Do I need the Conversions API as well as the pixel?

It is recommended. Sending events from the server as well as the browser makes tracking more reliable when browsers block scripts.
