GST on Shopify for Indian Sellers: Tax Settings, HSN Codes, GST Invoice Apps and B2B GSTIN Capture

Set up GST on Shopify in India: tax settings, tax-inclusive prices, HSN codes, a GST invoice app, B2B GSTIN capture and what to confirm with your CA.

Key takeaways
  • Set tax collection for India, tax-inclusive prices and collection overrides in Shopify, using rates confirmed in writing by your CA.
  • Shopify does not issue GST invoices by itself at the time of writing, so choose an invoice app that handles HSN codes, the tax split, numbering and credit notes.
  • Business buyers need a way to give their GSTIN, through a cart field, a checkout extension or Shopify B2B features, and it must reach the printed invoice.

To handle GST on Shopify, you set India's tax options in the taxes and duties settings, decide whether prices include tax, add overrides where products carry different rates, store an HSN code on each product and install a GST invoice app, because Shopify does not issue a GST-compliant invoice by itself at the time of writing. Business buyers need a way to give their GSTIN at checkout. Your CA should confirm the rates and invoice format before you take real orders.

This guide covers the Shopify side of that work: where the settings are, what to check in an invoice app and where the common mistakes happen. It is not tax advice, and the rules change, so treat every tax point here as something to confirm. If you want the settings and invoice app configured and tested, that is part of my Shopify store setup service. The same ideas on WooCommerce are explained in the WooCommerce GST setup guide, which goes further into CGST, SGST and IGST.

What to settle with your CA first

Shopify can only apply the rules you give it. Before touching the settings, get these answers in writing:

  • the GST rate for each product type you sell, and whether any rate depends on the selling price;
  • the HSN code for each product type, and how many digits your invoices need to show;
  • whether prices on the store should include GST, which is what most Indian shoppers expect;
  • how shipping charges, COD fees and discounts should be taxed;
  • how in-state and out-of-state sales should appear on the invoice;
  • the invoice number format, and whether it restarts each financial year;
  • whether e-invoicing applies to your business-to-business sales, given your turnover.

Bring the answers to whoever sets up the store; the Shopify setup guide for India shows where tax fits among the other launch tasks. Most GST problems on new stores come from guessing these points rather than from Shopify itself.

Shopify tax settings for India

The tax options sit in the taxes and duties area of the settings, with India listed under the regions you sell to. The exact screens change as Shopify updates the admin, so use these as the things to look for rather than a click path:

  1. Collect tax in India. Make sure tax collection is switched on for India and that your business details are correct.
  2. Prices include tax. Turn on the option that treats your listed prices as tax-inclusive if that is what your CA advised, so a product listed at a round figure costs exactly that at checkout.
  3. Default rate. Shopify may pre-fill a rate for India. Treat it as a placeholder and set it to the rate that applies to most of your catalogue.
  4. Overrides. Where some products carry a different rate, create a collection for them and add a tax override for that collection.
  5. Shipping. Decide, with your CA, whether tax is charged on shipping, and set the matching option. How the shipping rates themselves are built is covered in Shopify shipping in India.
  6. Per product. Each product has a setting for whether tax is charged on it. Check that exempt items are marked correctly and that nothing else is.

Rate-by-price rules, where a product falls into one rate below a price and another above it, are awkward to express in Shopify's settings. Stores often handle them with separate collections and overrides, or with an app. Check the result at checkout with real prices on both sides of the boundary.

HSN codes on products

Shopify products have a field for a harmonised system code in the shipping and customs section. It was designed for international shipping, and the number of digits it expects may not match what your Indian invoices need. Many invoice apps let you enter HSN codes in their own settings or read them from a product metafield instead.

A metafield is usually the tidiest choice: you create a field called HSN code once, it appears on every product's edit screen, and it can be filled in bulk through a product spreadsheet import. Whichever place you choose, use only one, and make sure the invoice app reads from it. The guide to Shopify theme customisation explains metafields in more detail.

Choosing a GST invoice app

Several apps generate GST invoices for Shopify stores, and their features differ. I won't recommend one here because listings and features change; test two or three with real orders before committing. Check each against this table.

B2B buyers and GSTIN capture

A registered business buying from you will want its GSTIN and company name on the invoice. Shopify's standard checkout has an optional company name field, but at the time of writing there is no dedicated GSTIN field in it by default. Common ways to collect it are:

  • a field on the cart page saved to the order as a note or attribute, which the invoice app then reads;
  • a checkout field added by an app or extension, where your plan allows checkout changes;
  • Shopify's own B2B features on higher plans, which store company details against the customer;
  • for occasional buyers, a manual step where staff add the GSTIN to the order before the invoice is issued.

Whichever route you choose, validate the format so typing mistakes are caught, and check that the GSTIN actually reaches the printed invoice.

In-state and inter-state sales

In broad terms, a sale within your own state usually carries CGST and SGST, and a sale to another state usually carries IGST. In practice the split depends on the place of supply, which for goods sent to a customer is generally where they are delivered, with exceptions. Shopify's checkout shows the tax amount, but the split printed on the invoice normally comes from the invoice app comparing the delivery state with your registered state.

That makes three things worth testing: an order to your own state, one to another state, and one where the billing and shipping states differ. Show the results to your CA. If you sell to customers abroad, ask separately how exports should be invoiced; that is outside this guide.

A GST checklist before you go live

  1. Rates, HSN codes, invoice format and treatment of shipping, COD fees and discounts confirmed by your CA.
  2. Tax collection for India switched on, with business details correct.
  3. Prices set as inclusive or exclusive of tax, as advised.
  4. Default rate and collection overrides set, and exempt products marked.
  5. HSN codes stored in one place that the invoice app reads.
  6. Invoice app tested with an in-state order, an inter-state order and a B2B order with a GSTIN.
  7. Credit note created correctly for a refund and a partial refund.
  8. COD order invoiced and reversed correctly when refused.
  9. Monthly export checked by your accountant for the first filing.

FAQs

Does Shopify generate GST invoices in India?

Not by itself at the time of writing. Shopify calculates tax at checkout and sends order confirmations, but a GST-compliant invoice with your GSTIN, HSN codes and the tax split normally comes from an invoice app.

Should Shopify prices include GST?

Most Indian stores show tax-inclusive prices because shoppers expect the listed price to be the final price. Confirm the choice with your CA and set the matching option in Shopify.

Where do I put HSN codes in Shopify?

In a product metafield, the customs code field or the invoice app's own settings, depending on what your app reads. Use one place consistently.

Can customers enter their GSTIN at checkout?

Not in a dedicated field by default at the time of writing. It can be collected on the cart page, through a checkout extension where your plan allows it, or with Shopify's B2B features on plans that include them.

Do I still need a CA if the app calculates GST?

Yes. The app applies the rules you give it. Your CA confirms the rates, the invoice format and how filing will work, and should review the first month's invoices.
