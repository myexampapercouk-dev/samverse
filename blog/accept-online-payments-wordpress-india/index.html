How to Accept Online Payments on a WordPress Website in India: UPI, Cards, Gateways, KYC and Setup

Key takeaways
  • Open an account with an Indian payment gateway in your business name, complete KYC and publish your policy pages, then connect the gateway to WooCommerce, a payment form or payment links.
  • Set up webhooks so orders paid by UPI are marked paid even if the customer never returns to your site, and check the settlement cycle so you know when money reaches your bank.
  • Test in test mode first, including failed, cancelled and refunded payments on a phone, then switch to live keys and confirm with a real low-value order.

To accept online payments on a WordPress website in India, open an account with an Indian payment gateway such as Razorpay, Cashfree, PayU, PhonePe Payment Gateway or CCAvenue in your business name, complete its KYC checks and make sure your site shows contact details and policy pages. Then install the gateway's official plugin for WooCommerce, or use a payment form or payment links if you don't need a full store, add your test keys, set up webhooks and test every outcome before switching to live keys. Customers can then pay by UPI, cards, net banking and wallets in one checkout.

Payment setup touches the website, the gateway dashboard and your business paperwork, which is why it is part of every store I build as a WooCommerce developer: the gateway is set up in test mode first, with successful, failed and cancelled payments, order emails and webhooks tested before going live. This guide explains each step so you know what is involved.

Still choosing a provider? How to choose a payment gateway in India covers what to compare, from fees to international cards. This guide is about getting one working on WordPress.

How UPI works on a website

UPI comes through the gateway, not as a separate integration. On a phone, the checkout can open the customer's UPI app directly; on a desktop, it usually shows a QR code to scan. Either way, the customer leaves your page to approve the payment and may never come back to it, especially on a weak connection. That is why webhooks matter: the gateway tells your site the payment succeeded, server to server, and the order is marked paid regardless of what the customer's browser does. For recurring payments such as memberships, gateways offer UPI AutoPay and card mandates, subject to RBI's e-mandate rules.

KYC and getting approved

Payment aggregators in India work under RBI rules, so onboarding includes proper verification. Requirements vary by gateway and business type, but expect:

  • PAN of the business or proprietor
  • A current account or bank account in the business name
  • Business proof, such as a GST certificate, Udyam registration or shop and establishment licence
  • Address and identity proof for directors, partners or the proprietor
  • A business category the gateway supports; some categories are restricted or need extra approval

Gateways also review your website. Before applying, make sure it is live (not a "coming soon" page) and shows:

  • Your business name, address, phone number and email
  • Clear products or services with prices
  • Terms and conditions and a privacy policy
  • Refund and cancellation policies, and shipping or delivery terms if you ship goods

Privacy policy basics for Indian websites covers the privacy page. Missing policies are among the most common reasons applications are sent back.

Settlements: when the money reaches your bank

Settlement is when the gateway pays collected money, minus its fees and GST on those fees, into your bank account. Standard cycles are commonly around two working days after the payment (written T+2), though new accounts can start on a longer cycle and some gateways offer faster settlement for an extra charge. Check the cycle in your agreement, and in what situations the gateway can hold a settlement, such as unusual volumes or disputes.

Use the gateway's settlement reports to match payouts against WooCommerce orders. Refunds are usually deducted from future settlements. If you are GST-registered, see WooCommerce GST setup and invoices for the store side.

Setting up a gateway in WooCommerce, step by step

  1. Apply early. Approval is in the gateway's hands, so start while the site is being built. Most gateways give you test-mode access straight away.
  2. Install the official plugin from the WordPress plugin directory or the gateway's documentation.
  3. Generate test API keys in the gateway dashboard and paste them into WooCommerce > Settings > Payments, under the gateway's settings.
  4. Set up the webhook. Copy the webhook URL the plugin shows into the gateway dashboard, add a webhook secret if asked, and select the payment events the plugin's documentation lists.
  5. Check store settings: currency set to Indian rupee, the checkout page working, order emails enabled.
  6. Test every outcome using the checklist below.
  7. Switch to live keys once your account is activated, update the webhook for live mode, place a real low-value order and refund it.

Test mode: what to check before going live

Gateways publish test card numbers and test UPI details in their documentation for this. Test on a phone as well as a desktop.

Not every site needs a cart. Common problems and fixes

  • Application sent back: usually missing policy pages, prices or contact details on the site, or a business category that needs extra documents.
  • Paid orders stuck at "Pending payment": almost always a missing or wrong webhook, or test keys in one place and live keys in another.
  • Payment option missing at checkout: the gateway isn't enabled, the currency isn't INR, or the plugin doesn't support the block checkout.
  • Checkout errors after an update: a plugin conflict or a security rule blocking the gateway's callback.

Fixing a payment gateway that isn't working in WooCommerce goes through each of these in detail.

Keeping payments secure

With a gateway's hosted page or popup, card details are entered on the gateway's side rather than stored on your server, which keeps most of the card security burden with the gateway. Your side of the job is a secure site: HTTPS everywhere, WordPress and plugins kept updated, strong unique admin passwords with two-factor login, and regular checks that nobody has added unknown scripts to the checkout page.

FAQs

Which payment gateway is best for a WordPress website in India?

There's no single best one. Razorpay, Cashfree, PayU, PhonePe and CCAvenue all support UPI, cards and net banking and work with WooCommerce. Choose on fees for your mix of payment methods, settlement cycle, KYC fit for your business type and the quality of the plugin.

Can I accept UPI payments on WordPress without a gateway?

You can show a static UPI QR code from your bank, but payments aren't linked to orders automatically, so you have to check each one by hand. A gateway confirms UPI payments and updates orders for you.

Do I need GST registration to get a payment gateway?

Not always. Many gateways onboard proprietors with PAN, a bank account and other business proof, but requirements depend on the gateway, your business type and category. Ask your CA whether you need GST registration for your business itself.

How long does payment gateway approval take?

It varies with the gateway, your documents and how complete your website is. Apply early, use test mode to build and test in the meantime, and make sure policies and contact details are live before you apply.

Can I use Stripe for a website in India?

Stripe has limited new sign-ups for Indian businesses in recent times. Check its current status for your business before planning around it; Indian gateways such as Razorpay or Cashfree are the usual choice for domestic payments.

Can I accept payments from customers outside India?

Yes, through international card acceptance on an Indian gateway, which is often a separate activation, or through PayPal for international payments. Ask about currency conversion, fees and export paperwork for your CA.
