How Much Is a Landing Page? What Drives the Cost to Build One, and What a Good Cost per Lead Looks Like

What is a good cost per landing page? What drives the build cost in India, how to judge a quote, and why cost per lead is a separate number to track.

Key takeaways
  • A good cost per landing page is judged by what the quote includes and whether the page can lower your cost per lead, not by the lowest price.
  • Copy, design approach, form integrations, tracking and revision rounds are what move the build cost of a landing page.
  • Never cut mobile speed, a tested form or conversion tracking, or you cannot tell whether the page or the ads are the problem.

A good cost per landing page is one your campaign can recover in leads: a price that covers focused copy, mobile speed, a tested form and conversion tracking, because those are what lower your cost per lead from ads. How much is a landing page? According to my WordPress website cost guide, one landing page typically comes to about ₹5,000 to ₹15,000, compared with ₹15,000 to ₹40,000 for a typical small business site. Where your page falls depends on who writes the copy, whether the design is adapted or custom, what the form connects to, how much tracking is set up and how many changes are included. That is the cost to build. The cost per lead is a different number, driven mostly by your ads and offer, and I cover it separately below.

If you want a quote for your own campaign, landing page design lists what I include. The rest of this article explains each cost driver so you can compare quotes fairly.

Two different costs: to build, and per lead

Cost to buildCost per lead
What it isWhat you pay to have the page written, designed, built and connectedYour ad spend divided by the leads the page produces
When you payOnce, plus any later changesEvery month the campaign runs
Main driversCopy, design, sections, integrations, tracking, revisionsClick cost in your market, offer, page conversion rate, lead quality, follow-up speed
Who controls itYou and the developer, through scopeMostly the ads and the offer; the page influences conversion

A cheap page that converts poorly can cost far more over a campaign than a better page, because every wasted click is paid for. That is the honest reason to care about build quality, not because a higher price guarantees results.

What drives the cost to build

1. Copywriting

The words do most of the selling. Cost depends on who writes them (you, the developer or a specialist copywriter), how much research goes into your offer, audience and ads, and how many versions you need for different ad groups, cities or services. A Hindi or regional-language version means extra writing and checking, not just translation. Even if you write the copy yourself, a review for clarity and ad match is usually worth paying for.

2. Design approach

ApproachSuitsTrade-off
Adapted templateQuick tests, tight budgetsCan look generic and carry unused code
Custom design on your existing siteOngoing campaigns matching your brandMore design and build time
Fully custom, lightweight buildLarger campaigns where speed and conversion matter mostMost time up front

Where it lives matters too. A page on your existing WordPress site reuses its hosting; a separate domain needs its own hosting, SSL and upkeep.

3. Length and sections

A free-consultation offer may need only a headline, benefits, proof and a form. A real estate project, course or B2B service often needs features, plans, a gallery, FAQs, a comparison table, a map and testimonials. Each section means more writing, design and mobile testing. Supplying your own photos, videos and genuine testimonials keeps this down.

4. Forms and integrations

  • Multi-step or conditional forms
  • Leads sent to a CRM, Google Sheets or an email tool
  • Instant WhatsApp or email alerts to your sales team
  • Booking, payment or brochure download after the form
  • Spam protection and a proper thank-you page

Each connection needs testing end to end with real submissions before ads go live; connecting website forms to a CRM explains the options for sending leads onward.

5. Tracking

If you pay for clicks, tracking is not optional. It can include GA4 events, Google Ads conversion tracking, the Meta Pixel, call and WhatsApp click tracking, and UTM tags. It takes longer across several ad platforms or when leads must be matched back to campaigns in a CRM. Ask each quote which conversions will be tracked; see Google Ads conversion tracking setup.

6. Speed

Many ad clicks come from phones on patchy data. Compressing images, limiting fonts and scripts and testing on real devices takes time, more so on a heavy theme. Ask whether speed testing is in the quote.

7. A/B testing

Building a second version of the headline, offer or form, setting up the test and reading results is extra work, and only makes sense with enough traffic. A/B testing landing pages explains when it is worth it.

8. Revisions and campaign edits

Agree up front how many revision rounds are included before launch, and how later edits are charged: included for a period, per change, or under a monthly arrangement. Same-day changes during a live campaign usually cost more than changes that can wait.

What is a good cost per landing page lead?

There is no single good figure, and I would be wary of anyone quoting one without knowing your business. Cost per lead depends on:

  • Your market: click costs vary by industry, city and season
  • Your offer: a free site visit attracts more leads than a paid consultation
  • The page: message match with the ad, speed, form length and trust signals
  • Lead quality: a cheaper lead that never buys is not cheaper
  • Follow-up: slow replies waste leads you have already paid for

A practical way to set your own target: work out what a customer is worth to you, how many leads you typically need to win one, and the most you can pay per lead while still making money. Then track actual cost per lead and, more importantly, cost per customer. Google Ads budget for small businesses walks through the arithmetic.

Is your cost per landing page good? A quick way to judge

Instead of comparing one landing page price with another in isolation, compare what each quote includes against what the page has to do. A low price that leaves out tracking or mobile speed is not good value, because you will pay for it in wasted ad spend. A higher price is only worth it if the extras will be used.

QuestionGood signWarning sign
Who writes the copy?Copy is written or edited around one offer and one audiencePlaceholder text you are expected to fill in later
Is it tested on mobile?Fast on a mid-range phone, form usable with a thumbOnly shown as a desktop preview
Where do leads go?Email alert plus a sheet or CRM, tested before launch"The form will work" with no test
Is tracking included?Conversion tracking for the ad platforms you use, checked with a test leadTracking left to "your ads person"
What changes are included?A stated number of revision rounds and campaign editsEvery change billed separately with no estimate
Can it be reused?A template you can copy for the next offer or cityA one-off build nobody else can edit

If a quote ticks the good-sign column at a price within your budget, it is a good cost per landing page, even if it is not the cheapest one you were offered.

How to brief a landing page so quotes compare

  1. Goal and offer: what visitors should do and what they get
  2. Traffic source: Google Ads, Meta, email or a mix, with ad copy if you have it
  3. Copy: supplied, edited or written from scratch
  4. Design: template, custom or matching your site, with two or three pages you like
  5. Sections: a rough list from headline to FAQ
  6. Form and integrations: fields, where leads go, who gets alerts
  7. Tracking: platforms and conversions
  8. Where it lives: existing site or new domain
  9. Revisions, changes and testing: how long the campaign runs
  10. Deadline: when ads start

Then check every quote against the same list. How to compare website quotes explains why quotes differ, and landing page mistakes that waste ad budget shows where cutting corners costs you later.

Quote checklist

  • Who writes the copy, and how many versions
  • Design approach and where the page is hosted
  • Number of sections
  • Form integrations and alerts included
  • Which conversions are tracked
  • Speed testing on mobile
  • Revision rounds before launch, and the rate for later edits
  • Whether an A/B variant is included

Where the money goes on a typical page

To make the drivers concrete, here is how the work on a single lead-generation page usually splits. The proportions shift with every project, so read this as a description of tasks, not a price breakdown.

StageTasksWhat makes it bigger
PlanningUnderstanding the offer, audience, ads and competitors' pagesSeveral services, cities or audiences

Design and buildLayout, mobile version, images, buttons, thank-you pageCustom design, galleries, video, many sections

Ways to keep the cost down without hurting results

  • Write a first draft of the copy yourself, in plain words, and pay for an edit
  • Supply real photos of your work, team or premises
  • Keep the form short and send leads to one place to start with
  • Build on your existing WordPress site instead of a new domain
  • Track the conversions you will actually use, not every possible event
  • Launch one strong version first, and test a variant only once traffic justifies it

The things not to cut are mobile speed, a working form with alerts, and conversion tracking. Without those, you cannot tell whether the page or the ads are the problem.

FAQs

How much is a landing page in India?

Going by the cost guide here, one landing page typically costs ₹5,000 to ₹15,000. Custom copy, several integrations, multi-platform tracking or A/B variants can take it higher.

What is a good cost per landing page lead?

It depends on your market, offer and customer value. Set a target from what a customer is worth to you, then measure real cost per lead and per customer.

Is a landing page cheaper than a website?

Usually, because it is one page with one goal. Landing page vs website explains when you need which.

Does the landing page price include ads?

No. Ad spend is paid to the platform and is separate from the build.

Why do landing page quotes vary so much?

Each developer assumes a different scope. A shared brief makes quotes comparable.

What is a good cost per landing page?

One that includes focused copy, mobile speed, a tested form and conversion tracking at a price your campaign can recover in leads. Going by the cost guide here, a single landing page typically falls in the ₹5,000 to ₹15,000 range, with the scope deciding where in that range.
