Websites for Event and Wedding Planners: Showcasing Your Work and Winning Enquiries

By Sameer Gupta · 27 September 2026 · 3 min read

On this page
  1. Show real events
  2. Explain your services clearly
  3. Build trust
  4. Make enquiring easy
  5. Performance with lots of photos
  6. SEO and marketing
Key takeaways
  • Show real events with short stories, and explain your services and process.
  • Testimonials, vendor partners and a short enquiry form build trust and leads.
  • Compress large galleries and target local wedding and event searches.

Couples and companies choosing an event or wedding planner are trusting you with a day that has to go right. Your website should show that you can deliver, visually and emotionally, and make it easy to start a conversation.

Show real events

  • Galleries of real weddings and events you've planned, with a short story for each
  • Variety: intimate ceremonies, large weddings, destination events, corporate conferences, launches
  • Photos of décor, venues, stage setups and guest experiences

Credit photographers where appropriate, and get client permission before publishing.

Explain your services clearly

  • Full planning, partial planning, day-of coordination
  • Décor and design, venue sourcing, vendor management, guest hospitality, logistics
  • Packages or "starting from" ranges to help clients self-qualify
  • Your planning process, from first meeting to event day

Build trust

  • Testimonials and video messages from real clients
  • Vendor and venue partners you work with
  • Features in publications or awards you've genuinely received
  • Your team, with photos

Make enquiring easy

Use a short form asking for event type, date, city, guest count and budget range, plus WhatsApp for quick conversations. Many clients browse on their phones late at night, so make sure everything works well on mobile. See WhatsApp on your business website.

Performance with lots of photos

Event galleries can be huge. Compress images, use thumbnails in grids, lazy-load galleries and choose good hosting so pages stay fast; see image optimization for WordPress.

SEO and marketing

  • Target "wedding planner in {city}", "destination wedding planner {place}" and "corporate event management {city}"
  • Write planning guides: checklists, budgets, venue ideas, timelines
  • Create pages for key venues or destinations only where you have real experience and photos
  • Use landing pages for wedding-season campaigns; see landing page design
  • Keep a strong Google Business Profile and Instagram presence, linking back to your site

A beautiful, fast website with real stories and easy enquiries helps you book the events you want. See WordPress website development.
