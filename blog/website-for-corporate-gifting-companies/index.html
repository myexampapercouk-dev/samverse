Website for Corporate Gifting Companies: Catalogues, Branding and Bulk Quotes

What a website for corporate gifting companies in India needs: catalogues, branding options, bulk quote forms, Diwali campaigns, GST and Shopify or Woo.

Key takeaways
  • Corporate buyers want to see products, branding options, minimum quantities and lead times quickly, then send a bulk quote request without friction.
  • A catalogue with a quote request flow usually works better than a plain shop cart, though WooCommerce or Shopify can combine both for smaller orders.
  • Plan festive campaigns such as Diwali well in advance, with hampers, deadlines and a clear process for logo approval and delivery to many addresses.

A website for a corporate gifting company in India should let an HR, admin or procurement person do three things quickly: browse gift ideas by budget and occasion, see how branding works, and request a bulk quote. If your site shows products, minimum order quantities, branding methods and lead times, and has a simple quote form with WhatsApp, it is already ahead of many gifting suppliers who only share PDFs on request.

This guide covers the pages to build, whether to use a catalogue or a full store, how WooCommerce and Shopify each fit, and how to prepare for the festive rush.

Who buys corporate gifts and what they look for

The buyer is usually not the person receiving the gift. It is someone in HR, admin, marketing or a founder's office, often working to a deadline and a budget per head. They are comparing a few suppliers and want answers they can forward to their manager. Typical questions are:

  • What can I get within a certain budget per person?
  • Can you print or engrave our logo, and how will it look?
  • What is the minimum order quantity?
  • How long will production and delivery take?
  • Can you deliver to many employee addresses across India, not just our office?
  • Will I get a proper GST invoice?

Your website should answer most of these before the buyer has to call.

Catalogue with quote vs full online store

Most corporate orders are custom: quantity, branding and packaging change every time, so a fixed shop price rarely fits. That is why many gifting companies use a catalogue with a quote request instead of a checkout. There is a middle path too.

ApproachWorks well forWatch out for
Catalogue plus quote formCustom branded bulk ordersSlow replies lose buyers
Full store with checkoutReady-made hampers, small unbranded ordersPrices must stay current
HybridBuy small quantities online, quote for bulkClear rules on what goes where

A quote cart, where buyers add several products and quantities and send them as one request, is a useful pattern. The guide on RFQ forms for B2B websites explains what fields to ask for.

WooCommerce or Shopify for a corporate gifting business

Both platforms can run a gifting catalogue well. The right one depends on how you sell.

  • WooCommerce (WordPress): flexible for quote carts, hiding prices for some products, tiered pricing for quantities and custom fields such as logo upload. It suits companies whose orders are mostly custom and who want full control. See wholesale and dealer pricing in WooCommerce.
  • Shopify: simple to manage and reliable for ready-made hampers and gift boxes with fixed prices, UPI and card payments, and shipping integrations. Quote requests and quantity pricing usually need an app or some customisation. The Shopify store setup guide for India covers payments and GST settings.

If you sell many fixed-price festive hampers to individuals as well as to companies, Shopify can be a good fit. If almost every order is a branded bulk quote, WooCommerce often gives more room. The WooCommerce vs Shopify comparison for India goes deeper.

Showing branding options clearly

Branding is where buyers hesitate most. They worry the logo will look cheap or that colours will be off. Help them with:

  • Photos of real branded products for each method, such as an engraved bottle or an embroidered cap.
  • A short explanation of which method suits which material.
  • Logo file requirements, such as vector formats, and what happens if the buyer only has a low-resolution image.
  • A note that a digital mock-up is shared for approval before production, if that is how you work.
  • A logo upload field on the quote form so the buyer can attach it straight away.

Packaging, samples and the approval process

Corporate buyers rarely sign off alone. A manager or finance team usually approves the final choice, so make your process easy to explain internally. Describe each step on a single "How ordering works" page: the quote, an optional physical sample, a logo mock-up, written approval, production, quality check and dispatch. Say whether samples are charged and whether that charge is adjusted against the final order, if that is your policy.

Packaging deserves its own section. Many companies want a branded box, a printed card with a message from the leadership team, or eco-friendly packaging with no plastic. Show photos of the packaging options you genuinely offer, so the buyer can picture the unboxing their employees or clients will see. This also gives your catalogue pages useful, original content rather than copied supplier descriptions.

Festive season and Diwali corporate gifting

For many Indian gifting companies, the weeks before Diwali bring a large share of the year's orders. Buyers start planning earlier than many suppliers expect, so the website should be ready well ahead.

  1. Publish a Diwali gifting page early with hampers, budget bands and an order deadline for delivery before the festival.
  2. State production and dispatch cut-off dates clearly, and update them as the season fills.
  3. Feature ready-to-ship options for late buyers who cannot wait for custom branding.
  4. Prepare for multi-address delivery: let buyers upload a spreadsheet of addresses with the quote request.

Other occasions, such as new year, employee onboarding kits and work anniversaries, spread demand across the year. Give each its own page so they can be found in search.

GST invoices, payments and delivery across India

Corporate buyers need a GST invoice with their company GSTIN. Your quote form and checkout should collect the company name, GSTIN and billing address. Both WooCommerce and Shopify can generate GST invoices with the right setup; check the details with your CA. See WooCommerce GST invoices or Shopify GST invoices.

For payments, many companies pay by bank transfer against a proforma invoice, while smaller orders can be paid online by UPI or card through a gateway such as Razorpay. For delivery to many addresses, explain how you handle it: bulk dispatch to one office, or individual parcels through a courier partner, with tracking details shared afterwards.

Getting found by corporate buyers

Buyers search for specific things: corporate gifts in their city, Diwali hampers for employees, branded welcome kits, eco-friendly corporate gifts. Build pages around those phrases rather than one generic homepage.

  • Category and occasion pages with real descriptions, not just product grids.
  • City mentions where you genuinely deliver or have an office, without creating copied city pages.
  • A LinkedIn presence that drives traffic to your occasion pages before festive seasons.
  • Fast product images: compress them, because catalogues with hundreds of photos are often slow on mobile.

Website for corporate gifting companies: common mistakes

  • A catalogue that exists only as a downloadable PDF, which Google cannot use well and buyers cannot filter.
  • No minimum order quantities, leading to many small enquiries you cannot serve.
  • Stock product photos from suppliers that look the same as every competitor's.
  • A quote form that asks for too little, so every lead needs a long call to clarify.
  • Last year's Diwali dates still on the homepage.

FAQs

What should a website for corporate gifting companies include?

A catalogue by category, occasion and budget, a page explaining branding methods, minimum quantities and lead times, a bulk quote form with logo upload, examples of real branded work, and WhatsApp for quick questions.

Should a corporate gifting website show prices?

Showing price bands helps buyers filter by budget. For custom branded orders, many companies show a starting range and confirm the final price in a quote, because quantity and branding change the cost.

Is Shopify or WooCommerce better for corporate gifting?

Shopify suits fixed-price hampers and simple online orders. WooCommerce suits quote carts, quantity pricing and custom fields. Many gifting companies with mostly custom orders choose WooCommerce, but both can work.

Can buyers upload their logo and delivery addresses on the website?

Yes. A quote form can accept a logo file and a spreadsheet of delivery addresses, so you have everything needed to prepare the quote and mock-up.

When should we launch our Diwali corporate gifting page?

Well before the festival, because many companies plan and approve gifts weeks in advance. Publish it early, with clear order cut-off dates, so it has time to be found and shared.
