Marathi Website Design: Building a WordPress or Shopify Site in Marathi for Maharashtra Customers

Marathi website design for Maharashtra businesses: Marathi-only or bilingual, fonts, URLs, typing, SEO for Marathi searches and WordPress or Shopify setup.

Key takeaways
  • Decide first whether the site is Marathi-first, English-first with Marathi pages, or fully bilingual, because that choice sets the plugin, URL and SEO plan.
  • Use proper Unicode Marathi text with a tested Devanagari font, never text inside images or old legacy fonts, so Google and phones can read every word.
  • Have Marathi copy written or checked by a fluent writer, add a language switcher and hreflang if there are two versions, and track enquiries from Marathi pages separately.

Marathi website design means building a site whose main text, menus, forms and buttons are in Marathi, in proper Unicode Devanagari, so customers in Maharashtra can read it easily and find it when they search in Marathi. Both WordPress and Shopify handle Marathi well; the real work is in choosing the language structure, the font, the URLs and who writes the copy.

This guide is for shops, clinics, schools, co-operative societies, farm-input sellers, temples, builders and service businesses in Pune and across Maharashtra who are thinking about a Marathi or bilingual website.

Who needs a website in Marathi?

A Marathi website makes sense when a large share of your customers are more comfortable reading Marathi than English. Common cases include:

  • Local service businesses in Pune, Pimpri-Chinchwad, Nashik, Kolhapur, Satara and smaller towns, where customers phone and WhatsApp in Marathi.
  • Agri-input, dairy and rural retail businesses whose buyers are farmers.
  • Schools, coaching classes and colleges where parents prefer Marathi.
  • Temples, trusts, mandals, NGOs and co-operative societies.
  • Clinics and hospitals explaining procedures to patients and families.
  • Builders and plot sellers whose buyers come from nearby towns and villages.

If your buyers are mostly corporate purchase teams or export customers, English stays the main language and Marathi may only be useful for a few local pages.

Marathi-only, English-first or bilingual?

This is the most important decision, because it changes the build.

StructureGood forWhat it involves
Marathi-onlyTemples, local societies, rural sellers, Marathi publicationsOne language, simplest build, WordPress or Shopify set to Marathi
Marathi-first with a few English pagesLocal shops and clinics with some English-speaking visitorsMain site in Marathi, a small English section or summary page
Fully bilingual (Marathi and English)Schools, hospitals, builders, larger brandsEvery page in both languages, a translation plugin or Shopify Markets, hreflang tags, double the content upkeep

Bilingual sites cost more to run, not just to build, because every new page, offer and price change has to be updated twice. Be honest about who will keep the Marathi side current. A half-translated site, where new pages appear only in English, looks neglected. Marathi on WordPress

WordPress supports Marathi as an admin and site language. Under Settings, General, you can set the site language to Marathi, which translates the built-in parts of WordPress and many themes, such as date formats and some buttons.

  • Marathi-only site: set the site language, write every page in Marathi and check the theme, contact form and footer for leftover English labels.
  • Bilingual site: use a multilingual plugin such as Polylang or WPML, which links each Marathi page to its English twin and adds a language switcher.
  • Forms: translate field labels, placeholder text, error messages and the thank-you message. These are often forgotten.
  • Page builders: Elementor and the block editor both handle Devanagari text; test headings and buttons on a phone because Marathi words can be longer than the English ones the design was made for.

If you want help setting this up, a WordPress developer in Pune who reads Marathi can check details that a developer elsewhere might miss, such as wrong conjuncts or awkward line breaks.

Marathi on Shopify

Shopify lets you add Marathi as a store language and translate products, collections, pages and theme text with the Translate and Adapt app. Checkout text is translated by Shopify where the language is supported, so test a full order in Marathi before launch. If the language is not fully covered in checkout, keep checkout in English and say so clearly on the cart page.

For Marathi product pages, write the product name the way customers say it, then add the English or brand name in brackets if buyers search both ways. A Shopify developer can set up separate Marathi URLs and a language switcher so Google sees both versions.

Fonts: making Marathi text look right

Marathi uses the Devanagari script. Many English-focused themes load a Latin-only font, so the browser falls back to a system font and the Marathi text looks mismatched. Pick a font that includes Devanagari and test it.

  • Free Google Fonts with Devanagari support include Mukta, Noto Sans Devanagari, Hind, Baloo 2, Tiro Devanagari Marathi and Yatra One for display headings.
  • Load only the weights you use, usually regular and bold, to keep pages fast.
  • Check conjuncts and the Marathi letters ळ and the eyelash ra (र्‍) render correctly in your chosen font.
  • Increase line height slightly; Devanagari has marks above and below the line that can collide.

More detail on font choices, loading and testing is in Hindi and Devanagari fonts on your website; the same advice applies to Marathi.

Never use legacy fonts or text in images

Some older Marathi content is typed in legacy fonts such as Shree-Lipi or Kruti Dev, where the letters only look Marathi because of the font; underneath, the text is English characters. Google cannot read it, phones show gibberish, and copy-paste breaks. Convert any such text to Unicode before it goes on the site, and check by copying a line into a plain text editor.

The same applies to Marathi text inside banners, pamphlet images and scanned PDFs. Search engines and screen readers cannot read it well. Put the key information in real text on the page and use images only for decoration or photos.

URLs and slugs for Marathi pages

You have two options for page addresses:

  • English or transliterated slugs, such as /seva/ or /services/. These are short, easy to share on WhatsApp and do not turn into long encoded strings.
  • Marathi slugs, such as a URL written in Devanagari. These work, but when copied they often become long percent-encoded strings that look broken in messages.

Most businesses are better off with short English or transliterated slugs and Marathi page titles and headings. For bilingual sites, a folder per language such as /mr/ for Marathi and /en/ for English is the clearest setup.

SEO for Marathi searches

People search in three ways: in Marathi script, in Marathi typed in English letters (for example "pune madhye dentist"), and in English. Your pages should cover the way your customers actually search.

  • Put the main Marathi phrase in the page title, the main heading and the first paragraph.
  • Write a Marathi meta description; Google may show it for Marathi searches.
  • Use your exact business name and address the same way everywhere, including Google Business Profile, in both scripts if you use both.
  • For bilingual sites, add hreflang tags so Google shows the Marathi page to Marathi searchers. See hreflang tags for India.
  • Find Marathi and transliterated keywords with free tools, as shown in keyword research for small businesses.

For local visibility in Pune specifically, combine this with local SEO for Pune businesses: Google Business Profile, reviews and area pages matter as much as language.

Who should write the Marathi content?

Machine translation is a useful first draft but should not go live unchecked. It often picks formal or Hindi-influenced words, gets honorifics wrong and mistranslates product terms. Customers notice, and it weakens trust.

  • Best: write the Marathi copy first, in the voice you use with customers, then create English from it if needed.
  • Good: translate from English, then have a fluent Marathi speaker who knows your trade review every page.
  • Keep a short glossary of trade words, product names and preferred spellings so every page uses the same terms.

Decide in advance who supplies and approves the Marathi text. Writing it into your website brief avoids delays at the end of the project.

Typing and editing Marathi in the dashboard

Whoever updates the site needs a way to type Marathi. Options include the Marathi keyboard on Android and iPhone, Google Input Tools style transliteration, and the built-in Marathi keyboards in Windows and macOS. Test that your team can edit a page and publish it without the text breaking. Copying from Word documents typed in legacy fonts is the most common cause of garbled pages.

Launch checklist for a Marathi website

  1. Language structure decided: Marathi-only, Marathi-first or bilingual.
  2. All text in Unicode, no legacy fonts, no key text inside images.
  3. A Devanagari font loaded and tested on Android and iPhone.
  4. Menus, forms, error messages, footer, cookie notice and WhatsApp button text translated.
  5. Marathi page titles and meta descriptions written.
  6. Language switcher and hreflang tags if bilingual.
  7. Google Business Profile details consistent with the site.
  8. A test enquiry and, for stores, a test order placed in Marathi.
  9. Enquiries from Marathi pages tracked separately in analytics.

FAQs

Can a WordPress website be fully in Marathi?

Yes. Set the site language to Marathi, write every page in Unicode Marathi, and translate the theme, form and footer labels. WordPress itself has a Marathi translation for its interface.

Does Shopify support Marathi?

Shopify lets you add Marathi as a store language and translate products, pages and theme text. Test checkout in Marathi before launch and keep English as a fallback where translation is incomplete.

Which font is best for a Marathi website?

Choose a free Unicode Devanagari font such as Mukta, Noto Sans Devanagari or Hind, load only the weights you need and test the text on real phones.

Will a Marathi website rank on Google?

Marathi pages can rank for Marathi and transliterated searches if the text is real Unicode, the titles and headings use the phrases people search, and the site has the usual local SEO basics in place.

Should I use Marathi or English URLs?

Short English or transliterated URLs are usually better because Marathi URLs turn into long encoded strings when shared. Keep page titles and headings in Marathi.

How much more does a bilingual Marathi and English website cost?

It depends on the number of pages and who writes the translations. Expect extra work for the translation plugin, both sets of content and ongoing updates, so ask for the Marathi side to be quoted separately.
