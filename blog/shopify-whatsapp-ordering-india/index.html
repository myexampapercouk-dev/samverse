Shopify WhatsApp Ordering in India: Chat Buttons, Order Updates and Taking Orders on WhatsApp

How to set up WhatsApp ordering on a Shopify store in India: chat buttons, order and COD confirmation messages, abandoned cart reminders and what to avoid.

Key takeaways
  • Keep Shopify checkout as the place orders are placed and paid; use WhatsApp for questions, order updates, COD confirmation and cart reminders.
  • A click-to-chat button needs no app, while automated order messages need the WhatsApp Business Platform through an approved provider or app, with opted-in customers and approved templates.
  • If you take orders inside chats, create them in Shopify as draft orders with a payment link so stock, GST invoices and shipping stay in one system.

To set up WhatsApp ordering on a Shopify store in India, add a click-to-chat button for questions, connect a WhatsApp Business Platform app for order confirmations and COD checks, and keep Shopify checkout as the place where orders are actually placed and paid. Orders taken inside a chat should still be created in Shopify, usually as a draft order with a payment link, so stock, invoices and shipping stay in one place.

Indian shoppers are used to asking a seller a question on WhatsApp before they buy, and many small brands began by selling only through chats. This guide covers how to bring that habit into a Shopify store without losing the order records, GST invoices and courier bookings that Shopify gives you. Three ways WhatsApp fits a Shopify store

"WhatsApp ordering" means different things to different store owners. It helps to separate the three jobs, because each needs different tools.

JobWhat the shopper seesWhat you need
Pre-sale chatA WhatsApp button on product pages to ask about size, fabric or deliveryA click-to-chat link; no app required
Order updatesOrder confirmation, COD confirmation, shipping and delivery messages on WhatsAppThe WhatsApp Business Platform through an app or provider, with approved message templates
Ordering in chatThe shopper tells you what they want in a chat and pays through a linkDraft orders in Shopify, a payment link and a clear process for your team

Most stores need the first two. The third suits repeat customers, wholesale buyers and products that need a conversation first, such as custom sizes or made-to-order pieces.

Step 1: add a WhatsApp chat button

The simplest version is a link to WhatsApp's click-to-chat address with your business number and an optional pre-filled message. On a phone it opens WhatsApp; on a computer it opens WhatsApp Web or the desktop app.

<a href="https://wa.me/91XXXXXXXXXX?text=Hi%2C%20I%20have%20a%20question%20about%20this%20product">Ask on WhatsApp</a>

Replace the Xs with your number in international format, without the plus sign, spaces or a leading zero. On Shopify you can add this in a few ways:

  • A custom liquid or rich text block on the product template in the theme editor, so the button sits near the add to cart button.
  • A small theme snippet that adds the product title and URL to the pre-filled message, so you know which product the shopper means. This is a short code change on a duplicate theme.
  • A chat app from the Shopify App Store that adds a floating button. It is quick to install, but check how much script it loads on every page.

A floating button on every page is easy to add and easy to overdo. On mobile it can cover the add to cart bar or the cookie notice. Test on a real phone, and consider showing it only on product, cart and contact pages. Step 2: send order updates on WhatsApp

Automated order messages are a different product from the WhatsApp Business app on your phone. They go through the WhatsApp Business Platform, which Meta offers through approved providers and through Shopify apps that connect to it. In general terms, this involves:

  1. A business number registered on the platform. A number already in use on the WhatsApp Business app usually has to be moved or a new number used; check the provider's current process.
  2. Business verification and a display name, handled through Meta's business tools.
  3. Message templates for each automated message, approved before use. Order confirmations, shipping updates and delivery notices fall under utility messages; offers and cart reminders are treated as marketing.
  4. Customer opt-in, collected at checkout or account sign-up, before you send them messages.

Pricing is set per conversation or message type by Meta and the provider, and it changes, so read the current pricing pages of the app you choose rather than relying on old figures. Messages worth automating first:

  • order confirmation with the order number and a link to the order status page;
  • COD confirmation asking the shopper to confirm or cancel;
  • shipped, with the courier and tracking link;
  • out for delivery and delivered.

Keep Shopify's email notifications switched on as well. Some customers do not opt in to WhatsApp, and email remains the record.

Step 3: confirm cash on delivery orders

For many Indian stores, the most useful WhatsApp message is the COD confirmation. A shopper who places a COD order is sent a message with buttons to confirm or cancel. Confirmed orders go to the courier; cancelled ones are closed before you pay to ship them; orders with no reply after a set time get a call or are held.

Most COD confirmation apps tag the order in Shopify with the reply, so your team can filter by tag before booking pickups. This works alongside the other COD controls, such as order value limits and pin code rules, described in the guide to Shopify cash on delivery in India. What it cannot do is stop every refusal at the door, so keep watching your return to origin rate by pin code.

Step 4: abandoned cart reminders on WhatsApp

Shopify can email shoppers who leave checkout after entering their contact details. WhatsApp apps can send a similar reminder to a phone number, which many Indian shoppers notice faster than an email. Treat it carefully:

  • send it only to shoppers who opted in to WhatsApp messages;
  • send one reminder, maybe two, not a sequence every few hours;
  • link straight back to the saved checkout, not to the home page;
  • offer help before a discount, so customers do not learn to abandon carts for a code.

Reminders are classed as marketing messages, which usually cost more and face stricter rules than order updates. The ideas behind good reminders are the same on any platform; see abandoned cart recovery for timing and wording.

Step 5: taking orders inside WhatsApp chats

If customers message you to order instead of using the store, do not fulfil those orders outside Shopify. Create them in Shopify so stock, invoices and shipping stay accurate:

  1. In the Shopify admin, create a draft order with the products, quantities, the customer's details and shipping.
  2. Send the customer the invoice or checkout link from the draft order. They pay through your normal payment gateway, including UPI where your gateway supports it.
  3. For cash on delivery orders taken by chat, mark the draft as payment pending only after you have confirmed the address, the pin code and the amount in writing in the chat.
  4. The paid order then follows the same route as a website order: GST invoice, courier booking, tracking and WhatsApp updates.

Some apps let shoppers build a cart on the website and send it to you on WhatsApp instead of checking out. That suits stores where every order needs a conversation, but it adds a manual step to every sale, so most stores keep it for wholesale or custom work only.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Replacing checkout with chat for everyone. Every manual order is time your team spends typing, and errors in addresses and amounts rise.
  • Messaging people who did not opt in. It annoys customers and can get your number limited or blocked.
  • Running two WhatsApp apps at once. Duplicate messages confuse customers, and both apps load scripts on the storefront.
  • Using a personal number. Use a business number that the team can share and that stays with the business.
  • No reply plan. A chat button with slow replies does more harm than no button. Set business hours and an away message.

FAQs

How do I add WhatsApp ordering to my Shopify store in India?

Add a click-to-chat button for questions, connect a WhatsApp Business Platform app for order and COD confirmation messages, and create any orders taken in chat as Shopify draft orders with a payment link. Keep the normal checkout for everyone else.

Can customers place an order on WhatsApp instead of the Shopify checkout?

Yes, but create the order in Shopify yourself as a draft order and send a payment link. That keeps stock, GST invoices and shipping in one system instead of a chat history.

Do I need an app for a WhatsApp button on Shopify?

No. A click-to-chat link in a theme block or snippet is enough for a chat button. You need an app or a provider only for automated messages such as order confirmations and cart reminders.

Can I use the WhatsApp Business app for automated Shopify order messages?

Not for automated messages at scale. Those go through the WhatsApp Business Platform via a provider or Shopify app, with approved templates. The phone app suits manual replies and small broadcast lists.

Does WhatsApp COD confirmation reduce returns?

It helps you cancel orders that would have been refused before you pay to ship them. It does not remove all refusals, so combine it with order value limits, pin code rules and tracking your return rate.

Will a WhatsApp chat app slow my Shopify store?

It can, if it loads a large script on every page. A plain link in the theme adds almost nothing. Check speed before and after installing, as described in fixing a slow Shopify store.
