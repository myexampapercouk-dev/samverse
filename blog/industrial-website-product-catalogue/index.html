How to Build a Product Catalogue Website for an Industrial Company

By Sameer Gupta · 27 September 2026 · 3 min read

Key takeaways
  • Most B2B catalogues should use "Request a Quote" rather than a cart.
  • Every product page needs specifications, drawings, a datasheet and a pre-filled quote button.
  • Organise categories the way buyers search, and add filters and model-number search for large catalogues.

For industrial companies, the product catalogue is the heart of the website. Buyers and engineers want to find the right product quickly, check specifications and request a quote. A well-structured catalogue does exactly that, and ranks for the specific product searches buyers make.

Catalogue or online store?

Most industrial and B2B companies don't need a cart and checkout. Prices depend on quantity, specifications and delivery. A catalogue with "Request a Quote" is usually the right model. WooCommerce can still power it (for product management and filters), with the cart replaced by quote requests.

Structure your categories like buyers think

  • Group by product type first (for example Pumps → Centrifugal Pumps → End Suction Pumps)
  • Offer secondary browsing by industry or application ("Pumps for Water Treatment")
  • Keep category names in the terms buyers actually search

What every product page needs

  1. Clear product name and model numbers
  2. Photos and drawings, including dimension drawings where relevant
  3. Specification table: capacity, dimensions, materials, ratings and standards
  4. Applications and industries served
  5. Downloadable datasheet / brochure (PDF)
  6. Request a Quote button, pre-filled with the product name
  7. Related products and accessories

Make quote requests effortless

  • Ask for product, quantity, company name, location and contact details
  • Allow file uploads for drawings or specifications
  • Send enquiries to the right sales person instantly, and reply fast
  • Offer WhatsApp for quick questions

Filters and search

For large catalogues, filters by capacity, material, size or application save buyers time. A good site search that understands model numbers is essential.

SEO for product catalogues

  • One page per product or product family, each with a unique title and description
  • Use specific keywords ("SS 304 storage tank 5000 litre manufacturer")
  • Add product and organization schema markup
  • Write descriptive alt text for product images
  • Link categories, products and related articles together

Keep it manageable

Your team should be able to add products, update specifications and upload datasheets themselves. Import product data from spreadsheets to launch large catalogues quickly.
