WooCommerce Product Bundles, Upsells and Cross-Sells: How to Set Them Up for an Indian Store

Set up WooCommerce product bundles, upsells and cross-sells: linked products, bundle plugins, honest pricing, stock, GST and where to show offers.

Key takeaways
  • Upsells suggest a better version, cross-sells suggest an add-on, and bundles sell a set together; each belongs in a different place in the store.
  • WooCommerce linked and grouped products cover simple cases; a bundle plugin is needed for real kits with their own price, stock rules and options.
  • Price bundles honestly, check stock and GST handling on test orders, and judge offers by order value and margin, not by clicks.

WooCommerce product bundles, upsells and cross-sells are three different ways to raise order value. An upsell suggests a better version of the product someone is viewing, a cross-sell suggests something to add alongside it, and a bundle sells several products together, often at a combined price. WooCommerce handles upsells and cross-sells out of the box; true bundles need a plugin or custom code.

Here is how I set them up for Indian stores so they help customers rather than clutter the page.

What's the difference?

When they help, and when they don't

Offers help when they solve a real need: completing a set, saving a second delivery, or choosing a size that suits the customer better. They hurt when they push unrelated products, slow the page down, or interrupt checkout with pop-ups. A good test: would a helpful shop assistant suggest this at this moment? If not, leave it out.

Stores that tend to gain from bundles include beauty and personal care (routines), food and grocery (hampers and combos), electronics accessories, baby products, stationery and gifting. Single high-value items, such as furniture, benefit more from careful upsells and service add-ons like installation.

Built-in options: linked and grouped products

For upsells and cross-sells, open a product, go to Product data > Linked Products, and add products in the Upsells and Cross-sells fields.

  • Upsells appear on the product page, usually below the description, as "You may also like".
  • Cross-sells appear in the cart. The block-based cart in newer WooCommerce versions shows them differently from older themes, so check after each theme update.
  • Grouped products list several simple products on one page with separate quantity boxes. Each item keeps its own price and stock.

Linking products one by one is fine for a small catalogue. For hundreds of products, a plugin that sets rules by category or tag saves hours.

Bundle plugins for real bundles

When you want "Starter Kit: three items, one price, one add-to-cart button", you need a bundle plugin. Features to look for:

  • Bundle price as a fixed amount or as a discount on the sum of the items.
  • Stock taken from each item, so a bundle cannot be sold if one part is out of stock.
  • Optional items and quantity limits, for "build your own box" offers.
  • Variation support, so a customer can choose a shade or size inside the bundle.
  • Clear display of bundle items in the cart, order emails and invoices.
  • Compatibility with your shipping, invoice and inventory plugins.

Before choosing between a plugin and a custom build, read plugin vs custom code in WordPress.

Bundle and upsell ideas by type of store

The best offers come from your own order history: open past orders and look for products that are often bought together. These starting points are common for Indian stores:

Skincare and beautyMorning or night routine setLarger size of a bestseller; travel minis in the cart
Food, spices, snacksFestival hamper or tasting boxFamily pack instead of a single pouch
Mobile and electronics accessoriesCover, guard and cable comboFast charger alongside a cable
Home and kitchenMatching set of containers or linenCare products or spare parts

GiftingCurated boxes by occasion or budgetGift wrap and a message card

Start with two or three bundles built around your best sellers rather than dozens of combinations. Each bundle needs its own photos, description and stock checks, so a short list you maintain well beats a long one that quietly goes out of date. Seasonal bundles, such as Diwali hampers or back-to-school kits, should have clear start and end dates so they are not left on sale with outdated contents.

For "build your own box" offers, set a minimum and maximum number of items and show a running total as the customer picks, so the price is never a surprise in the cart.

Pricing bundles honestly

Show the real saving. If a bundle is priced at the same total as buying items separately, do not label it a "deal"; present it as convenience instead. If you show a struck-through price, it should be the genuine total of the separate items at their current prices. Shoppers in India compare quickly across marketplaces, and inflated "you save" figures damage trust fast. Coupons and bundles can also stack in ways you did not intend, so decide the rules and test them, as covered in WooCommerce coupons and discounts.

GST, invoices and stock for bundles

Bundles raise practical questions that are easy to miss:

  • GST: if the items in a bundle carry different GST rates, how the bundle is taxed matters. Ask your CA how to treat it before you set the tax class, and check that invoices show what your CA expects. See WooCommerce GST invoices in India.
  • Stock: decide whether the bundle has its own stock or uses the stock of its items. Using item stock prevents overselling.
  • Shipping weight: make sure the bundle weight is correct, or courier rates will be wrong.
  • Returns: write down whether a customer can return one item from a bundle, and how the refund is worked out.

Where to place offers

  • Product page: one upsell row and, if relevant, a small "frequently bought together" block near the add-to-cart button.
  • Cart: two to four cross-sells that genuinely fit the items in the cart.
  • Free-shipping nudge: "Add a little more for free delivery", only if you actually offer it.
  • After purchase: a thank-you page or follow-up email suggesting a refill or accessory.
  • Collections: a "Combos" or "Gift sets" category so bundles can be found directly.

Avoid pop-ups at checkout. Once someone is paying, the job is to get out of their way; WooCommerce checkout optimisation explains why.

Keep the page fast

Every recommendation block loads extra products, images and sometimes scripts. Limit rows to a handful of items, compress images, and check speed after adding a bundle plugin on a mid-range phone. Slow product pages lose more sales than an upsell will win back, especially for shoppers on patchy mobile data outside the big metros. WooCommerce speed optimisation covers the details.

Shopify comparison

If you are deciding between platforms, Shopify handles upsells and bundles mostly through apps and its own bundle features, while WooCommerce gives you more control through plugins and code. Both can do the job; the difference is cost structure and flexibility, as explained in WooCommerce vs Shopify in India.

Measuring what works

Track average order value, the share of orders containing a bundle, and margin per order. A popular bundle that sells at a thin margin may not be worth pushing. Look at which cross-sells actually get added to carts and remove the ones nobody clicks. Change one placement at a time and compare similar periods, avoiding festival weeks that distort results. Returns matter as well: if bundle orders come back more often than single items, the bundle may be mixing products people do not really want together.

FAQs

How do I create product bundles in WooCommerce?

Install a bundle plugin, create a bundle product, add the items, set the pricing rule and stock behaviour, then test the cart, invoice and order email.

What is the difference between upsells and cross-sells in WooCommerce?

Upsells suggest a better or larger alternative on the product page. Cross-sells suggest complementary items, usually in the cart.

Can WooCommerce make bundles without a plugin?

Grouped products list items together, but a single bundle price with shared stock needs a plugin or custom code.

How is GST charged on a product bundle?

It depends on the items and how the bundle is supplied. Ask your CA, then set tax classes and check the invoice output.

Do upsells slow down a WooCommerce store?

They can if they load many products or heavy scripts. Keep recommendation rows short and test page speed on mobile.
