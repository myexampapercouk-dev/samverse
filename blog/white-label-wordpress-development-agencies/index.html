White-Label WordPress Development: How Agencies Scale Without Hiring

By Sameer Gupta · 27 September 2026 · 3 min read

Key takeaways
  • White-label developers build sites under your agency's brand while you keep the client relationship.
  • Fixed project pricing protects margins; NDAs and no client contact protect your brand.
  • Choose partners on live work, design accuracy, communication and reliability, starting with a trial.

Many agencies hit the same wall: more website projects than their team can deliver, but not enough steady work to justify another full-time developer. White-label WordPress development solves this by letting you outsource the build while keeping your brand and client relationship.

What is white-label WordPress development?

A white-label developer builds websites on your behalf. You sell and manage the project with your client; the developer delivers the WordPress site behind the scenes. Your client sees only your agency. No credits, no direct contact unless you want it.

Why agencies use white-label developers

  • Take on more projects without hiring, training or paying idle salaries
  • Protect margins with fixed project costs you can mark up
  • Handle busy periods without missing deadlines
  • Focus on strategy and sales while delivery is handled
  • Access specialist skills such as WooCommerce, speed optimization or complex Elementor builds

How it typically works

  1. Brief: you share designs (Figma/XD), content, references and requirements.
  2. Quote: the developer confirms scope, timeline and a fixed price.
  3. Build: development happens on a staging site under your branding.
  4. Review: you review (and show your client), and revisions are made.
  5. Launch: the site goes live on your client's hosting, with handover documentation.
  6. Ongoing: optional maintenance and updates, still under your brand.

Smooth projects start with a clean design file; share the Figma to WordPress handoff guide with your designers.

Pricing models

  • Fixed price per project: the most common. Easy to quote your client with a margin.
  • Hourly: useful for small fixes and ongoing changes.
  • Monthly retainer: a set number of hours or sites per month for agencies with steady volume.

How to choose a white-label partner

  • Portfolio of live sites built to a high standard
  • Design accuracy: can they match your Figma designs closely?
  • Communication: responsive, clear and in your working hours
  • Confidentiality: willing to sign an NDA and never contact your clients
  • Technical quality: fast, mobile-friendly, SEO-ready builds your clients can edit
  • Reliability: realistic timelines, met consistently

Start with a small trial project before handing over bigger ones.

Keeping quality high

  • Use a checklist for each launch: responsive checks, forms, speed, SEO basics, browser testing
  • Keep design systems (fonts, colours, spacing) consistent across Figma and WordPress
  • Agree on revision rounds and turnaround times upfront
  • Keep access and credentials organised and secure

Is white-label right for your agency?

If you regularly turn down or delay website projects, or your team spends more time building than selling and strategising, white-label development can help you grow without the overhead of new hires.
