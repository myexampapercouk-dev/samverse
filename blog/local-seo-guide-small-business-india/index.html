Local SEO is the work of making your business show up when people nearby search for what you offer, such as "dentist near me", "AC repair in Kothrud" or "CA in Noida". Those searches show a map with a few businesses at the top, then normal results. Google says local results are based mainly on relevance, distance and prominence, so the work comes down to a complete Google Business Profile, genuine reviews, consistent business details, useful local pages on your website, and tracking what brings enquiries.

How local search works

For a search with local intent, Google usually shows a map pack of Business Profiles first, then ordinary web results, and Google Maps shows its own list. Google's help pages describe three main factors:

  • Relevance: how well your profile and website match the search, through your categories, services, description and the words on your pages.
  • Distance: how far you are from the searcher, or from the area named in the search. You can't change this, which is why you won't rank everywhere in a big city.
  • Prominence: how well known and trusted your business is, judged from reviews, mentions, links and your website's general strength.

Most local searches in India happen on phones, many with "near me" and many in Hindi, Marathi or a mix with English. The person searching usually wants to call, get directions or message you now, so every step below is about making that easy.

Step 1: Set up and complete your Google Business Profile

For most local businesses the profile brings more calls than the website itself. Claim or create it and complete verification, using your business Google account so the business owns it, not an employee or agency. Then:

  • Choose the most accurate primary category, which matters more than any other field, and add relevant secondary ones.
  • Decide between showing an address (customers visit you) and a service area (you visit them). Don't show a home address you don't want customers turning up at.
  • Fill in hours, phone, services or products, a plain description and attributes, and add special hours for festivals and holidays.
  • Add real photos of your premises, team and work, and keep adding them.
  • Link to the most relevant page of your website rather than always the homepage.

The Google Business Profile checklist covers every setting, monthly upkeep and the mistakes that get profiles suspended.

Step 2: Earn genuine reviews, and reply to every one

Reviews affect both where you appear and whether people pick you. The method is simple and slow: ask every customer after a good experience, with your direct review link on WhatsApp or a QR code at the counter, and reply to every review, good or bad. Don't buy reviews, offer anything in exchange, or ask only the customers you think are happy; Google's policies, at the time of writing, forbid all three. How to get more Google reviews ethically has message templates, timing and staff routines.

Step 3: Keep your NAP consistent and build citations

NAP means name, address and phone. Use exactly the same details on your website, your Business Profile and every directory: the same business name, the same address format with PIN code and landmark, and the same main number. Then list the business where your customers and Google look:

  • General directories such as Justdial and Sulekha, and IndiaMART if you sell to businesses
  • Bing Places and Apple Business Connect, so you appear in Bing and Apple Maps too
  • Industry associations, trade bodies and chambers of commerce

A handful of accurate listings beats hundreds of junk ones, and paid "submit to 500 directories" packages do more harm than good. Business directories and citations in India explains which listings help and how to handle sales calls from directories.

Step 4: Build service and location pages on your website

Your website gives Google the detail a profile can't. Give each main service its own page that says what you do, where, for whom, how it works and how to enquire, using the words customers search with, including your city and areas. Beyond that, what you build depends on how your business works:

Your situationWhat to build
One shop, clinic or office customers visitStrong service pages and a contact page with address, landmark, map and hours
You travel to customers, such as plumbers, pest control or CCTV installersService pages that list the areas you cover, plus area pages only where you have real local work to show
Several branchesOne page per branch with its own address, hours, team and photos, and one Business Profile per branch
One office serving several citiesPages for cities you genuinely serve, each with genuinely different content, not the same page with the name swapped

Copy-paste area pages with only the city name changed are doorway pages under Google's spam policies. Step 5: Local schema and on-page basics

LocalBusiness structured data, or a more specific type such as Dentist, Restaurant or Plumber, tells Google your business name, address, phone, hours and website in a format it reads reliably. Rank Math and Yoast can output it from their settings, and it must match your Business Profile and what is visible on the page. It helps Google understand you; it doesn't guarantee a ranking or a special display.

Alongside it, cover the basics on every local page: a title that names the service and area, your phone as a tap-to-call link, WhatsApp, the address with a landmark, and a page that loads quickly on mobile data. Name the areas you serve in natural language. On Sahni Power Solutions, a generator rental website I built, the FAQ names Bangalore localities such as Whitefield, Electronic City, Peenya and Hebbal alongside its Pan India service, which ties the site to the areas the company covers.

Step 6: Earn local links and mentions

Mentions from other local organisations show Google that you are an established business. Good sources are suppliers and manufacturers you work with, trade associations, local business groups, events you sponsor, schools or charities you support, and local news coverage of something genuinely newsworthy. Bought links and link packages break Google's spam policies and aren't worth the risk.

Step 7: Track what local SEO brings in

  • Business Profile performance: calls, direction requests, website clicks and the searches that found you.
  • Tagged profile links: add UTM parameters to the website link on your profile so GA4 shows those visits separately.
  • Search Console: queries that include your city or areas, and which pages they reach.
  • Enquiry source: ask "How did you hear about us?" on forms and calls, and note the customer's area.

Review these monthly. Calls and direction requests matter more than rankings for a single keyword, because rankings change with where the searcher stands.

Not showing on Google Maps at all?

If you can't find your business even by name, the problem is usually the profile rather than the website: it may be unverified or suspended, have the wrong category or a misplaced pin, or be competing with a duplicate listing. Local SEO in Pune

In a city the size of Pune, proximity matters a lot: a business in Baner rarely ranks for searches made in Hadapsar. Think in areas, decide whether Marathi pages would help your customers, and use Pune-specific listings and associations. Local SEO for Pune businesses covers Pune and Pimpri-Chinchwad in detail.

How long does local SEO take?

Fixing and completing a Business Profile can make a difference within weeks, especially if it was incomplete. Competitive categories in big cities take months of steady reviews, photos and website improvements. Nobody can honestly guarantee a map pack position, because distance from the searcher is outside your control.

Quick local SEO checklist

  1. Verified, complete Google Business Profile in your business's own account
  2. The right primary category, real photos and accurate hours, including holidays
  3. A steady flow of genuine reviews, every one replied to
  4. Identical name, address and phone on your website and key directories
  5. One strong page per main service, and area or branch pages only where they are genuine
  6. LocalBusiness schema matching your profile
  7. Tap-to-call, WhatsApp and a fast mobile site
  8. Monthly check of profile insights and enquiry sources

FAQs

What is local SEO?

It is improving your Google Business Profile, reviews, listings and website so your business appears when people nearby search for what you offer, in the map pack, Google Maps and normal results.

Is a Google Business Profile free?

Yes. Creating and managing a Business Profile is free. Be wary of calls claiming you must pay Google to stay listed or to rank higher.

Do I need a website for local SEO?

A profile alone can bring calls, but a website gives Google far more to understand about your services and areas, and gives customers somewhere to check you before they call. The two work best together.

Should I create a page for every area in my city?

Only for areas you genuinely serve and can say something specific about, such as local work, timings or travel. Near-identical pages with the area name swapped can be treated as doorway pages.

Can I use a virtual office address on my profile?

Generally not. Google's guidelines, at the time of writing, say a virtual or shared office shouldn't be used unless it is staffed during your business hours. If you travel to customers, use a service area instead; profiles with ineligible addresses risk suspension.
