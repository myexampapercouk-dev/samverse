School and Coaching Institute Websites: What Parents and Students Look For

By Sameer Gupta · 29 September 2026 · 3 min read

On this page
  1. What parents and students want to see
  2. Essential pages
  3. Features that increase enquiries
  4. Keep it updated
  5. Get found by local families
  6. Next step

Choosing a school or coaching institute is a big decision for families, and it almost always starts with online research. Parents compare several institutes' websites before calling any of them. Here's what they look for, and how to make sure your website makes the shortlist.

What parents and students want to see

  • Results and achievements: board results, competitive exam selections and toppers, presented honestly
  • Faculty: who will teach, their qualifications and experience
  • Courses and batches: subjects, timings, duration and batch sizes
  • Fees and admission process: clear steps, key dates and required documents
  • Facilities and safety: classrooms, labs, library, transport and security
  • Location and timings: map, directions and contact details

Essential pages

  1. Home: who you are, key achievements, courses overview and an admission enquiry button
  2. About: history, vision, management and accreditation
  3. Courses / Academics: a page per course or class group
  4. Admissions: process, eligibility, dates, fees (if shared) and an enquiry form
  5. Results: year-wise results and toppers
  6. Faculty: profiles with photos
  7. Gallery and events: real photos of campus life
  8. Notice board: announcements, holidays and circulars
  9. Contact: phone, WhatsApp, map and timings

Features that increase enquiries

  • A short admission enquiry form on every page
  • WhatsApp button for quick questions
  • Downloadable prospectus
  • Online fee payment (via a gateway like Razorpay). See accepting online payments on WordPress.
  • Demo class or counselling session booking for coaching institutes

Keep it updated

Nothing damages trust faster than last year's admission dates or an old notice board. Build the site so staff can post notices, events and results themselves in minutes.

Get found by local families

  • Target searches like "best CBSE school in {area}" or "NEET coaching in {city}" in titles and content
  • Create course pages with genuinely useful detail
  • Complete your Google Business Profile and encourage parent reviews
  • Publish helpful articles on exam preparation and admissions

Next step
