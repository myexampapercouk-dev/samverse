Websites for Printing and Packaging Companies: Getting More B2B Orders

How printing and packaging companies can win more B2B orders online: product and capability pages, samples, specifications, MOQs, quote calculators, file upload and SEO.

Sameer Gupta 27 September 2026 · 3 min read
On this page
  1. Organise by product
  2. Show your capability
  3. Make quoting easy
  4. SEO for printing and packaging
Key takeaways
  • Create a page per product type with samples, specs, MOQs and lead times.
  • Show machinery, quality and compliance, and offer file upload and sample requests.
  • Target specific product searches with unique, detailed pages.

Brands, startups and agencies searching for printing or packaging partners want to know quickly: can you make what I need, at my quantity, to my quality standard, and how fast? A clear website answers these and turns searches into quote requests.

Organise by product

Create a page for each product type, such as mono cartons, rigid boxes, corrugated boxes, labels, pouches, brochures and visiting cards. Each should include:

  • Photos of real samples you've produced
  • Materials, sizes, finishes (matte, gloss, foil, embossing) and printing options
  • Minimum order quantities and typical lead times
  • Industries and uses (food, cosmetics, pharma, e-commerce)
  • A "Request a quote" button

Show your capability

  • Machinery, capacity and in-house processes
  • Quality checks, certifications and food-grade or pharma compliance where applicable
  • Brands you've worked with, with permission
  • Sustainability options such as recycled materials and eco-friendly inks

Make quoting easy

  • Quote forms asking for product, size, quantity, material, finish and delivery location
  • File upload for artwork and dielines
  • A sample request option
  • WhatsApp for quick questions

For standard products, simple online ordering with fixed price tiers can work; see WooCommerce development.

SEO for printing and packaging

  • Target specific product searches: "custom rigid boxes manufacturer", "printed pouches for food"
  • Unique product pages with specifications and real photos
  • Guides on choosing materials, finishes and packaging for different products

The approach mirrors other B2B sites; see building a product catalogue website and manufacturer websites.
