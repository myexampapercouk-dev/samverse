Key takeaways
  • All the common form plugins handle a standard contact or quote form; the differences are stored entries, spam tools, conditional logic, file uploads, multi-step forms and integrations, and which of those are free.
  • No form plugin fixes email delivery on its own, so pair any of them with SMTP, stored entries and spam protection, then send a test enquiry from your phone.

Contact Form 7, WPForms, Gravity Forms, Fluent Forms and Elementor Pro's form widget all handle a standard contact or quote form well. The real differences are whether entries are stored in the dashboard, which spam protection is built in, and whether conditional logic, file uploads, multi-step forms and integrations come free or in a paid plan. Choose by the forms your site actually needs, and whatever you pick, pair it with SMTP and spam protection, because those decide whether enquiries reach you.

Every site I build through WordPress website development includes contact forms and a WhatsApp button, tested before launch to make sure the emails arrive, and the form plugin is chosen to fit the site rather than out of habit. The plugins below are listed alphabetically, not ranked. Features and pricing change, so treat the details as correct at the time of writing and check each plugin's current plans before you decide.

What a business form plugin needs to do

  • Send notifications reliably, to more than one address if needed, with a reply-to set to the visitor's email.
  • Store every entry in the dashboard, so nothing is lost if an email fails.
  • Block spam without puzzles that put real visitors off.
  • Handle the fields you need: phone numbers, dropdowns for the service, file uploads for drawings or documents.
  • Show or hide fields based on earlier answers, if your forms need it.
  • Connect to your tools: a CRM, Google Sheets, an email list or a payment gateway.
  • Stay light, loading its scripts only where it's needed, and accessible, with proper labels and clear error messages.

Each plugin in brief

Contact Form 7

Free and long-established. Forms are written with tags such as [text* your-name] rather than dragged into place, which developers often like and owners can find awkward. It doesn't store entries unless you add Flamingo, and by default it loads its scripts on every page, which a developer can limit. A sensible choice for simple contact forms on a site someone technical looks after.

Elementor Forms

Not a separate plugin, but the form widget in Elementor Pro. Its strength is design: the form matches the page without extra styling. It stores submissions, supports file uploads and step-by-step forms, and connects to common email tools. If your site already runs Elementor Pro, it avoids another plugin; if it doesn't, it isn't a reason on its own to buy Pro. Is Elementor Pro worth it? covers that decision.

Fluent Forms

A drag-and-drop builder whose free version includes stored entries, conditional logic and several spam options, with file uploads, multi-step forms and more integrations in Pro at the time of writing. Worth a look if you want more than a basic form without paying.

Gravity Forms

Paid only, and built for forms that do more than send an email: calculations, multi-page applications, approvals and data passed to other systems. Its add-ons cover many CRMs, email tools and payment gateways, though some sit on higher licence tiers. Often more than a five-page business site needs, and well suited to forms that are central to how the business runs.

WPForms

Beginner-friendly, with form templates and a guided builder. The free Lite version handles basic contact forms with spam protection; entry management, conditional logic, file uploads and multi-page forms are in paid plans at the time of writing.

Spam protection: layers, not puzzles

Whichever plugin you choose, the approach is the same: a honeypot field first, then an invisible challenge such as Cloudflare Turnstile or reCAPTCHA v3 for persistent bots, then a content filter such as Akismet for spam typed by hand. Avoid image puzzles on a lead form, because they cost real enquiries, especially on phones. How to stop contact form spam shows where each setting lives in each plugin.

Email delivery: the part no form plugin fixes

Every plugin above sends its notifications through WordPress's own mail function, which by default sends from your hosting server. Those emails often land in spam or never arrive, and the form still shows "Thank you" to the visitor, so nobody notices until enquiries seem to dry up. Three habits fix most of it:

  1. Send through SMTP or a transactional email service, using an SMTP plugin, with SPF, DKIM and DMARC records set up for your domain. Some form plugin makers also publish SMTP plugins, but any SMTP plugin works with any form plugin.
  2. Store entries, so a failed email never means a lost lead.
  3. Send a test enquiry from your phone after every plugin, hosting or email change.

WordPress not sending emails? walks through the SMTP setup step by step.

Integrations: CRM, Google Sheets and WhatsApp

An email notification is a doorbell, not a record of your leads. Most of these plugins can also send each entry to a CRM, a Google Sheet or an email list, either through their own add-ons or a webhook to an automation tool, and these connections are often a paid feature. If your team replies on WhatsApp, a thank-you message with a WhatsApp link, or a form that opens WhatsApp with the details already typed, keeps the conversation where your customers prefer it. Connecting WordPress forms to a CRM or Google Sheets covers the options.

If you want to take a payment through a form, such as a booking fee, check which gateways the plugin supports in India before you commit. Payment support differs a lot between plugins and plans.

Conditional logic and multi-step forms

Conditional logic shows or hides fields based on earlier answers: choose "Generator rental" and a capacity field appears; choose "Maintenance" and it doesn't. Multi-step forms split a long form into short screens. Both help when you need several qualifying questions, and both are overkill for a name, phone and message. Switching form plugins

  1. Export existing entries, if the old plugin stores them.
  2. Rebuild each form in the new plugin, with the same fields, notifications and confirmation messages.
  3. Update conversion tracking, since a thank-you page or success event may change.
  4. Replace the forms on each page, then test every one from a phone and a desktop.
  5. Deactivate and delete the old plugin once you're sure nothing still uses it.

A note on custom forms

Not every site needs a plugin. This website, samverse.space, isn't built on WordPress: it's custom-coded and hosted on Hostinger, and its contact form uses a small PHP script on the server to email each enquiry, then opens WhatsApp with the same details typed in. Spam is handled by a hidden honeypot field, a simple addition question and a check that drops anything sent within seconds of the page loading. The Samverse case study explains the setup. On a WordPress site, a well-configured plugin is usually the simpler choice.

FAQs

Which WordPress form plugin is free?

Contact Form 7 is entirely free, and Fluent Forms and WPForms have free versions with paid upgrades. Gravity Forms is paid only, and Elementor Forms comes with Elementor Pro. What each free version includes changes, so check the current feature lists.

Do I need a paid form plugin for a business website?

Not for a simple contact or quote form. Paid plans earn their cost when you need something a free version leaves out, such as entry management, conditional logic, file uploads, multi-step forms, payments or CRM integrations.

Why are my form emails going to spam or not arriving?

Usually because WordPress is sending them from the hosting server without proper authentication. Set up SMTP with SPF, DKIM and DMARC for your domain, and store entries so nothing is lost while you fix it.

Can I use Elementor's form widget without Elementor Pro?

No. The form widget is a Pro feature. On free Elementor, add a form plugin and place its form on the page with a shortcode or the plugin's own widget.

Will a form plugin slow my website down?

A single form plugin rarely makes a noticeable difference if it loads its scripts only on pages with forms. Check this after installing, and don't keep two form plugins active for the same job.
