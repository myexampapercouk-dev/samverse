Google Merchant Center for WooCommerce: Set Up Free Listings and Fix Disapprovals

Key takeaways
  • Connect WooCommerce to Google Merchant Center with the free Google for WooCommerce extension or a feed plugin, then set shipping and returns to match your site.
  • Most disapprovals come from prices, GST or stock that do not match the product page, missing shipping details, poor images or missing contact and policy pages.
  • Free listings cost nothing, and the same clean product data later powers Shopping and Performance Max ads once conversion tracking is in place.

To show your WooCommerce products on Google for free, create a Google Merchant Center account, verify your website, and sync your products using the free Google for WooCommerce extension (previously called Google Listings & Ads) or a feed plugin. Then set your shipping and return details, make sure prices (including GST) and stock match your product pages, and fix anything Merchant Center flags. Approved products can appear as free listings in the Shopping tab and other Google surfaces, and the same data powers Shopping ads later.

Here's the full setup, which sync method to choose, the product data Google needs, and how to fix the disapprovals that trip up most Indian stores. If you'd rather have it set up and tested for you, that's part of my WooCommerce development work.

What Merchant Center does for a WooCommerce store

Merchant Center is where Google stores your product information. Once your products are approved, they can show with an image, price and store name when people search for what you sell.

Free listingsShopping ads and Performance Max
CostFreePay per click, through Google Ads
Where products can appearShopping tab, and other surfaces such as Search, Images and LensProminent ad slots across Search, Shopping, YouTube and more
ControlLittle: Google decides when to show themBudgets, bids, products and targeting
What you needMerchant Center and approved productsThe same, plus a linked Google Ads account and conversion tracking

Start with free listings. They cost nothing, and getting your product data clean is the hard part of Shopping ads anyway.

Before you start: get the store ready

Google checks your website as well as your products. New stores are often suspended for "misrepresentation" simply because basic trust information is missing. Before connecting anything, make sure you have:

  • Contact details on the site: a phone number, an email address and ideally a business address, matching your Merchant Center business information
  • A clear returns and refunds policy page; see returns and refunds for WooCommerce stores
  • Shipping information: where you deliver, costs and typical delivery times
  • Prices that include GST, since Google expects Indian prices to include tax; see WooCommerce GST setup
  • A working HTTPS checkout that lets a real customer complete an order
  • No placeholder content: no "Lorem ipsum", sample products or "coming soon" pages
  • Honest claims: no fake discounts, invented reviews or "best in India" claims you can't back up

Step 1: Create your Merchant Center account

  1. Sign in at Google Merchant Center with the Google account your business will keep long term, not a developer's personal account.
  2. Enter your business name, country (India) and website.
  3. Verify and claim your website. Merchant Center offers an HTML tag, a file upload or the Google tag; if you use the Google for WooCommerce extension, it can handle this for you.
  4. Fill in business information, customer service contact details and, later, link your Google Ads account.

Google renames Merchant Center menus fairly often, so if a setting isn't where you expect, use the search or the settings (gear) menu.

Step 2: Choose how to sync your products

MethodHow it worksBest for

Setting up Google for WooCommerce

  1. Go to Plugins > Add New, search for "Google for WooCommerce", then install and activate it.
  2. Open Marketing > Google for WooCommerce and click Get started.
  3. Connect the accounts it asks for: a WordPress.com account, your Google account, and a new or existing Merchant Center account.
  4. Choose where you sell (India) and how shipping rates are set: synced from WooCommerce or entered manually.
  5. Let the first sync run, then check the product feed screen for warnings.

Each product's edit screen gets a Google for WooCommerce section for extra attributes such as brand, GTIN, MPN and condition, and a channel visibility setting to keep particular products out of the sync.

Using a feed plugin instead

Create the feed in the plugin, map WooCommerce fields to Google's attributes (brand, GTIN, Google product category), and copy the feed URL. In Merchant Center, add a new product data source from a file, choose to fetch it from a link, and schedule a daily fetch. Make sure the fetch runs after your stock and price changes usually happen.

Step 3: Product data that gets approved

AttributeWhat Google wants
titleBrand, product type and key attributes: "YourBrand Cotton Kurti for Women, Blue, Size M"
descriptionAccurate details about the product, without promotional text or other products
image_linkA clear photo on a plain background, with no watermark, logo overlay or "sale" text
price and sale_priceIn INR, including GST, exactly as shown on the product page
availabilityIn stock, out of stock or preorder, matching the site
brand, gtin, mpnThe manufacturer's barcode (GTIN) where one exists; brand and MPN otherwise
identifier_existsSet to "no" for handmade or own-brand items that genuinely have no GTIN
item_group_idLinks variations (sizes, colours) of the same product; WooCommerce variations handle this

Titles matter most for which searches you show up for. Use the words shoppers search, not internal codes, and put the important attributes early.

Step 4: Set shipping and returns

In Merchant Center's shipping and returns settings, add a shipping service for India with your costs, any free-shipping threshold and realistic delivery times (handling time plus transit time). Add your return policy: the return window, whether returns are free, and the policy URL. If Google for WooCommerce syncs shipping from your WooCommerce zones, check the result, because complex rules don't always translate. Whatever you enter has to match what a shopper sees at checkout; see WooCommerce shipping setup for India.

Common disapprovals and how to fix them

Check the products that need attention in Merchant Center, and the diagnostics in Google for WooCommerce, every week at first:

IssueUsual causeFix
Price mismatchFeed price excludes GST, a scheduled sale price, a currency switcher, or a cached page showing an old priceShow GST-inclusive prices, sync sale prices, exclude product pages from long cache lifetimes
Availability mismatchStock changed after the last sync, or caching shows old stockSync more often, and purge cache when stock changes
Missing shippingNo shipping service set for IndiaAdd shipping in Merchant Center or the extension
Image issuesWatermarks, promotional text, collages or placeholder imagesUpload clean main images; keep lifestyle shots as additional images
Incorrect identifiersMade-up or reused GTINsUse real barcodes, or identifier_exists "no" where none exist
MisrepresentationMissing contact details or policies, unclear business identity, unrealistic claimsFix the site, then request a review from Merchant Center
Landing page not workingDeleted products, password protection, or pages blocked in robots.txtKeep product URLs live and crawlable

Caching is a frequent hidden cause of price and stock mismatches; see WordPress caching explained for what to exclude.

Product structured data

WooCommerce adds basic Product structured data to product pages, and SEO plugins can extend it. Google uses it to cross-check your feed, and Merchant Center's automatic item updates can use it to correct price and availability when they drift. Test a few product pages after any theme or plugin change; see schema markup explained and how to test structured data.

From free listings to Shopping ads

Once products are approved and stable, link Google Ads and the same data can run Shopping or Performance Max campaigns. Set up purchase tracking first, so you can see which products and campaigns actually make money; see Google Ads conversion tracking on WordPress. Start with your best sellers and products with healthy margins. The same product data can also feed Instagram and Facebook; see connecting WooCommerce to Meta's catalog.

FAQs

How do I connect WooCommerce to Google Merchant Center?

Install the free Google for WooCommerce extension, open Marketing > Google for WooCommerce, connect your Google account and Merchant Center, choose India as your selling country, set shipping, and let it sync your products. Alternatively, use a feed plugin to create a feed URL and add it to Merchant Center as a scheduled file.

Is Google Merchant Center free for WooCommerce stores?

Yes. Merchant Center, the Google for WooCommerce extension and free product listings cost nothing. You only pay if you run Shopping or Performance Max ads through Google Ads, or if you choose a paid feed plugin.

Is Google for WooCommerce the same as Google Listings & Ads?

Yes. Google Listings & Ads was renamed Google for WooCommerce. It's the official free extension for syncing WooCommerce products to Merchant Center and running Google ads from your dashboard.

Do I need GTINs (barcodes) for my products?

If a product has a manufacturer barcode, include it, because it helps Google match the product. For handmade, custom or own-brand products with no barcode, set identifier_exists to "no" rather than inventing a number. If you want barcodes for your own brand, they're issued in India through GS1 India.

Why are my WooCommerce products disapproved in Merchant Center?

The most common reasons are price or stock that don't match the product page, prices without GST, missing shipping details, images with text or watermarks, and incorrect identifiers. Account-level suspensions are usually misrepresentation: missing contact details, policies or business information. Merchant Center lists the exact issue for each product.

How long does it take for products to show on Google?

New products and accounts go through a review, which often takes a few days. After approval, free listings can start showing, but Google decides when and where, so impressions build gradually.
