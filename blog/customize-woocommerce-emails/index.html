Customising WooCommerce Order Emails: Branding, Content and Deliverability

How to customize WooCommerce emails: built-in settings, the email editor, template overrides, GST details, WhatsApp updates and SMTP so order emails arrive.

Key takeaways
  • Start with WooCommerce's built-in email settings: logo, colours, footer text and which emails go to customers and admins.
  • For deeper changes use the block email editor or a customiser plugin, or override templates in a child theme, never in the plugin folder.
  • A beautiful email is useless if it lands in spam; send through SMTP with SPF, DKIM and DMARC, and test every order status.

To customize WooCommerce emails, start in WooCommerce, Settings, Emails: add your logo, set brand colours, edit footer text and choose which emails are sent. For more control, use the newer block-based email editor where available or an email customiser plugin. Developers can override templates by copying them into a child theme. Then make sure emails arrive by sending them through SMTP with proper SPF, DKIM and DMARC records.

Order emails are the most-read messages your store sends. For Indian shoppers paying by UPI or choosing cash on delivery, a clear confirmation with order details, delivery expectations and support contact reduces anxious calls and cancellations.

Many Indian stores use "Completed" to mean shipped. If so, rename that meaning clearly in the email text, or add a custom "Shipped" status with its own email, so customers are not told an order is complete before it arrives.

How to customize WooCommerce emails: built-in settings

Go to WooCommerce, Settings, Emails. Here you can:

  • Enable or disable each email, and set recipients for admin emails (for example, the warehouse and accounts teams).
  • Edit the subject, heading and additional content for each email.
  • Use placeholders such as {site_title}, {order_number} and {order_date} in subjects and headings.
  • Set the header image (your logo), footer text, base colour, background and body text colours.
  • Set the "From" name and address. Use your store name and an address on your own domain.

Recent WooCommerce versions also include a block-based email editor and a live preview. If your version has it, it is the easiest way to adjust layout and wording without code.

What to put in Indian order emails

  • Clear order summary: items, variants, quantities, prices and totals including tax and shipping.
  • Payment method: "Paid via UPI", "Cash on delivery: please keep the exact amount ready", or bank transfer details for on-hold orders.
  • GST details: your GSTIN and whether prices include GST, as your CA advises. Attach or link a GST invoice if you generate one. WooCommerce GST invoices for India covers invoice plugins.
  • Delivery expectations: dispatch time and typical delivery window, honestly stated.
  • Tracking: courier name and tracking link once shipped, often added by Shiprocket or courier plugins.
  • Support: WhatsApp number, phone and email, with support hours.
  • Policies: short links to returns, exchanges and cancellation policies.

Avoid overloading the email with promotions. One small cross-sell or a link to follow on Instagram is plenty.

Template overrides done safely

WooCommerce email templates live in the plugin's templates/emails folder. To change one, copy it into your child theme at woocommerce/emails/ and edit the copy. Never edit the plugin's own files; updates will overwrite them. After WooCommerce updates, check WooCommerce, Status for outdated template warnings and update your copies. Updating WordPress safely explains why staging matters here.

Make sure order emails arrive

By default, WordPress sends email through the server's PHP mail function. On shared hosting, these emails often land in spam or never arrive, especially at Gmail and Outlook.

  1. Use an SMTP plugin connected to a proper sending service: your business email provider or a transactional service.
  2. Set SPF, DKIM and DMARC DNS records for your domain. Business email deliverability explains each one.
  3. Match the From address to the domain you authenticated.
  4. Turn on email logging so you can see what was sent and whether it failed.
  5. Test by placing real test orders with Gmail, Outlook and Yahoo addresses.

If emails stopped altogether, WordPress not sending emails walks through the fixes.

Writing better email copy

The default WooCommerce wording is polite but generic. A few small changes make order emails more useful and reduce support messages.

EmailDefault feelBetter approach
Processing (prepaid)"We have finished processing your order"Confirm payment received, say when it will be packed and dispatched
Processing (COD)Same as prepaidExplain any confirmation call or message, and that payment is collected on delivery
On-hold (bank transfer)Bank details in a blockClear steps, the reference to use and how long you will hold the order
Completed or shipped"Your order is complete"Courier, tracking link, expected delivery and what to do if a parcel is damaged
RefundedAmount refundedAmount, method and the usual time banks or UPI take to show it

Subject lines that customers recognise

Put the store name and order number in every subject, for example "Your order #1045 from Store Name is on its way". Customers search their inbox by store name when they have a problem, and a clear subject also looks less like spam. Keep subjects short enough to read on a phone, and avoid capital letters and too many symbols.

Different languages

If you sell mainly in one region, a short line in the local language alongside English can feel welcoming. Multilingual plugins can translate email templates fully if your store runs in more than one language; test each version with a real order.

WhatsApp and SMS alongside email

Many Indian shoppers check WhatsApp before email. Order updates over WhatsApp, through the official WhatsApp Business Platform via an approved provider, can complement email for confirmation, dispatch and delivery messages. Keep email as the record: it carries the invoice, full details and policies, and it is what customers forward to their bank or accounts team when they need proof of purchase. Always collect consent for WhatsApp messages, and avoid sending marketing to customers who only agreed to order updates.

Shopify order emails for comparison

If you are comparing platforms, Shopify handles customer notifications under Settings, Notifications, using editable Liquid templates with a preview. Branding and wording are easy to change; deliverability is handled by Shopify, though you should still authenticate your sender domain. Many of the same content rules apply: clear payment method, GST information, tracking and support details. Shopify development can cover notification templates too.

Test every status before going live

  • Place test orders with each payment method: UPI or gateway, COD and bank transfer.
  • Move each order through processing, completed, refunded and cancelled.
  • Check each email on a phone, in Gmail and Outlook, in light and dark mode.
  • Check the admin email goes to the right people.
  • Check that personal data shown in emails is limited to what the customer needs.

Common mistakes

  • Editing templates inside the WooCommerce plugin folder
  • Using a Gmail address as the From address on a store domain
  • Huge header images that look broken on mobile
  • Leaving the default "Thanks for shopping with us" text when your process needs more explanation, such as COD verification calls
  • Not checking emails after a WooCommerce update
  • Sending the admin new-order email to one person who is often on leave, so orders sit unnoticed
  • Forgetting that payment gateway, shipping and invoice plugins can each add their own text or attachments, which may repeat or contradict your template
  • Showing a support number nobody answers during the hours customers usually call

Custom statuses like "Shipped" work best as part of a clear order management workflow that the whole team follows.

FAQs

How do I customize WooCommerce emails?

Use WooCommerce, Settings, Emails for logo, colours and text, the block email editor or a customiser plugin for layout, and child theme template overrides for exact control.

Can I add a GST invoice to WooCommerce order emails?

Yes. Invoice plugins can attach a PDF invoice to order emails. Check the format and details with your CA.

Why are WooCommerce emails going to spam?

Usually because they are sent through the server without authentication. Use SMTP and set SPF, DKIM and DMARC for your domain.

Can I add a custom "Shipped" email?

Yes, with a custom order status plugin or a small custom plugin that adds the status and its email.

Will template changes break after updates?

Overrides can become outdated. Check WooCommerce, Status after updates and refresh the copies on staging first.
