AI Search Optimization for Your Website: How to Get Cited in Google AI Overviews and AI Assistants, From Clear Answers and Schema to Crawler Access, llms.txt and Measurement

AI search optimization: how AI Overviews and assistants pick sources, and how clear answers, schema and crawler access make your site easier to cite.

Key takeaways
  • AI Overviews and AI assistants mostly draw on pages that search engines can crawl and index, so the foundation is the same as good SEO: clear, trustworthy pages that answer questions directly.
  • Write quotable answers, keep your business details identical everywhere, add accurate structured data, and check that robots.txt and security tools don't block the crawlers you want.
  • llms.txt is an optional, unofficial proposal with unproven benefit; measure what you can through Search Console, GA4 referrals and asking new enquiries how they found you.

AI search optimization means making your website easy for AI-powered answers to find, understand and cite: Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode, and assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Perplexity and Claude when they search the web. At the time of writing there is no special markup or secret formula. These tools largely draw on pages that are crawlable, indexed and trustworthy, and they favour pages that answer a specific question clearly. So the work is mostly good SEO done carefully: direct answers, clean structure, consistent business details, accurate structured data, real experience, and not blocking the crawlers you want.

Making a site easy for search engines and AI tools to crawl and understand, from structure and schema to Search Console, is part of my WordPress SEO services. What I won't do is promise that a particular assistant will cite you. Nobody can honestly promise that, because each tool chooses sources in its own way and changes how it does so often.

This guide is about being cited by AI tools. If you're using AI to help write your content, that's a separate subject: see using AI tools for website content responsibly.

How AI answers use websites

Assistants that search the web while answeringTheir own crawlers and, for some, a search engine's index, such as Bing's for CopilotCrawler access, Bing Webmaster Tools, content quality

The middle row is changing fastest. Which index each assistant uses, and how it picks sources, varies between tools and over time, so treat any precise claim about it with caution.

What is known, and what isn't

  • Known: Google's guidance at the time of writing is that the same SEO best practices apply to its AI features, with no extra files or markup required, and that its usual snippet controls, such as nosnippet, also apply to AI Overviews.
  • Known: cited pages tend to answer the exact question being asked, clearly and near the top of a section.
  • Known: pages that can't be crawled or indexed can't be used for live answers.
  • Not known: the exact factors any assistant uses to choose sources. Tools that claim to measure an "AI ranking" are estimating, and anyone selling guaranteed AI citations is selling hope.

Write clear, quotable answers

AI answers are built from passages that state something clearly. Make it easy for a tool, and a person, to lift the right sentence:

  • Lead each section with the answer in one or two sentences, then explain.
  • Use headings that match how people ask, such as "How long does terrace waterproofing last?", rather than "Our Expertise".
  • Be specific: areas served, hours, what's included, the steps in your process, and prices only if you publish them.
  • Use tables for comparisons and options, and short FAQs for the questions customers ask before buying.
QuestionVague answerQuotable answer
Do you service ACs on Sundays?We offer flexible, customer-friendly service timings.Yes. We service split and window ACs across Pune every day, including Sundays, from 9 am to 7 pm. Book on WhatsApp.
Do you deliver outside the city?We serve customers far and wide.We deliver across Maharashtra in 3 to 5 days and ship to other states by courier.

These are illustrations for a fictional business; your answers must be true for yours. FAQs for SEO covers choosing and writing FAQ answers.

Structure pages so they're easy to extract

  • One main topic per page, with descriptive H2 and H3 headings in a logical order.
  • Important information in the page's text, not only inside images, PDFs or videos.
  • Key content visible in the HTML, not loaded only after clicks inside sliders or tabs that crawlers may not trigger.
  • Short paragraphs, lists for steps and tables for comparisons.
  • Fast, mobile-friendly pages that load without errors, because crawlers and visitors both give up on broken ones.

Make your business a clear entity

AI tools need to know that the "Example Dental Clinic" on your website, on Google Maps, on Practo and on Justdial is the same business. Inconsistent names, phone numbers or addresses make that harder.

  • Use the same name, address, phone and service descriptions on your website, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn and directory listings. Business directories and citations in India covers which listings matter.
  • Write a real About page: who runs the business, since when, where you work and what you specialise in.
  • Link your official profiles from the site, and list them in your Organization or LocalBusiness markup.
  • Earn mentions elsewhere: associations, suppliers, local news and partners describing what you do. Assistants, like search engines, give weight to what others say about you.

Structured data

Schema markup states facts about your business, services, products and articles in a form machines can't misread. Google says it isn't required for its AI features, but it helps Google understand a page, and it must always match the visible content. For most small businesses that means Organization or LocalBusiness, Service, Article, BreadcrumbList and, for stores, Product. Schema markup explained covers which types to use and a JSON-LD example.

Show experience and trust

When several pages say the same thing, the one with evidence of real experience is the safer source to cite. Name who wrote or reviewed the content, show credentials where they matter, and back claims with case studies, photos of your own work and genuine reviews. Crawl access: robots.txt and AI crawlers

Each AI company uses named crawlers, and robots.txt lets you allow or block them separately. The names below are as published at the time of writing; check each company's documentation, because they change.

CompanyCrawler namesWhat they're for
GoogleGooglebot; Google-ExtendedGooglebot crawls for Search, including AI Overviews. Google-Extended is a control token for use in Gemini models, and Google says it doesn't affect Search
MicrosoftBingbotBing search, which Copilot draws on
OpenAIOAI-SearchBot; GPTBot; ChatGPT-UserSearch results in ChatGPT; model training; visits made when a user asks ChatGPT to open a page
AnthropicClaude-SearchBot; ClaudeBot; Claude-UserSearch; model training; user-requested visits
PerplexityPerplexityBot; Perplexity-UserIts search index; user-requested visits

For most small businesses, being cited is useful, so allow the search crawlers. Whether to allow training crawlers is a separate choice. This robots.txt allows everything except one training crawler:

User-agent: GPTBot
Disallow: /

User-agent: *
Allow: /

Sitemap: https://example.com/sitemap.xml

robots.txt is a request that reputable crawlers follow, and some companies say their user-requested fetchers may treat it differently. Also check your security plugin, firewall and CDN: some offer one-click AI bot blocking, or switch it on by default, which can quietly block crawlers you wanted. robots.txt explained covers the file itself. llms.txt: an emerging, unofficial proposal

llms.txt is a plain-text file at the root of a site, written in Markdown, that summarises what the site is and links to its most important pages, intended for AI tools to read. It was proposed in 2024 and is not an official standard. Google has said it doesn't use llms.txt for Search, and at the time of writing there's little public evidence that the major assistants rely on it. It is cheap to add and harmless, which is the honest case for it. A short one looks like this:

# Example Dental Clinic

> A dental clinic in Kothrud, Pune, offering check-ups, root canal
> treatment, implants and braces. Open Monday to Saturday.

## Key pages
- [Treatments](https://example.com/treatments/): every treatment with what to expect
- [Contact](https://example.com/contact/): address, hours, phone and WhatsApp

This site, samverse.space, publishes one: it is custom-coded rather than built on WordPress, hosted on Hostinger, and its build script regenerates llms.txt with every service, case study and article whenever the site is rebuilt. The Samverse case study explains how. On WordPress, some SEO plugins now offer an option to generate one. Don't expect it to change your visibility on its own.

Measuring AI visibility

  • Search Console: at the time of writing, clicks and impressions from AI Overviews and AI Mode are included in the normal Web search figures, not reported separately.
  • GA4 referrals: visits from chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai, gemini.google.com and copilot.microsoft.com show up as referral traffic. A saved report or a custom channel group keeps them in one place. Setting up GA4 and Search Console covers the basics.
  • Ask the assistants: once a month, ask your main customer questions in the tools your customers use and note whether you're mentioned. Answers vary between people and over time, so look for trends, not single results.
  • Ask your enquiries: add "AI assistant, such as ChatGPT" to the "How did you hear about us?" options on your form.

What doesn't work

  • Hidden text or instructions aimed at AI tools, which search engines treat as spam
  • Pages stuffed with questions and keywords that nobody would read
  • Mass-produced AI pages, which break Google's scaled content policies
  • Buying mentions, fake reviews or "AI ranking" packages

FAQs

Is AI search optimization different from SEO?

Mostly not. Google says the same practices apply to its AI features. The emphasis shifts slightly towards clear, quotable answers, consistent business information across the web and checking AI crawler access.

How do I get my business mentioned in ChatGPT and other assistants?

Make sure your site can be crawled, answer customers' questions clearly, keep your details identical on every listing, and earn genuine mentions on other reputable sites. No one can guarantee a mention.

Should I block AI crawlers?

Blocking search-related crawlers can stop you being cited in AI answers. Training crawlers are a separate choice. Decide deliberately, and check that a security tool hasn't decided for you.

Do I need an llms.txt file?

No. It is an unofficial proposal, Google doesn't use it for Search, and its benefit is unproven. It's harmless and quick to add if you want one.

Can I see whether AI Overviews send me traffic?

Not separately at the time of writing. Search Console includes them in the Web search totals. Referrals from AI assistants are visible in GA4.
