WordPress vs Squarespace: Which Is Better for Your Business Website?

WordPress vs Squarespace in India: ownership, flexibility, cost over time, Indian payments, SEO, moving later, and when Shopify suits a store better.

Key takeaways
  • Squarespace is quick and tidy for simple portfolio-style sites, while WordPress gives full ownership, more flexibility and a far larger pool of Indian developers and plugins.
  • For Indian payments, GST invoices, regional shipping and local integrations, WordPress with WooCommerce or Shopify is usually the more practical choice.
  • If selling online is the main goal, compare Shopify too; if content, leads and long-term control matter most, WordPress is the safer base.

For most Indian businesses, WordPress is the better choice than Squarespace because you own the site, can add almost any feature, and can easily find local developers and Indian payment integrations. Squarespace suits people who want a simple, good-looking site they can manage alone without plugins or hosting. If your main goal is an online store, Shopify is also worth comparing.

Both platforms can produce a professional website. The difference shows up over time: when you need a new feature, want to change providers, or start selling to customers who pay by UPI and expect GST invoices. This guide compares WordPress vs Squarespace from an Indian business point of view.

Ownership: who really controls your website

With self-hosted WordPress, you own everything: the files, the database and the domain. If you are unhappy with your host or developer, you can move the whole site elsewhere. With Squarespace, the site runs only on Squarespace. You can export some content, but the design, many page layouts and store settings cannot be moved to another platform as they are.

This matters more than it seems at the start. A business website often lasts many years, and moving later usually means rebuilding. Whatever you choose, keep the domain registered in your own name and keep admin access yourself; see the website ownership checklist.

Flexibility and features

Squarespace includes the basics well: pages, a blog, galleries, simple forms, basic e-commerce and scheduling. If you need something it does not offer, your options are limited.

WordPress can do almost anything through plugins or custom code. Common needs for Indian businesses that are easy on WordPress include:

  • Booking systems for clinics, salons and classes
  • Multi-step lead forms with WhatsApp and CRM integration
  • Hindi or regional language versions of pages
  • Membership areas, course portals or member directories
  • Product catalogues with enquiry buttons instead of checkout
  • Integration with Indian SMS, shipping and accounting tools

The trade-off is responsibility. Every plugin adds updates to manage, and poorly chosen plugins can slow the site or create security risks. A well-built WordPress site uses only what it needs.

Cost over time

Squarespace charges a recurring subscription, with hosting included, and the price depends on the plan and features you need. WordPress itself is free, but you pay for hosting, a domain, any premium theme or plugins, and usually a developer to build and maintain it.

Neither is always cheaper. A simple site may cost less on Squarespace if you build it yourself. A site that needs custom features, Indian integrations or ongoing marketing work often costs less in the long run on WordPress, because you are not paying for a higher plan just to unlock one feature. Compare your own needs over three to five years, not just the first year. To estimate a WordPress build, try the website cost calculator.

Indian payments and selling online

This is where the difference is clearest for Indian businesses. Customers expect UPI, cards, net banking, wallets and often cash on delivery, along with GST-compliant invoices and Indian courier integrations.

WordPress with WooCommerce supports Indian gateways such as Razorpay, PayU and Cashfree, plus COD, GST invoice plugins and shipping integrations such as Shiprocket. Squarespace's payment and shipping options are designed mainly for other markets, so check carefully whether what you need is available in India before committing. See payment gateways in India compared for the options.

What about Shopify for online stores?

If your website is mainly a shop, the real comparison is often WordPress with WooCommerce versus Shopify, not Squarespace. Shopify is built for selling: it handles hosting, checkout and security, and supports Indian payment gateways, COD and shipping apps. WooCommerce gives more control and flexibility, especially for custom pricing, content-heavy stores or catalogue-plus-enquiry setups.

SEO and speed

Both platforms let you edit titles, descriptions and URLs, create sitemaps and publish blogs, so either can rank. Squarespace handles many technical basics automatically but gives less control. WordPress, with an SEO plugin, gives full control over schema, redirects, indexing and site structure, which matters more as the site grows.

Speed depends mostly on how the site is built. Squarespace sites are reasonably fast by default but heavy templates and large images can slow them. WordPress speed depends on hosting, theme and plugins; a lean build on good hosting is fast, while a bloated one is slow. If your WordPress site is already slow, WordPress speed optimization can usually fix it without a rebuild.

Who will look after the site?

A practical question that often decides the platform is who will keep the site running after launch. On Squarespace, the platform handles hosting, security and software updates, and you edit content in its editor. If something is missing, you mostly wait for Squarespace to add it or work around it.

On WordPress, someone needs to update WordPress, the theme and plugins, take backups and watch security. Many owners do simple content edits themselves and leave updates to a developer on a monthly plan. In India it is easy to find help for this, and you can change who looks after the site at any time because you own it.

Before choosing, list the people involved: who writes content, who answers enquiries, who handles payments and who you call when something breaks. If the answer to the last one is "nobody", a hosted builder or Shopify may suit you better for now. If you already work with a developer or agency, WordPress lets them build exactly what you need and hand over a site your team can edit.

Also think about the people who need access. WordPress has user roles, so a content writer can publish blog posts without being able to change plugins or payment settings, which suits growing teams.

WordPress vs Squarespace in India: which should you choose?

Choose Squarespace if you want a simple portfolio or brochure site, you will build and edit it yourself, you don't need Indian payment gateways or special integrations, and you prefer one subscription with no maintenance.

Choose WordPress if you want full ownership, expect to add features over time, need Indian payments or integrations, plan to invest in SEO and content, or want a developer to build something tailored to your business.

Consider Shopify if selling products online is the main purpose and you want the platform to handle the technical side.

Moving from Squarespace to WordPress later

Many businesses start on a website builder and move to WordPress as they grow. The move is very doable but is essentially a rebuild: blog posts and some pages can be imported, but the design, forms and store need to be recreated. Plan redirects from old URLs to new ones so you keep any search rankings, and move the domain carefully so email is not interrupted. Website migration covers this process.

FAQs

WordPress vs Squarespace: which is better for a small business in India?

WordPress usually suits Indian small businesses better because of ownership, flexibility, Indian payment support and the number of local developers. Squarespace suits simple sites you will manage alone.

Is Squarespace easier than WordPress?

Yes, for building a simple site yourself. WordPress has a learning curve at setup, but once built, editing pages and posts is straightforward.

Is WordPress better for SEO than Squarespace?

Both can rank. WordPress gives more control over technical SEO, which helps larger or content-heavy sites. For a small site, content quality matters more than the platform.

Can I accept UPI payments on Squarespace?

Check Squarespace's current list of supported payment providers for India. WordPress with WooCommerce and Shopify both support UPI through Indian gateways.

Should I use Shopify instead of Squarespace for an online store?

For a store selling in India, Shopify or WooCommerce is usually the more practical choice because of Indian payment, COD, GST and shipping support.

Can I move my Squarespace website to WordPress later?

Yes, but it is a rebuild rather than a one-click move. Pages and posts are copied or exported, the design is recreated, your domain is pointed to the new hosting, and any changed URLs are redirected so you keep the visibility you have built. Connecting your own domain from day one makes this much easier.
