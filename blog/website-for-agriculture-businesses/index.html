Websites for Agriculture and Agri-Input Companies

What agriculture businesses (seeds, fertilisers, irrigation, farm equipment, agri-produce traders) need online: product catalogues, crop guides, dealer locators, regional languages and enquiries.

Sameer Gupta 27 September 2026 · 3 min read

Key takeaways
  • Serve farmers, dealers and bulk buyers with clear paths for each.
  • Catalogues need crop suitability, usage guidance and a dealer locator.
  • Regional languages, helpful crop content and light pages widen reach.

Agriculture businesses serve farmers, dealers, distributors and institutional buyers, often across regions and languages. A clear website builds credibility with all of them and helps generate dealer and bulk enquiries.

Know your audiences

  • Farmers: practical product information, usage guidance, where to buy
  • Dealers and distributors: product range, margins and dealership enquiries
  • Institutional and export buyers: specifications, certifications and capacity

Product catalogue

  • Products by category: seeds, fertilisers, crop protection, irrigation, equipment, produce
  • Crops and conditions each product suits
  • Usage, dosage or application guidance where appropriate, consistent with labels and regulations
  • Pack sizes and downloadable leaflets

Where to buy

A dealer locator or list of dealers by state and district helps farmers find your products locally and supports your distribution network.

Regional languages

Many farmers prefer regional languages. Key pages in the languages of your main markets can dramatically improve reach; see multilingual websites.

Helpful content

Crop guides, seasonal advice and videos demonstrating products attract searches and build trust. Keep advice accurate and practical.

Enquiries

  • Dealership enquiry form
  • Bulk and export enquiry form
  • WhatsApp and toll-free numbers

Mobile and speed

Many visitors use basic phones on rural networks. Keep pages light and fast; see speed on Indian mobile networks.
