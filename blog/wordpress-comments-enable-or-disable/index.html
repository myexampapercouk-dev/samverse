WordPress Comments: Should a Business Website Enable Them?

Should a business website allow comments on WordPress? Pros and cons, spam and moderation, when comments add value, alternatives, and how to disable or manage them properly.

Key takeaways
  • Enable comments only if you'll moderate and they add genuine value.
  • Most brochure-style business sites are better with comments off.
  • Use FAQs, WhatsApp prompts or social discussion as alternatives.

WordPress comes with comments enabled on posts by default. For many business websites, they bring more spam than value. For others, they build community. Here's how to decide.

When comments add value

  • You publish articles that genuinely spark discussion or questions
  • You have time to reply and moderate regularly
  • Comments add useful information (questions, experiences) to posts
  • You're building a community or publication

When to disable them

  • Most comments you receive are spam
  • Nobody has time to moderate
  • Your site is mainly service pages and brochure content
  • Unanswered or spammy comments would look neglected
  • Regulated industries where public comments could create compliance issues

The downsides

  • Spam and malicious links
  • Moderation time
  • Low-quality comments reflecting on your brand
  • Extra scripts and database load

If you enable comments

  • Require moderation for first-time commenters
  • Use spam filtering
  • Add links in comments as nofollow/UGC (WordPress does this by default)
  • Reply promptly and helpfully
  • Close comments on older posts automatically

Alternatives

  • An FAQ section updated with common questions; see FAQ sections
  • A "questions? message us on WhatsApp" prompt at the end of posts
  • Discussion on LinkedIn or social posts where you share articles

How to disable comments

Turn off comments for new posts in Settings → Discussion, close them on existing posts in bulk, and remove comment sections from theme templates if needed. This site uses WhatsApp and a contact form instead of comments.
