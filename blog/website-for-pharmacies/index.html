Websites for Pharmacies and Medical Stores: What to Include

By Sameer Gupta · 27 September 2026 · 3 min read

On this page
  1. Core information
  2. Prescription and delivery enquiries
  3. Compliance first
  4. Health and wellness products
  5. Privacy
  6. Local SEO
  7. Mobile first
Key takeaways
  • Show locations, hours, services and fast phone and WhatsApp contact.
  • Online medicine sales are regulated, so confirm current requirements before selling online.
  • Protect prescription data and win "near me" searches with a strong Google Business Profile.

For a neighbourhood pharmacy or chain of medical stores, a website mainly helps people find you, trust you and contact you quickly, especially for home delivery. Online sale of medicines is regulated, so it's important to plan features carefully.

Core information

  • Store locations, opening hours (including late-night or 24-hour stores) and maps
  • Phone and WhatsApp for orders and availability questions
  • Services such as home delivery areas, health check-ups, blood pressure or sugar testing, and equipment rental
  • Pharmacist details and licence information displayed as required

Prescription and delivery enquiries

A simple, secure way for customers to request medicines and share prescriptions for the pharmacist to review, followed by confirmation over phone or WhatsApp, is often the most practical approach for local pharmacies.

Compliance first

Selling medicines online, especially prescription drugs, is subject to law and regulation, which can change. Before adding online ordering or payment for medicines, confirm the current requirements with a legal adviser. Over-the-counter health and wellness products may be simpler to sell online. Keep health information general and accurate, and avoid treatment claims.

Health and wellness products

Many pharmacies sell supplements, personal care, baby care, and medical devices. These can be presented as a catalogue or an online store where permitted. See WooCommerce development.

Privacy

Prescriptions and health details are sensitive. Use HTTPS, collect only what's needed, restrict access to uploaded prescriptions, and publish a clear privacy policy.

Local SEO

  • Target "medical store near me", "24 hour pharmacy in {area}" and "medicine home delivery {city}"
  • A complete Google Business Profile for each store, with accurate hours
  • Consistent name, address and phone everywhere; see the local SEO guide

Mobile first

People often search for pharmacies urgently on their phones. Fast pages, tap-to-call, WhatsApp and clear hours matter most. For related healthcare websites, see websites for doctors and clinics.
