Online Appointment Booking on Your Website: Which Tool to Use, How to Set It Up and How to Cut No-Shows

How to add online appointment booking to your website: WhatsApp requests, Calendly, Amelia, Bookly, reminders, Razorpay deposits and fewer no-shows.

Key takeaways
  • Pick the booking method by how you work: a request form or WhatsApp for a single practice, an embedded scheduler for consultants, a WordPress booking plugin for staff and services, or your clinic or salon software's own widget.
  • Set services, durations, buffers, holidays and booking windows carefully, and keep phone and walk-in bookings in the same calendar to avoid double bookings.
  • Reminders with an easy reschedule link, plus deposits for first visits or long appointments, do more to cut no-shows than anything else.

To add online appointment booking to your website, choose one of four routes: a booking request form or WhatsApp button that your staff confirm, an embedded scheduling service such as Calendly, a WordPress booking plugin such as Amelia, Bookly or Simply Schedule Appointments, or the booking widget from the clinic, salon or gym software you already use. The right one depends on whether you need real-time slots, several staff, payments and records. Whichever you choose, the setup and the reminders matter more than the tool.

For clinics, I can start with an appointment enquiry form that emails the clinic and opens WhatsApp, or connect the booking system the clinic already uses; see websites for doctors and clinics. For salons, consultants, tutors and other service businesses, booking is part of a normal WordPress website build.

Below: how the options compare, what I've built for real clinics and a restaurant, how to set booking up so it doesn't double-book, and how to cut no-shows with reminders and deposits.

What I've built: booking requests that suit small practices

Real-time booking isn't always the best answer. Many doctors run sessions rather than fixed slots, emergencies change the day, and reception already keeps a diary. For those practices, a well-built booking request works better than a calendar that pretends the schedule is fixed.

  • Dr. Nitish Gupta, pulmonologist in Agra: a "Book an Appointment" button in the top bar on every page leads to a form that asks for name, email, phone, a preferred date and a time slot, with the dropdown listing the clinic's morning and evening sessions. The clinic receives a requested date and session, not a bare "please call me". See the Dr. Nitish Gupta case study.
  • Dr. Sunaina Dental Care, Agra: the "Book Now" and "Book Appointment" buttons open a WhatsApp chat with the clinic, because that's how its patients already message. A three-field form is the backup. See the Dr. Sunaina Dental Care case study.
  • Dr. Sudhir Arora: the online consultation page has one instruction, to send a WhatsApp message to book, and the link opens WhatsApp with the message already written.
  • Southern Flavours, a restaurant in Solihull: a short table reservation form asks for party size and date, and shows "Reservation received! We'll confirm shortly." on screen as soon as it's sent.

If you outgrow requests, because staff spend too long confirming or you want deposits, move to a scheduler or a plugin. More on WhatsApp buttons and pre-filled messages in WhatsApp on your business website.

Choosing a booking tool

Calendly and other embedded schedulers

Calendly, Zoho Bookings and Google Calendar's appointment schedules let visitors pick a free slot from your calendar and add a video link for online sessions. They're quick to set up and embed on a WordPress page. Free plans are limited, and features such as several event types, team scheduling, reminders or payments may need a paid plan at the time of writing. Check which payment gateways each supports for Indian customers before you rely on it for deposits.

Amelia

Amelia is a WordPress booking plugin built around services, staff and locations, with a customer and employee panel, calendar sync and payments. There is a limited free version and paid licences for the full feature set. It can take payments directly or through WooCommerce, which opens up Indian gateways such as Razorpay; check its current gateway list.

Bookly

Bookly has a free core plugin on WordPress.org with many features, such as extra staff, SMS notifications, payment gateways and recurring appointments, sold as paid add-ons. It suits businesses that want to start small and add pieces as they need them. Add up the add-ons you'll need before comparing it with Amelia.

Simply Schedule Appointments

Simply Schedule Appointments has a free version for basic bookings and paid tiers that add things like Google Calendar sync, payments, SMS reminders and team booking at the time of writing. It's straightforward to set up for consultants and small teams.

Plugin features and pricing change often, so check each vendor's site and try the free or demo version with your real services before you buy.

Set it up so it doesn't double-book

Most booking problems come from setup, not the tool. Before going live, configure:

  • Services: names, durations and prices, written the way customers describe them.
  • Staff: who provides which services, and each person's working hours.
  • Breaks and holidays: lunch, weekly offs and festival closures such as Diwali, added in advance.
  • Buffer time between appointments for cleaning, notes or travel.
  • Minimum notice: how close to the time someone can book, so nobody books a slot that starts in ten minutes.
  • Booking window: how far ahead people can book, often a few weeks.
  • Time zone: essential if you offer online consultations to people abroad.
  • Calendar sync: two-way sync with Google Calendar or Outlook, so personal commitments block slots too.

The most common cause of double bookings is phone and walk-in appointments that never reach the online calendar. If reception takes bookings by phone, they must enter them in the same system, or online booking should only offer slots reception has released.

Collect only what you need

Name, phone number and the service are usually enough. Avoid asking for medical history or reports in an open booking form: that's sensitive personal data that needs careful storage and access control. DPDP Act basics for websites covers consent and privacy notices.

Reminders: email, SMS and WhatsApp

Automatic reminders are one of the most effective ways to reduce missed appointments. A common pattern is a confirmation when the booking is made, a reminder the day before and another a couple of hours before, each with a link to reschedule or cancel.

  • Email is built into every booking tool, but easy to miss. Make sure your site sends email through SMTP so reminders don't land in spam.
  • SMS in India needs your sender ID and message templates registered on the DLT system, which SMS providers help you through. Booking plugins usually connect to SMS through an add-on or provider.
  • WhatsApp reminders sent automatically need the WhatsApp Business Platform (API) through a provider, with message templates approved in advance and per-message charges. The free WhatsApp Business app is fine for sending reminders by hand.

Deposits and online payments with Razorpay

Asking for payment up front changes behaviour: people who've paid are more likely to turn up. You don't need it for every appointment, but consider a deposit or full payment for first-time customers, long appointments, packages and high-demand slots.

  • Booking plugins can take payments through gateways such as Razorpay, Cashfree or PayU, usually via an add-on or WooCommerce, so customers can pay by UPI, card or net banking.
  • If you take bookings by WhatsApp, a Razorpay payment link sent with the confirmation is a simple way to collect a deposit without changing the website.
  • State the cancellation and refund policy next to the payment, in plain words: how much notice, and what's refunded.

Many clinics prefer payment at the visit, and that's fine. See accepting online payments on WordPress in India for gateway setup.

Cutting no-shows at clinics and salons

  1. Send reminders with a one-tap reschedule link. People who can't easily reschedule tend not to come and not to tell you.
  2. Take deposits from first-time customers and for long or high-value appointments.
  3. Set a clear cancellation window and show it at booking.
  4. Confirm next-day appointments by WhatsApp the evening before, and release unconfirmed slots to a waiting list.
  5. Keep a simple record of no-shows by service and booking channel, so you know where to tighten the rules.

Salons have the extra puzzle of staff and service combinations; websites for salons and spas covers that side.

Make the booking button easy to find

  • Put a "Book Appointment" button in the header and the first screen of every page, alongside tap-to-call and WhatsApp.
  • Keep the booking page fast and simple on a phone, where many people will book.
  • Add your booking link to your Google Business Profile, so people can book straight from Google; see the Google Business Profile checklist.
  • Track completed bookings as a conversion in Google Analytics so you know which pages and campaigns bring appointments.

FAQs

How do I add an appointment booking system to my website?

Decide whether you need booking requests or real-time slots. For requests, add a form with preferred date and session plus a WhatsApp button. For real-time slots, embed a scheduler such as Calendly or install a WordPress plugin such as Amelia, Bookly or Simply Schedule Appointments, then set up services, hours, buffers and reminders.

What is the best appointment booking plugin for WordPress?

It depends on your needs. Amelia suits multi-staff businesses that want a polished all-in-one plugin, Bookly suits those who want a free core and paid add-ons, and Simply Schedule Appointments suits consultants and small teams. Try each with your real services before buying.

Can I take online appointment bookings for free?

Yes, with limits. A booking request form with WhatsApp costs nothing beyond the website, and Bookly, Simply Schedule Appointments and several schedulers have free versions. Reminders by SMS or automated WhatsApp, payments and multiple staff usually need paid plans or add-ons.

Can customers pay a deposit when they book online?

Yes. Most booking plugins can take payments through Indian gateways such as Razorpay, directly or via WooCommerce, with UPI and cards. If you book over WhatsApp, a payment link sent with the confirmation does the same job.

How do I send WhatsApp appointment reminders automatically?

Automatic reminders need the WhatsApp Business Platform through a provider, with approved message templates, connected to your booking tool. Providers charge per message. For a small practice, sending reminders by hand from the WhatsApp Business app can be enough.

Is it safe to collect patient details in a booking form?

Collect only what you need to book: name, phone and the reason for the visit in general terms. Don't collect reports or detailed medical history through an open form, and explain in your privacy notice what you collect and why.
