Online Appointment Booking on Your Website: Setup, Reminders and Fewer No-Shows

How to add online appointment booking to a business website: booking tools and WordPress plugins, services and staff setup, calendar sync, reminders, deposits, cancellation policies and reducing no-shows.

Key takeaways
  • Choose a scheduling tool, WordPress plugin or industry software for bookings.
  • Set services, staff, hours and buffers, and sync with your calendar.
  • Automatic reminders, deposits and clear cancellation rules reduce no-shows.

For clinics, salons, consultants, tutors and many service businesses, letting customers book online saves hours of phone calls and captures bookings at any time of day. Here's how to set it up well.

Options

  • Scheduling services embedded in your website, such as Calendly-style tools
  • WordPress booking plugins such as Amelia, Bookly or Simply Schedule Appointments
  • Industry software: clinic, salon or fitness management systems with online booking

Industry software is often best if you need records, billing and staff management; simpler tools suit consultants and small teams.

Set up services properly

  • Service names, durations and prices
  • Staff members and which services each provides
  • Working hours, breaks and holidays
  • Buffer time between appointments
  • How far in advance people can book

Calendar sync

Sync bookings with Google Calendar or Outlook so you don't get double-booked with other commitments.

Reminders

Automatic reminders by email, SMS or WhatsApp a day before and a few hours before greatly reduce missed appointments. WhatsApp reminders usually need a WhatsApp Business API provider with approved message templates.

Deposits and payments

  • Take a deposit or full payment for longer or high-value appointments
  • Offer UPI and cards
  • State your cancellation and refund policy clearly

Businesses that need a consultation before a booking, such as tattoo studios, can pair an enquiry form with a deposit; see websites for tattoo studios.

Reduce no-shows

  • Reminders with easy reschedule links
  • Deposits for first-time customers
  • A clear cancellation window
  • Confirmation messages that feel personal

Make booking visible

  • A "Book appointment" button in the header and on every service page
  • A "Book" button on your Google Business Profile, where supported
  • WhatsApp as an alternative for people who prefer chat

Keep forms short and private

Ask only for what you need. For health or sensitive services, avoid collecting detailed information in booking forms, and have a privacy policy; see privacy basics.

Track bookings

Track completed bookings as key events; see GA4 events.
