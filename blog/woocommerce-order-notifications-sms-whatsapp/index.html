WooCommerce WhatsApp Order Notifications, SMS and Email: How to Set Them Up in India

Set up WooCommerce WhatsApp order notifications, SMS and email in India: Business API templates, DLT rules for SMS, opt-in, COD confirmation and testing.

Key takeaways
  • Email remains the record for every order; WhatsApp and SMS are for short, timely updates such as confirmation, dispatch and delivery.
  • Automated WhatsApp messages need the WhatsApp Business Platform with approved templates, and business SMS in India needs DLT registration first.
  • Collect clear opt-in at checkout, send only messages customers expect, and test every order status before going live.

WooCommerce WhatsApp order notifications send customers automatic updates, such as order confirmed, shipped with tracking, and delivered, through the WhatsApp Business Platform using pre-approved message templates. In India, most stores combine them with email (the full record) and sometimes SMS, which requires DLT registration. The setup involves a WhatsApp provider or plugin, templates for each order status, customer opt-in and careful testing.

This guide explains which messages are worth sending, how each channel works in India, and how I set them up in WooCommerce.

Fewer, well-timed messages work better than a message for every internal status change. "Processing" and "on hold" are your statuses, not the customer's.

Email: the record that must arrive

WooCommerce sends order emails by default, but many stores have delivery problems because the site sends mail through the hosting server. Use a proper SMTP or transactional email service, set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC on your domain, and send a test order to Gmail and Outlook addresses. Customise the emails with your logo, support contact, return policy link and GST invoice attachment. See customising WooCommerce emails.

WhatsApp: manual or the Business Platform?

There are two very different ways to use WhatsApp for orders:

  • WhatsApp Business app (manual): staff send messages from a phone. Fine for a handful of orders a day, but it does not scale and depends on someone remembering.
  • WhatsApp Business Platform (API): messages are sent automatically when an order status changes, through Meta directly or an approved provider. Business-initiated messages must use templates approved by Meta, and are charged per conversation or message under Meta's current pricing.

For automated order notifications, you need the platform route. A WooCommerce plugin or a provider's integration connects order statuses to your templates.

How to set up WooCommerce WhatsApp order notifications

  1. Choose a provider that supports the WhatsApp Business Platform and has a WooCommerce integration.
  2. Verify your business with Meta and register a phone number that is not already used on a regular WhatsApp app.
  3. Write templates for each message: order confirmation, COD confirmation, shipped, out for delivery. Use variables for name, order number, amount and tracking link.
  4. Submit templates for approval and wait for them to be approved before connecting them.
  5. Map templates to order statuses in the plugin, and to shipping events if your courier integration supports it.
  6. Format phone numbers consistently with the country code, since Indian customers type numbers in many ways.
  7. Test with real orders for each payment method and status.

Writing templates that get read

  • Start with what happened: "Your order #1234 has been shipped."
  • Include the one action they might need: the tracking link or a reply option.
  • Keep the brand name clear, so it does not look like spam.
  • Avoid promotional lines inside order updates; templates in the utility category should stay transactional.
  • Offer a support route, such as "Reply here for help".

COD confirmation on WhatsApp

For stores with many cash on delivery orders, a WhatsApp message asking the customer to confirm the order before dispatch can reduce fake orders and returns. Typical flow: the order is placed, a template with "Confirm" and "Cancel" buttons is sent, the order status updates based on the reply, and unconfirmed orders are followed up by a call. More on reducing COD risk in WooCommerce cash on delivery in India.

Connecting courier tracking to messages

The most useful update is usually "shipped, here is your tracking link", but WooCommerce does not know when a courier picks up a parcel unless something tells it. Common setups in India:

  • A shipping aggregator such as Shiprocket syncs AWB numbers and status back to WooCommerce, and the order status change triggers the WhatsApp template.
  • Staff add the tracking number manually and mark the order as shipped, which sends the message.
  • The aggregator sends its own tracking messages, and the store sends only the order confirmation, so customers do not get the same update twice.

Pick one source for each message. Duplicates from the store and the courier confuse customers and cost you per message. Shipping setup is covered in WooCommerce shipping for India.

Which channel for which store?

Store situationSensible starting point
A few orders a day, mostly prepaidGood email setup, manual WhatsApp for questions
Growing order volume, many COD ordersEmail plus automated WhatsApp with COD confirmation
Customers in areas with weak data coverageEmail and WhatsApp, with SMS as a fallback for key updates
Accounts that need OTP loginSMS for OTPs through a DLT-registered provider

Start with the smallest set that solves your real problem, usually "where is my order" questions or COD returns, and add channels only when the first one is working well.

SMS in India: DLT registration first

Business SMS in India is regulated through the DLT system. Before your store can send SMS:

  • Register your business as a principal entity on a DLT platform run by a telecom operator.
  • Register a sender ID (header) for your brand.
  • Register each message template, with variables marked as the platform requires.
  • Use an SMS provider that passes your registered template IDs with each message.

Unregistered messages may not be delivered. The rules and portals change, so check the current process with your SMS provider. Many stores now use SMS only as a backup to WhatsApp or for OTPs.

When customers reply

Customers will reply to order messages: "Can you deliver tomorrow instead?", "Wrong size, can I exchange?". Plan who reads these replies and how quickly. Most providers offer a shared inbox where several staff can answer from a browser, with the order number visible. Set expected reply hours, add an auto-reply outside them, and link the conversation to the order where the tool allows it. An unanswered reply undoes the good impression the automatic update created, so this matters as much as the templates themselves.

Opt-in, and not spamming

Ask for consent at checkout with a clear checkbox, such as "Send me order updates on WhatsApp". Record that consent with the order. Keep order updates separate from marketing: a customer who agreed to delivery updates did not necessarily agree to offers. Make it easy to stop messages, and mention WhatsApp and SMS updates in your privacy policy. For marketing messages, see WhatsApp marketing for small businesses.

Some stores go a step further and let customers place the order on WhatsApp in the first place. The guide to taking Shopify orders on WhatsApp covers how those orders reach the admin.

Shopify stores

Shopify has built-in email notifications and supports WhatsApp and SMS through apps that connect to the same Business Platform and DLT rules. The approach above applies; the setup happens in the app instead of a WooCommerce plugin. If you are choosing a platform, compare them in WooCommerce vs Shopify in India.

Testing checklist before going live

  • Place prepaid and COD test orders, and confirm each message arrives once, not twice.
  • Check that variables (name, order number, amount, link) fill correctly.
  • Try phone numbers with and without +91, spaces and a leading zero.
  • Change statuses manually in the admin and confirm the right template fires.
  • Check refunds and cancellations do not send a "shipped" message by mistake.
  • Confirm email still arrives if the WhatsApp provider is down.

Once live, review failed messages weekly in the provider dashboard. Repeated failures for the same customer usually mean a mistyped number, so a quick call can fix the record before the next order. Fit it into your order management workflow so staff know who checks what.

FAQs

How do I send WooCommerce order notifications on WhatsApp?

Use the WhatsApp Business Platform through a provider with a WooCommerce plugin, get templates approved, map them to order statuses, then test every status.

Is WhatsApp order notification free?

Automated business messages are charged by Meta under its current pricing, and providers may add their own fees. Check current rates before choosing.

Do I need DLT registration for WooCommerce SMS in India?

Yes, business SMS generally requires registering your business, sender ID and templates on a DLT platform. Your SMS provider can guide you.

Can I use my normal WhatsApp number for automatic messages?

Not for automation through the platform while it is active on the regular app. Most businesses register a dedicated number.

Should I stop sending order emails if I use WhatsApp?

No. Email carries the full order record and invoice. WhatsApp complements it with short updates.
