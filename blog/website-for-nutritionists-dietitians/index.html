Website for Nutritionists and Dietitians: Attract Clients Online

How nutritionists and dietitians can build a website that brings consultations: credentials, programmes, online booking, pricing, responsible results, recipes and local SEO.

Sameer Gupta 27 September 2026 · 3 min read

Key takeaways
  • Show qualifications, programme pages, how consultations work and pricing.
  • Offer online booking, payment and WhatsApp, with a short intake form.
  • Share results responsibly, protect health data and publish practical recipes and guides.

People looking for a nutritionist or dietitian want someone qualified, relatable and practical. Many will also be comparing online consultants from other cities. A good website shows your expertise and makes booking easy.

What potential clients look for

  • Your qualifications and registrations
  • Whether you help with their specific goal or condition
  • How consultations work: online, in person, follow-ups
  • Programme costs
  • Your approach: practical food, not extreme diets

Essential pages

  • About: qualifications, experience and your philosophy
  • Programmes: weight management, diabetes, PCOS, sports nutrition, pregnancy, kids, whatever you offer, each with its own page
  • How it works: first consultation, plan, follow-ups, support between sessions
  • Pricing or packages
  • Blog or recipes
  • Contact and booking

Make booking easy

  • An online booking calendar for consultations
  • Online payment for packages
  • WhatsApp for quick questions; see WhatsApp on your website
  • A short intake form before the first session

Share results responsibly

  • Use testimonials only with written consent
  • Avoid guaranteed weight-loss promises
  • Note that individual results vary
  • Be careful with before/after photos and follow your professional body's guidelines

Content that builds trust

Practical articles and recipes, such as healthy Indian breakfasts, managing festival eating and reading food labels, show your expertise and attract search traffic. A free meal planning guide can build an email or WhatsApp list; see lead magnets.

Privacy

Health information is sensitive. Keep intake forms secure, collect only what you need and have a clear privacy policy; see privacy policy basics.

Local and online SEO

  • A Google Business Profile if you see clients in person
  • Pages for each programme targeting specific searches
  • Reviews from happy clients; see getting more Google reviews

Much of the clinic website checklist applies to nutrition practices too.
