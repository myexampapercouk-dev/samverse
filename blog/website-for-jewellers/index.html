Websites for Jewellers: Product Photography, Gold Rate Pricing, Hallmark Details, Store Appointments and Selling Online

Plan a jewellery website: honest product photos, price by weight with making charges, a gold rate you control, hallmark details, store visits and WooCommerce.

Key takeaways
  • Show the price breakdown buyers expect: metal weight, purity, the rate used, making charges, stone charges and tax, with the rate coming from a source your store controls and a visible update time.
  • Sell lightweight and everyday pieces online, and use reserve-to-try, store appointments and WhatsApp video calls for bridal and high-value collections.
  • Build trust with accurate photos, purity and hallmark details stated in hedged, checkable terms, clear exchange and buyback policies, insured delivery and a secure checkout.

A jeweller's website has to earn trust before it can sell anything. Buyers want to see each piece clearly, understand exactly how the price is made up from weight, purity, making charges and stones, know that the purity is genuine, and be reassured about exchange, buyback and delivery. Most jewellers do best with a mix: an online store for lightweight and everyday pieces, and appointments, try-at-store reservations and WhatsApp video calls for bridal and high-value collections.

WooCommerce suits jewellers well because it handles product attributes, variations, Indian payment gateways and GST invoices on a site you own. This guide covers what is specific to jewellery.

The hybrid approach lets the website take orders from people who are ready to buy, and bring the rest into your showroom, where most high-value jewellery is still sold.

Product photography that sells jewellery

Jewellery is small, reflective and detailed, so it is one of the hardest products to photograph, and one of the easiest to misrepresent.

  • Consistent setup: the same background and lighting for every piece, so the catalogue looks like one collection
  • Several views: front, side, back, clasp and close-ups of stones and detailing
  • Scale: on-model or on-hand shots, with dimensions and weight in the text, because a pendant can look far larger in a close-up
  • True colour: yellow, rose and white gold should look as they do in the store; avoid editing that changes colour or hides flaws
  • Short videos showing sparkle and movement for diamond and stone pieces
  • Compressed images with zoom, so product pages stay fast on mobile data; see image optimisation for WordPress

Write a unique description for every piece: metal and purity, gross and net weight, stone details, size options, occasion and care. WooCommerce product page optimisation covers layouts that help buyers decide.

Pricing by weight: gold rate, making charges and transparency

Show how the price is made up

Indian buyers are used to a price breakdown at the counter, and they trust a website more when it shows the same thing. On each gold or silver product page, list the metal weight, the purity, the rate per gram used, making charges (per gram or as a percentage, whichever you use), stone or diamond charges, and tax at the applicable rate. If you charge wastage separately, say so and explain it.

A gold rate from a source you control

Many jewellers want a "today's gold rate" box on the homepage. Take the rate from a source your store controls, such as a rate your staff enter in the dashboard each morning, or a rate feed you subscribe to with your own adjustment applied, rather than copying figures from another website. Show the purity it applies to and when it was last updated, and make the same rate drive product prices so the homepage and the cart never disagree.

Decide what happens when the rate changes between adding to cart and paying. Common approaches are to recalculate at checkout, or to hold the price for a short window once payment starts. Whatever you choose, state it in your terms.

How this works in WooCommerce

Each product stores its net weight, purity and making charge as attributes or custom fields. A plugin or a small custom function then calculates the price from the current rate, so a rate update reprices the whole catalogue at once. Ring and bangle sizes can be set up as variations with their own weights. Pieces with a fixed price, such as silver or fashion jewellery, can sit alongside without any calculation. Dynamic pricing like this needs custom development, so ask for it to be quoted as a separate item, and test it carefully with the gateway in test mode before launch.

Purity, hallmarking and certificates

Purity is the question behind every jewellery purchase. Keep the wording accurate and checkable:

  • State purity in karats and fineness for every gold product, such as 22K or 18K, exactly as hallmarked
  • At the time of writing, BIS hallmarking with a unique HUID code applies to most gold jewellery sold in many districts of India, and buyers can verify a HUID through BIS's official app; check the current BIS rules for your district and products, and explain how buyers can verify what they buy
  • For diamonds and gemstones, mention a lab certificate only for pieces that have one, and name the lab on that product page
  • Describe lab-grown diamonds clearly as lab-grown, never in a way that could be mistaken for natural, and check the current rules on terminology
  • Don't use "certified", "pure" or "guaranteed" loosely; say what was certified, by whom

Appointments, reserve-to-try and video calls

For bridal and high-value pieces, the website's job is to start the conversation and bring the buyer into the store:

  • Book a store visit: date, time slot, occasion and the collection they want to see, so staff can prepare a tray. Online appointment booking compares tools and reminders.
  • Reserve to try in store: a button on product pages that holds a piece for a set period under your own policy
  • WhatsApp video calls: many families shortlist bridal sets over a video call before travelling to the showroom
  • Custom design enquiries: reference image upload, metal, budget range and the date they need it by

Saree and ethnic wear stores serve the same wedding shoppers with similar video-call selling; see websites for saree and ethnic wear stores.

Policies that build trust: exchange, buyback, returns and delivery

  • Exchange and buyback: how old gold is valued, what is deducted and whether the policy differs for gold, diamonds and silver
  • Returns for online orders: the window, the condition required and which items are not returnable, such as resized or engraved pieces
  • Delivery: insured shipping, tamper-evident packaging, delivery against OTP or ID if your courier offers it, and what happens if a parcel is damaged
  • Cash on delivery: usually limited for jewellery; set an order value cap or allow it only for low-value items; see cash on delivery in WooCommerce
  • Invoices and tax: a proper GST invoice for every order; see WooCommerce GST setup

Rules on PAN for high-value purchases and on cash payments also apply to jewellers, so ask your CA how your checkout and invoices should handle them. Payment gateways may set their own limits for high-value transactions, so check with yours before launch.

Collections and occasion pages

Organise the catalogue the way people shop and search:

  • By category: rings, earrings, necklaces, bangles, chains, mangalsutras, pendants
  • By metal and purity: 22K gold, 18K gold, diamond, platinum, silver
  • By occasion: bridal, daily wear, festive, gifting, with seasonal pages for festivals such as Akshaya Tritiya and Dhanteras kept at a permanent address and updated each year
  • Filters for weight range, price range and size once a category grows

Security for a high-value store

A jewellery store is an attractive target for card skimmers and fake orders. Keep WordPress, the theme and plugins updated, use strong staff logins with limited roles, watch for unusual order patterns, and use a reputable payment gateway so card details never touch your server. The WooCommerce security checklist covers payments, card-testing bots and staff accounts.

Local SEO for jewellers

  • A complete Google Business Profile for each showroom, with real photos and accurate hours, including festival timings
  • Store pages for each branch, with directions, parking and the collections each one carries
  • Page titles using searches people make, such as "gold jewellery shop in Laxmi Road, Pune" or "bridal jewellery in {city}"
  • Product and JewelryStore structured data, and buying guides on purity, hallmarks and care written by your team

Common mistakes on jewellery websites

  • Prices with no breakdown, or a homepage gold rate that does not match the cart
  • Photos edited so heavily that the piece looks different in store
  • Purity and certification claims with nothing to verify
  • No exchange or buyback policy, the first thing many buyers look for
  • Unlimited cash on delivery on high-value pieces
  • Huge image files that make product pages slow on mobile

FAQs

Can a jewellery website update prices with the daily gold rate?

Yes. Products store weight, purity and making charges, and prices are calculated from a rate your staff enter or a feed you control. Test the setup carefully and state what happens if the rate changes during checkout.

Should I sell gold jewellery online or only show a catalogue?

Many jewellers sell lightweight and everyday pieces online and keep bridal and heavy pieces by appointment. Start with the pieces that are easiest to ship and price, and expand from there.

What should I say about hallmarking on my website?

State purity exactly as hallmarked, explain how buyers can verify a HUID through official channels, and check the current BIS rules that apply to your products and district.

Is WooCommerce secure enough for a jewellery store?

It can be, with good hosting, regular updates, limited staff access and a reputable payment gateway that handles card details. Security is ongoing work, not a one-time setting.

Can customers book a showroom visit online?

Yes. A booking form with date, time, occasion and the collection they want to see helps your staff prepare, and a reserve-to-try button on product pages brings buyers in for a specific piece.
