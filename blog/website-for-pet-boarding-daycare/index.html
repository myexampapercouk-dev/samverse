Websites for Pet Boarding, Daycare and Pet Hotels: Trust, Bookings and Peak-Season Demand

Website for pet boarding or dog daycare in India: real facility photos, vaccination rules, daily routine, updates, bookings and festival-season demand.

Key takeaways
  • Owners decide on a boarding facility from its website first, so show real photos of every area, who is on site at night and what happens in an emergency.
  • Publish clear rules on vaccinations, temperament checks, food and medication, along with what a typical day looks like, to answer anxious questions before they are asked.
  • A booking or enquiry form with dates, pet details and documents, plus early holiday-season pages, helps you fill peak dates without endless phone calls.

A website for pet boarding or dog daycare should answer one question above all: will my pet be safe and happy here? Show real photos of every area, explain who is on site day and night, publish your vaccination and health rules, describe a typical day, and make booking dates easy. Do that well and the site fills your calendar before Diwali, summer holidays and long weekends.

Leaving a dog or cat with strangers, even for one night, is a big step for most owners, and they read boarding websites carefully. This guide is for dedicated boarding facilities, dog daycare centres and pet hotels. If boarding is one part of a pet shop or grooming business, see websites for pet shops, grooming and boarding.

What anxious owners want to know

  • Where will my pet sleep, and how big is the space?
  • Is someone there overnight?
  • Will my dog be with other dogs, and how are groups chosen?
  • What do you feed, and can I send my pet's own food and medicines?
  • What happens if my pet falls ill or gets hurt?
  • Will I get photos or updates while I'm away?
  • Do you offer pick-up and drop?

Every one of these deserves a clear answer on the site, ideally on the services page and repeated in the FAQs.

Show the facility with real photos and video

Owners want to picture exactly where their pet will spend the day and night. Use your own photos and a short walk-through video of:

  • Sleeping areas: kennels, rooms or suites, with sizes and bedding.
  • Indoor and outdoor play areas, fencing and flooring.
  • A separate, quiet area for cats, away from barking dogs.
  • How pets are kept cool in summer and dry during the monsoon.
  • Feeding, bathing and isolation areas, and how you clean between stays.

Never use stock photos or images of someone else's facility. Owners who visit will notice, and trust is the whole business. Photos of happy regulars help too, with their owners' permission.

If you publish prices, show what each rate includes and what costs extra. If prices depend on size and length of stay, explain the factors. Showing prices on your website discusses both approaches.

Health, vaccination and admission rules

Clear rules reassure owners that every other pet in the facility is also healthy. Publish:

  • Required vaccinations and how recent they must be, and whether you need the vaccination card uploaded before arrival.
  • Deworming and tick and flea treatment requirements.
  • Policies for puppies, senior pets, pets in heat and pets on medication.
  • Temperament assessments for group daycare.
  • Your arrangement with a nearby veterinary clinic, and what happens in an emergency, including how and when the owner is contacted.

Check with your vet about which vaccinations to require, and keep the page updated.

A typical day, hour by hour

A simple daily schedule turns a vague promise of "lots of love and care" into something owners can picture: morning walk, breakfast, play groups, rest time in the afternoon heat, evening walk, dinner, lights out and the night check. Note how you adjust for older or anxious pets. This one section answers many phone calls before they happen.

Updates while owners are away

Owners travelling for a wedding or a work trip want to know their pet is fine. Explain how you share updates: daily photos on WhatsApp, a short video, or a shared album. Say how often and at what time, so you set expectations you can meet in peak season. Add a WhatsApp button on the site for quick questions too.

Booking and enquiry forms

Boarding needs more information than a typical appointment. A good booking form asks for:

  • Check-in and check-out dates and times.
  • Pet name, species, breed, age, weight and sex, and whether neutered.
  • Vaccination card upload.
  • Food, feeding times and any medication.
  • Behaviour notes: with other dogs, with people, fears such as fireworks.
  • Owner's phone, an emergency contact and the pet's regular vet.

For daycare, regular customers prefer a quick booking calendar for their usual days. Depending on your setup, the form can confirm instantly or send a request you approve. Advance payment for peak dates can be collected through a gateway such as Razorpay. Online booking on your website explains the options and how they work with payments.

Policies that prevent disputes

Most arguments between boarding facilities and owners come from things nobody wrote down. Publish short, plain policies and link them from the booking form so owners agree to them before arrival:

  • Cancellation and date changes, especially for peak dates when you turn other pets away.
  • Late pick-up, early check-in and what counts as an extra day.
  • Belongings: what owners can bring, such as bedding, toys and food, and that you can't be responsible for chewed items.
  • Veterinary costs: who pays if a pet needs treatment during a stay, and the owner's consent for emergency care.
  • Pets that don't settle: what you do if a dog is very stressed, aggressive or not eating, and when you call the owner.

A consent form signed at first check-in, kept with the pet's record, completes the picture. Ask a lawyer to review the wording if you are unsure.

Repeat customers and pet profiles

Boarding and daycare businesses grow through regulars. A simple customer account or saved pet profile means returning owners don't fill in breed, food and vet details every time, and only update vaccination records when they change. Reminders can go out when vaccinations are about to expire, or before school holidays, by email or WhatsApp. For daycare, packages of a set number of days, with a running balance, reward loyalty without discount wars.

Collect only the information you need, explain how you use it in a privacy policy, and keep uploaded documents secure, because vaccination cards and addresses are personal data.

Plan for festival and holiday demand

Diwali, Christmas and New Year, summer holidays and long weekends fill up early. Publish a peak-season page a couple of months ahead, with booking deadlines, minimum stay rules and a note on firecracker anxiety and how you manage it. Share the page link on Instagram and your Google Business Profile. Seasonal and festival campaigns covers timing and page structure.

Local search and reviews

Most owners search for "dog boarding near me" or "pet daycare in" their area. A complete Google Business Profile, real reviews and a page for each area you serve with pick-up help you appear. Ask happy owners for reviews after a stay, and reply to every one, including the critical ones, calmly and with what you changed. Add your full address, opening hours, check-in windows and a map to the contact page, plus parking notes, because first-time visitors often arrive with a nervous dog and need to find you quickly. Trainers and walkers can cross-refer clients; websites for dog trainers covers that neighbouring business.

FAQs

What should a pet boarding website include?

Real photos of every area, services and what they include, vaccination and admission rules, a daily routine, how updates are shared, emergency arrangements, a booking form and location details.

How do I take bookings for pet boarding online?

Use a booking or request form that captures dates, pet details, vaccination records and contacts. You can confirm manually or automatically and collect an advance payment for busy dates.

Should a boarding facility publish its prices?

Showing a starting price or what affects the rate filters out mismatched enquiries. If your pricing depends on size, length of stay and season, explain those factors instead.

How do I show that my facility is safe?

Real photos and video, overnight supervision details, cleaning routines, health rules and a clear emergency process with a named vet clinic. Honest reviews from regulars help most.

Can the website handle daycare packages?

Yes. Packages and recurring daycare days can be set up with a booking plugin, with reminders sent by email or WhatsApp.
