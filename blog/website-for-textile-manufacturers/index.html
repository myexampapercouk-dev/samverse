Websites for Textile Manufacturers and Exporters

What textile mills, garment manufacturers and fabric exporters need online: fabric and product catalogues, specifications, capacity, compliance certifications, sampling, MOQs and export enquiries.

Sameer Gupta 27 September 2026 · 3 min read

Key takeaways
  • Catalogues need GSM, count, composition, finishes, MOQs and lead times.
  • Show capacity, quality control and genuine compliance certifications.
  • Offer quote, sample and tech pack upload with fast replies.

Brands, buying houses and importers sourcing fabrics or garments evaluate suppliers on product range, quality, capacity and compliance. A professional website helps you get shortlisted, especially by overseas buyers who can't visit first.

Catalogue that buyers can use

  • Fabrics by type (cotton, linen, blends, knits, denim) or products (shirts, home textiles, uniforms)
  • Specifications: GSM, count, weave, width, composition, finishes, colour options
  • Real photos and close-ups of texture and finish
  • MOQs, lead times and sampling process

Capability

  • Spinning, weaving, knitting, dyeing, printing and stitching capacity
  • Machinery and in-house processes
  • Quality control and testing
  • Photos and videos of the facility

Compliance and sustainability

International buyers often require specific certifications (for example organic, recycled content or social compliance standards). Display the ones you genuinely hold, with certificate numbers, and explain sustainable practices you actually follow.

Enquiries and sampling

  • Quote form: product, specs, quantity, destination and timeline
  • Sample request option
  • Tech pack or artwork upload
  • WhatsApp and email with fast replies across time zones

Wholesale and D2C

Some manufacturers also sell directly. A separate retail store section can work alongside B2B enquiries; see WooCommerce development.

SEO

  • Target specific searches: "organic cotton fabric manufacturer India", "{product} exporter from {city}"
  • Unique pages for key fabric types with specifications
  • Guides on fabric selection and specifications for buyers
