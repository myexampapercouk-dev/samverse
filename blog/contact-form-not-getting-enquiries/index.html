Why Your Website Contact Form Isn't Getting Enquiries (and How to Fix It)

Getting traffic but no form submissions? Common reasons contact forms fail, from broken email delivery and spam filters to too many fields, with fixes that bring enquiries back.

If your website gets visitors but your inbox stays empty, the contact form is a common culprit. Sometimes it's technically broken; sometimes it just isn't persuading people to use it. Here's how to diagnose and fix both.

First: is the form actually working?

Fill in your own form right now and check whether the email arrives. You'd be surprised how many businesses discover their form has been silently failing for months.

Technical reasons forms fail

1. Emails aren't being delivered

Many WordPress sites send form emails using the server's basic mail function, which is often blocked or sent to spam. Use an authenticated sending method (SMTP or a transactional email service) and set up SPF and DKIM records for your domain.

2. Emails go to spam or an old address

Check spam folders, and confirm the form sends to an inbox someone actually reads.

3. A plugin or update broke it

Updates can break forms. Test forms after every update; see the maintenance checklist.

4. Over-aggressive spam protection

Some anti-spam tools block real people too. Use invisible methods (honeypot fields, time checks) or friendly checks rather than hard puzzles.

Persuasion reasons people don't fill it in

5. Too many fields

Every extra field reduces submissions. Ask only for what you need to reply: often name, phone or email, and a short message.

6. The form is hard to find

If it's only on a contact page, many visitors never see it. Add a short form or call to action to service pages and the end of articles.

7. No reason to trust you

Testimonials, client logos and a clear promise ("I'll reply within 24 hours") near the form make people comfortable sharing details.

8. Mobile problems

Tiny fields, wrong keyboards for phone numbers, or a submit button hidden behind a chat widget all stop mobile visitors.

9. No alternative

Some people prefer WhatsApp or a call. Offer both next to the form; see WhatsApp on your business website.

For more ways to turn visitors into leads, see 12 ways to get more enquiries from your website.

Make sure you never miss a lead

  • Send enquiries to email and a backup channel (WhatsApp or a Google Sheet)
  • Show a clear thank-you message so people know it worked
  • Track submissions in Google Analytics to spot sudden drops
  • Reply fast, because the first business to respond often wins

And confirm every submission properly with a thank-you page.

Quick fix checklist

  1. Test the form yourself today
  2. Set up authenticated email sending
  3. Cut fields to the essentials
  4. Add WhatsApp and phone next to the form
  5. Place calls to action on every key page
  6. Test again after every update

Broken forms are one of the most expensive silent problems a business site can have. A maintenance plan includes regular form checks so it can't happen unnoticed.
