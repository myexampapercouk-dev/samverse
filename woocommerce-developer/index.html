WooCommerce store setup in India, step by step

An Indian online store has more moving parts than a business website: payment gateway approval, cash on delivery rules, GST and couriers. This is how I set up a WooCommerce store, from your first message to your first real order.

  1. Free review and a fixed quote. Tell me what you sell, roughly how many products, how you want to be paid, where you ship and whether you are GST-registered. You get a written scope, a fixed price and a timeline.
  2. Payment gateway application. You apply to a gateway such as Razorpay, PayU, Cashfree or PhonePe in your business name, because the money settles to your bank account. Approval depends on the provider's KYC checks, so it is worth starting early.
  3. Catalogue plan. Categories, attributes and variations are agreed before any products go in.
  4. Design and build on staging. Shop, product, cart and checkout pages are built on a private staging link and kept light and simple for phones.
  5. Products, shipping and tax. Products are added or imported from your spreadsheet, then shipping zones, COD rules and GST settings are configured.
  6. Test orders. With the gateway in test mode, I check prepaid and COD orders, failed and cancelled payments, order emails, invoices and stock levels.
  7. Launch. Live keys go in, and a real low-value order confirms the whole flow works.
  8. Training. On a video call I show you how to process orders, issue refunds, update stock and add products, including from the WooCommerce mobile app.

Payments, COD, GST and shipping for Indian stores

UPI, cards and net banking

A gateway like Razorpay or Cashfree gives shoppers UPI, cards, net banking and wallets in one checkout. WooCommerce adds no transaction fee of its own; you pay only the gateway's charges. Webhooks are set up and tested so that a paid order is marked as paid even if the shopper closes the browser before returning to your site. An international option such as PayPal can be added for overseas customers. Choosing a payment gateway lists what to compare.

Cash on delivery

COD still matters to many Indian shoppers, but every refused delivery costs you shipping both ways. COD can be limited by order value or pin code, a COD fee can be shown clearly at checkout, and COD orders can be confirmed by phone or WhatsApp before dispatch. Offering COD without losing money covers the options.

GST and invoices

Tax is calculated on the customer's shipping state, with CGST and SGST for orders within your state and IGST for the rest. Each GST rate gets its own tax class, and an invoice plugin adds your GSTIN, HSN codes and consecutive invoice numbers. Business buyers can enter their GSTIN at checkout. Your CA should confirm the rates and invoice format; WooCommerce GST setup shows each step.

Shipping across India

Shipping zones usually run from your city to your state to the rest of India, with flat, weight-based or free-above-a-threshold rates. Accurate product weights matter, and a courier or shipping aggregator can be connected for labels and tracking emails. A pin code checker on product pages shows delivery dates and COD availability before checkout. See WooCommerce shipping setup for India.

Common questions

How much does a WooCommerce store cost?

It depends on the scope: the number of pages, the features and how much content or setup is involved. After a free review of your needs you get a fixed quote, so the price is agreed before any work starts.

Are you an ecommerce website development company in India?

No. Samverse is a freelance developer based in Pune, not a company, which is an alternative to hiring an ecommerce agency in India. You deal directly with the person building your WooCommerce or Shopify store, and you get a fixed quote after a free review.

What do your WooCommerce development services include?

Store setup, product and category structure, Indian payment gateways, GST tax classes and invoices, shipping and COD rules, checkout speed and a test order before launch. Custom features are quoted separately.

Which payment gateways can you set up?

Popular Indian gateways such as Razorpay, PayU, Cashfree and PhonePe, plus PayPal for international payments, and Cash on Delivery.

How many products can the store have?

WooCommerce handles anything from a few products to thousands. I can also import your existing product list from a spreadsheet.

Do you build ecommerce websites for Pune businesses?

Yes. Samverse is based in Pune and builds WooCommerce and <a href="/shopify-developer/">Shopify</a> stores for Pune and PCMC businesses, with calls or video meetings as needed. See the <a href="/wordpress-developer-pune/">website developer in Pune</a> page for local projects.

Can I manage orders on my phone?

Yes. You can manage orders from the WordPress dashboard in your browser or the WooCommerce mobile app.

How long does a store take to build?

Usually 2–4 weeks, depending on the number of products and features, once product details and photos are ready.

Can you set up GST invoices for my store?

Yes. Each GST rate gets a tax class, tax is split into CGST and SGST or charged as IGST based on the shipping state, and an invoice plugin adds your GSTIN, HSN codes and invoice numbering. Ask your CA to confirm the rates and invoice format before launch.

How do I reduce refused cash on delivery orders?

Limit COD by order value or pin code, show any COD fee clearly at checkout, and confirm COD orders by phone or WhatsApp before dispatch. Some stores also offer a small discount for paying online. These rules are set up to match how you work.

Should I choose WooCommerce or Shopify for an Indian store?

WooCommerce suits you if you want low running costs, full control and room for content and custom features, and you are happy for a developer to look after updates. Shopify suits you if you want a hosted platform and accept the monthly plan and app costs. If Shopify genuinely fits you better, I will say so, and I can build it on Shopify instead.

Can you move my store from Shopify or a marketplace to WooCommerce?

Yes. Products, categories and images can be moved across, along with customers and past orders where the old platform allows exports, and old URLs are redirected to protect your search visibility. See <a href="/shopify-to-woocommerce-migration/">Shopify to WooCommerce migration</a>. Marketplace listings usually need reworking into proper product pages for your own site.

Can WooCommerce sell services, bookings or digital products?

Yes. WooCommerce handles virtual and downloadable products, and with the right extension it can take bookings, appointments, deposits or subscriptions. For services that need a quote first, an enquiry form on the product page often works better than a checkout.

Can you add WooCommerce to my existing WordPress site?

Yes. The store is set up on a staging copy of your current site first, so your live pages keep working, then products, payments, shipping and emails are tested before it goes live.

Do I need a full-time WooCommerce developer?

Usually not. Most stores need a developer for the build, then occasional changes and monthly maintenance. A full-time developer makes sense only if you are shipping new features every week; otherwise a fixed-price project plus a maintenance plan is usually enough.

How do I choose a WooCommerce developer?

Look for live stores similar to yours, a written scope, a fixed quote, and a clear answer on who owns the hosting, domain and payment gateway accounts (it should be you). Ask how updates will be tested, because a broken update on a store can stop orders.

Will I own the website?

Yes. The domain and hosting are registered in your name, you get your own administrator login, and any temporary access used for the work can be removed.
